================================================================================
D. What a rebuild currently cannot restore
================================================================================

If everything in list A were deleted, `./build/build.sh` plus the four projector
invocations plus gen_wiki.main() would restore all of it — EXCEPT that the build
consumes wiki/index.md and wiki/overview.md, which are in list C. So the wiki
would rebuild with those two pages exactly as they are on disk today, and if
they were ever lost there is no command to recreate them.

That is the one real fragility this inventory found, and it should be fixed by
writing the index/overview generator into the repo before anything is deleted.

# Elementary Structures of Matter, Volume 2 — page 216

Cited from the Begriffsregister as `(2, 216)`. English is a DeepL translation; the German original follows each block and is the authority.

The $R_{3}$ structure determines that $J$ is not influenced by $s$. Thus, $J$ is obviously to be interpreted as the quantum number of the spatial spin $\hbar \sigma_{r}$, that is, of a quantum-like angular momentum of the relevant Hermitian form. 

The component $s$ of the straton spin, which remains fundamentally imaginary, can only be of an internal nature with respect to the straton and cannot appear as an external intrinsic angular momentum. It is an internal property of the conjunctor structure projected into the straton $R_{3}$ structure. However, this can only be a conjunctor isomerism with respect to the $R_{3}$-projection, such that, for the same protosim plex structure, different projected conjunctor structures can occur in $c$- or $d$-  form in $R_{3}$. The individual components of such an isomeric term family would then correspond to transformations that leave the metric and configurational elements, as well as the protosimplex charges, invariant. The terms of such a conjunctor-isomeric family are therefore subject to an isomorphism with respect to $J$, which manifests itself in $s$. For this reason, $\sigma_{t}=i$ can be denoted as a spin isomorphism or isomorphism spin because $Re \sigma_{t}=0$  , whereas the other term is identical to the space spin of a stratonic ($c, d$) or pseudostratonic ($a, b$) structure in $R_{3}$. 

The possible transformations of a spin isomorphism $s$ into $\sigma_{t}$ with respect to $J$ are determined by the components $s_{\mu}$ with $\mu \geqq 0$ of this part of $\sigma_{t}$. Since $\partial s=1$, we have $s_{\mu}=s-\mu$ in the interval $-s \leqq s_{\mu} \leqq+s$ if $\mu$ is an integer. If $\mu=0$ is excluded, so that the counting starts with $v=1$, then $s_{v}=s+1-v$ in the same interval $-s \leqq s_{v} \leqq +s$, while the natural numbers lie in the interval $1 \leqq \nu \leqq I < \infty$. Here, $v_{\max }=I$ describes, so to speak, the multiplet of possible spin isomorphisms—that is, the number of compositely $\bar{\lambda}$-terms that, for $J=$ const, can be transformed into one another by this isomorphism. To determine such a multiplet set $I$ of spin-isomorphic $\bar{\lambda}$-terms, $s_{v}=s+1-v$ is metronically integrated from a lower bound $a$ up to $I$, which

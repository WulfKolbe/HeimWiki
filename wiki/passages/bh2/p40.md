# Elementary Structures of Matter, Volume 2 — page 40

Cited from the Begriffsregister as `(2, 40)`. English is a DeepL translation; the German original follows each block and is the authority.

a reduction of the empirical natural constants to the  diameter of the universe. However, such a reduction must have extraordinarily far-reaching cosmological and cosmogonic consequences if the relationship between $D$ and the relevant condenser constants can be specified exactly—rather than approximately—based on the spectral function (27). Since $\mu$ is in $[k g]$, $v$ in $[s]$, and $\tau$ in $\left[m^{2}\right]$, but $D$ is dimensioned in $[m]$, it follows from dimensional considerations that $D(\tau)$ is a natural choice, which is advantageous in that $\tau$, unlike $\vartheta$, can be specified exactly. In any case, an investigation of the macromolecular structure of $R_{3}$ seems reasonable based on the lower bound of the term scheme in (27).

# Elementary Structures of Matter, Volume 2 — page 225

$\underset{a}{\boldsymbol{S}_{s}} s_{v} v=\stackrel{I}{\boldsymbol{S}}(s+1){ }_{a} \gamma v-\underset{a}{S} v \gamma v=(s+1)(I+1-a)-\frac{I}{2}(I+1)+$ 
$+\frac{a}{2}(a-1)$ when the methods from Section III, 1 are applied. In the Metron integral, $a=1$, because for the definition interval of the multiplet components, $1 \leqq \nu \leqq I$ holds. On the other hand, however, with $a=1$, because $-s \leqq s_{v} \leqq +s$, we also have $\underset{a=1}{I} s_{v} \partial v=\sum_{v=1}^{I} s_{v}=0$,  which, when substituted with $a=1$, gives $0=(s+1) I-(I+1) I / 2=$  $=I s-(I-1) I / 2$, which, for the number of spin-isomorphically transformable $\bar{\lambda}$-terms of such a multiplet, yields $I=2 s+1=P+1$ with $2 s=P$.

# Elementary Structures of Matter, Volume 2 — page 27

Cited from the Begriffsregister as `(2, 27)`. English is a DeepL translation; the German original follows each block and is the authority.

In the spectrum of complex condensations, there is a factor determined by $\pi^{2}$ that differs significantly from the value 1. However, this shifts the spectrum of complex Hermitian operators in (27) in such a way that the lower bound of the $d$-spectrum yields a value that numerically differs significantly from $m_{e}$. Such a situation, however, is incompatible with empirical evidence; for the lower bound of the $d$-spectrum must carry a charge field $e_{ \pm}$ and be temporally stable. In fact, however, apart from the electron, no $d$-structure can be found empirically that satisfies these requirements and at the same time exhibits the character of a lower bound of the entire $d$-spectrum.  For this empirical reason, the alternative logical branch for determining $\varrho$ is therefore irrelevant, so that relations (12) through (12b) are unambiguous. This unambiguity, implied by (32), thus allows us to conclude, with regard to the nucleon forces, that there must be a correlation between these nucleon forces—whatever their nature may be—and the macroscopic gravitational field structure.

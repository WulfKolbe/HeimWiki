# Elementary Structures of Matter, Volume 2 — page 230

Cited from the Begriffsregister as `(2, 230)`. English is a DeepL translation; the German original follows each block and is the authority.

According to VI., the $\bar{\lambda}_{(\mu v)}^{(\kappa \lambda)}=\underline{\bar{\lambda}}$ are synmetronic condenser terms of the corresponding fundamental condensers, which structure the compositional $\bar{\lambda}$. It always holds that $\underline{\bar{\lambda}} \perp \vec{Y}=\vec{v}+i \vec{w}$, where, by choosing appropriate coordinates, $\vec{v}=\overrightarrow{0}$, so that $\vec{Y}=i \vec{w}$ becomes possible. Since in this composite motion $\vec{Y}=i \overrightarrow{\mathcal{W}}$ of $R_{3}$ with $w^{2}=c^{2}+\dot{\varepsilon}^{2}+\dot{\eta}^{2}$ and $\dot{\varepsilon} = \dot{\eta} = 0$ is always attainable in the case of time-invariant $x_{5}$ and $x_{6}$, then $i \overrightarrow{\mathcal{w}} = \dot{\vec{x}}_{4}$ and thus $\vec{Y} \| \vec{x}_{4}$ or $\underline{\lambda} \perp \vec{x}_{4}$ be set without loss of generality. On the other hand, however, the $\bar{\lambda}$—as symmetrical condensation levels—determine, through their composition via conjunctive laws, the orientation of the ${ }_{x}^{p}(\mu)_{ \pm}^{q}$, which, according to the preceding discussion, in turn determine the orientation of the total spin, whose $R_{3}$-projection appears as the straton spin $\bar{s}_{x}$. This means, however, that the orientation of $\bar{s}_{x}$ is directly related to the temporal $R_{4}$ structure $\bar{\lambda} \perp \vec{x}_{4}$, and this structure is expressed, according to (5a), by the condition $\cos \alpha_{4} \neq 0$ of the world geometry with respect to $x_{4}$. According to this relation (5a), however, the following holds for the spacetime world $R_{4}^{+}$ and its $x_{5}$ stacked in $x_{5}$ with $k \geqq 0$ and $R_{3}^{+0} \equiv R_{3} \quad$ the condition $\cos \alpha_{4} > 0$ holds. However, since $\cos \alpha_{4} \neq 0$ also allows for $\cos \alpha_{4} < 0$ with an antiparallel time coordinate, the existence of an anti-spacetime $R_{4}^{-}$with anti-spaces $R_{3}^{-k}$ be conceived, although this cannot be directly verified. However, if this anti-spacetime $R_{4}^{-}$exists, then—relative to $R_{4}^{+}$—in addition to the flukton and conjunctor spins of the flux aggregates and in addition to the straton spin $\bar{s}_{x}^{+} \equiv \bar{s}_{x}\left(R_{4}^{+}\right)$, there would have to be corresponding enantiomeric structures as antistructures with the antistraton spin $\bar{s}_{x}^{-} \equiv \bar{s}_{x}\left(R_{4}^{-}\right)$, whose spin configurations are oriented in a mirror-symmetric manner  relative to $R_{4}^{+}$ such that they appear as normal structures with respect to $R_{4}^{-}$.

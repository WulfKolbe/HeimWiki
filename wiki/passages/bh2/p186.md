However, after substitution with $\operatorname{tg} F=b / a$, we always have  $\cos F=\left(1+t g^{2} F\right)^{-1 / 2}=\frac{a}{\sqrt{a^{2}+b^{2}}}$ and $\sin F=\left(1+c \operatorname{tg}^{2} F\right)^{-1 / 2}=$  $=\frac{b}{\sqrt{a^{2}+b^{2}}}$, so $a \cos F + b \sin F = \sqrt{\mathrm{a}^{2} + \mathrm{b}^{2}} = a_{k l}^{i} =$ const.

# Elementary Structures of Matter, Volume 2 — page 213

Cited from the Begriffsregister as `(2, 213)`. English is a DeepL translation; the German original follows each block and is the authority.

Semantic architectural units form a $R_{3}$condensation, which appears either as a straton determining the ponderability of the $c$- and $d$-structures, or as a pseudostratonic spatial sphere of influence of the imaginary condensations. This is the reason why the $a$- and $b$-hermetics can become effective in $R_{3}$ at all, and why the empirically observed transmutation of imaginary forms into complex forms becomes possible. On the other hand, these imaginary hermetic forms would then also have to possess the pseudostratonic properties of a spatial spin. If $m_{x}=\sum_{p, q} \underline{m}_{x}^{(p q)} \quad$ the selector of integer spin quantum numbers of such a straton or pseudostraton spin $\bar{s}_{x}$, and is its unit vector given by $\sum_{\mu} \bar{s}_{\mu}=\bar{s}_{0}$, then the selector of this straton spin of Hermitian form $x$ yields the representation  $\bar{s}_{x}=\sum_{\mu, p, q}{ }_{x}^{p}(\mu)^{q}= \pm \frac{\hbar}{2} \sum_{\mu} \bar{s}_{\mu} \sum_{p, q} m_{x}^{(p q)}= \pm \bar{s}_{0} \frac{\hbar}{2} m_{x}$. Using the current metron number $v$, the half-integer selector $\frac{\bar{s}_{0}}{2} m_{x} ; v=\bar{\sigma}(x)$ can be set in its active state, while `\bar{s}_{x} ; \(v=\bar{S}(x)` provides the measurable spin vector of integral type for the temporally stable flux aggregate of a symmetronic condenser structure.  Thus, the simple relationship $\bar{s}(x)= \pm \hbar \bar{\sigma}(x)$ holds, although it should be noted that this relationship, in the selector formulation (88), is a consequence of the synmetronic world selector (58).  Although this selector is Hermitian in the basis signatures, it is not so in the contrasignatures.

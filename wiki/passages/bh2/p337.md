The cubic equation of the protosimplex generator uniquely coordinates, for every  $V_{6}$ point a unique quadruplet $n_{j}$ in the $P_{4}$ lattice, which in turn determines a mass term $M(c, d)$ according to (98d) and (98e), also in a unique form. These $M$ in turn correspond to the condenser terms of composite hermetics from (19) and satisfy the equivalence principle $M c^{2}=E$, which in turn makes the energetic character of $\bar{\lambda} \widehat{[ }]$ becomes evident. However, due to this energetic equivalence, all metronic condensations are energetically excitable, so that for each of the 26 lattice points of the $V_{6}$lattice with respect to the associated $M(v x)=M(c, d)$ and the complementary $n_{j}$-quadruplets of the $P_{4}$ lattice. According to the general quantum principle of all interactions, such excitations can only correspond to positive integers $N \geqq 0$, such that only for $N=0$ can the 26 lattice points of the $V_{6}$ be represented by the complementary $n_{j}$ in the $P_{4}$ via the protosimplex generator, while the $V_{6}$lattice must be extended to a $V_{7}$ by $N>0$,  which must also be expressed by a factor in the protosimplex generator $W$.

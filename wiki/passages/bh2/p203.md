Condenser spins, which are realized in one of the two possible configurations, such that, from $N>1$ of all flux aggregates of the fundamental fluxes, the number $Z_{N}$ of possible spin isomerisms of the flucton in question can be averaged. If the stereoisomerism of the $N$ fundamental flows is fixed with respect to the screen field system of the protosimplex in question, then for $N=1$ the double spin configuration $Z_{1}=2$ must hold. For $N=2$, we then have $Z_{2}=2 \cdot 1 \cdot 2=4$, and for $N=3$, $Z_{3}=2 \cdot 2 \cdot 1 \cdot 2 \cdot 3=24$ is completely evident. The complete induction then yields, for any $N>3$, the number-theoretic law $Z_{N}=2^{N-1} \cdot N!$ for the possible spin isomerisms of the flucton. Since all fundamental fluxes of the flucton differ in their signature-isomeric correlation exponents and stereoisomeric flux aggregates, the flucton is constructed from $N$ conceptually distinct elements, for which (again relative to the screen field) there are $N!$ possible permutations. However, these $N$! permutations can only be different relative positions of the fundamental fluxes within the domain of definition of the flucoton in question. This means, however, that the $N!$ permutations are the possible stereoisomers of the flucoton with respect to the screen field system. If $S_{N}$ denotes the number of possible stereoisomers, then $S_{N}=N!$ and $Z_{N}=2^{N-1} \cdot S_{N}$ for the  number of spin isomerisms. However, for each of the $N!$ stereoisomeric basic flux combinations, there are $Z_{N}$ spin isomerisms, so that for the flukton in question there must be $E_{N}=Z_{N} \cdot S_{N}$ enantiostereoisomers. Here, the concept of enantiostereoisomerism of a flucton seems particularly meaningful, because for every fluctonic isomerism with respect to the total condenser spin (+) of all basic flows, there must always be a mirror-symmetric spin system (-) that is identical to (+) except for the spin orientation. Here, $( \pm)$ simply denote indices for the respective isomerism, where (-) is the enantiomer of (+). If $E_{N}^{( \pm)}$ denotes the number of these enantiostereoisomeric flucktons, which occur in pairs that are mirror-symmetric,  then $2 E_{N}^{( \pm)} = E_{N}$.

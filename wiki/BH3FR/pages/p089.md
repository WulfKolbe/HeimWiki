Light aging: A potentially possible photonic wavelength shift caused by possible gravitational dynamics, but which lies far below current spectroscopic limits of detectability. $(2,40)$ 

Matter-field quantum: A general term for all quantum states of the general matter field, encompassing both quantum states of a corpuscular nature with rest mass and those with vanishingly small rest mass. 

Maximon: A hypothetical particle of maximum rest mass, whose value (a natural constant) corresponds to the Planck mass and acts as a calibration factor. 

Mesosphere: The middle monometronic sphere in the → cosmogonic or eschatological spherical trinity. $(2,58)$ 

Mesozone: The → configuration zone of a term of → complex Hermetry that rises linearly in the → Protosimplex configuration and encloses the internal region. $(2,261)$ 

Metron: A geometric unit of last resort with at least one dimension, through which a general space becomes discontinuous because this metron is no longer divisible. 

Metron differential: The minimal change in a number-theoretic function or its → selector in the sense of a difference, because, under the assumption of the → metron, the limit to the differential (infinitesimal calculus) cannot be computed. 

Metron integral: A summation operation of → metron differentials that is analogous to infinitesimal integration. 

Metron integrand: The → selector over which the → metron integral is extended. 

Metron spin: A vector determined by the direction of rotation of a two-dimensional → Metron (normal to the Metron surface). 

Metron tensor: The tensorial → spin selector of a general field of two-dimensional → Metrons in a higher-dimensional space.

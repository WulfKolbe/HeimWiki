Viton systems appear. Linking the imaginary light-time (fourth world coordinate) as a condensation to the self-condensations yields the solution manifold of the Hermitian form $b$ as time condensations. The worldlines of all these structures lie in the conical asymptotic space of the double-shelled four-dimensional hyperbolic space of spacetime; that is, these worldlines are geodesic null lines in spacetime, from which it follows that the time condensations of the hermetic form $b$ are to be interpreted as photon fields, so that this hermetic form encompasses the entire electrodynamics and thus the entire optics. 

If, in place of the fourth world coordinate, real physical space is included in the condensation process and integrated into the self-condensations, then the hermitian form c appears as a spatial condensation. It can be shown that these terms lie on spacetime worldlines that are not located in the conical asymptotic space, but rather in the two-shell hyperbolic spaces of spacetime; thus, they possess rest mass, but cannot be the sources of time condensations—though they can be sources of gravitational fields—so that the terms of the form $c$ must be interpreted as electrically neutral elementary corpuscles. Finally, there is still the possibility that all world coordinates are involved in the condensation process, which, as a solution manifold, yields the spacetime condensations of the Hermitian form $d$. Here, too, these are terms with rest mass, but in which a source structure for the form $b$ which is to be interpreted as an electric charge field, so that the terms of the Hermitian form $d$ can only be electrically charged elementary corpuscles. 

The Hermetic forms $a$ and $b$ are of an imaginary nature; their terms in (24) have no rest masses, whereas the concept of rest mass (ponderability) first appears in (25) through (26b) in the complex condensations $c$ and $d$.

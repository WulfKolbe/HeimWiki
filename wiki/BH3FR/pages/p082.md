External Zone: The outer → configuration zone of a term → complex hermetry. $(2,262)$ 
Fine structure, metronic: The metronic structure of a space whose dimensional number exceeds that of the corresponding → metron. 

Fine-structure selector: A → selector that describes the fine structure of a metronized space. 

Fine-structure number: Any number in the sequence of → metron numbers selected by the → fine-structure selector. 
Field activator: The anti-Hermitian part of a → synmetronic fundamental tensor. $(2,82)$ 

Field selector: A → selector, also known as a → functional selector, whose  action appears as a field function. 
Flukton: A dynamic → prototrope serving as a structural element  of → metronic condensations. $(2,186)$ 

Flukton spin: Spin vector perpendicular to the plane of the cyclic motion of the → flukton. $(2,201)$ 
Flukton conjugation: Cyclic exchange of → fluktons. $(2,197)$ 
Flux aggregate: Aggregate of cyclic → condenser fluxes in the form of  → fluktons. $(2,158)$ 

Flux class: A one-dimensional system of → flux aggregates determined by  a specific → condenser signature and allowing for isomeric  flux classes. $(2,158)$ 
External-field correlation: The interrelation between specific → synmetronic structures and external fields, which is determined in the second → validity range by the → correlation exponent. $(2,112)$ 

External-Field Correlator: A → selector that describes the → external-field correlation. $(2,115)$ 

External-field selector: The → fundamental condenser supplemented by the → correlation tensor, in which the external field is then expressed by the → correlation tensor. $(2,108)$

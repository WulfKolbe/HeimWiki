Now that the state of expansion of space has reached a certain critical value, the subphysical conditions in the submicromar metronic range can cause the metrons corresponding to the three spheres, in their temporal succession within the double chronon of a “present,” form three tensorial selector fields that behave functionally like structural units and construct a world correlator from iterations of these structural units (53). This state of affairs would be consistent with the six-dimensional world tensor and two-dimensional metrons as described in (15b) and would, moreover, correspond to the heuristic conclusions (9) and (9a) regarding metric partial structures of the world. Consequently, one could regard the time of the onset of the aforementioned subphysical conditions as the time of the as-yet-unknown cosmogony of matter. Most likely, this cosmogony of matter unfolded catastrophically in the sense of a great many small initial explosions, either for each galaxy or for each galaxy system. However, this allows—regardless of speculations regarding the unknown cosmogony of matter—the lattice nuclei to be unambiguously assigned dependencies on the lattice selectors of the world tensor (53a), such that, through the action of sieve operators on the hypermatrix of the world correlator, the correlators of the four forms of hermeticism naturally arise as solutions to (19). This thus proves that the sought-after polymetry of the world tensorium—in the sense of three lattice nuclei—does indeed exist, so that the possibility of finding a term selector for the separation of the partial spectra of complex hermetry from (27) and (27a) seems entirely feasible.

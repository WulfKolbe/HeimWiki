Protosimplex: The simplest structure, consisting solely of the → prototropes  of the → flucton and the → shield fields. $(2,190)$ 

Protosimplex generator: A quantity formed multiplicatively from the → structural power, the → base rise, and an excitation function (dependent on the → resonance orders), through which the occupancy of the → configuration zones with → protosimplexes can be determined. $(2,324)$ 

Protosimplex charge: Describes the multiplication of the ⟶ protosimple xes in the resonance spectrum into an invariant basic pattern, to which the protosimplex charge 1 is always assigned. $(2,195)$ 

Protosimplex transfer: The shift of → protosimplexes from  the → mesozone to the → external zone as a result of linearity. $(2,341)$ 
Protosimplex valence: The number of → condenser signatures that define the respective → protosimplex. $(2,197)$ 

Protosphere: The outer monometronic sphere of the → cosmogonic or → eschatological spherical trinity. $(2,58)$ 
Prototropes: Primordial forms of elementary → synmetronic condensation stages that appear as → fluctones or → shield fields and  structure the → protosimplexes. $(2,190)$ 

Prototrope combinate: A correlative combination of → prototropes corresponding to a → coupling structure, which, as maxima of the sources of → correspondence fields, structure the → compositional condensation stage. $(2,191)$ 

Spatial helicity: The → temporal helicity related to the real subspace (universe) of the → world. $(2,285)$ 

Spatial Compressor: The → structural compressor related to the → world tensorium. 

Spatial Condensation: A → form of hermetry in which a → condensation of physical space is structurally integrated with the → self-condensation of the → transcoordinates.

Spatial Condenser: The → structural condenser relative to the world tensorium, which in this form, as a → functional selector, generates the → spatial compressor. 
Spatial Extension: The encroachment of world structures outside the non-space of spacetime into physical space. $(2,23)$ 

Spatial spin: A component of the general → straton spin of a → Hermitian form in real physical space. This space spin is identical to the empirical concept of spin, which is described as the product of parity and spin quantum number. $(2,211)$ 
Space-spin correspondence: An interaction determined by the → space spin. $(2,230)$ 
Spatial-spin neutrino: The component of the $\beta$ neutrino that is formally determined solely by the → spatial spin. $(2,353)$ 

Spacetime condensation: The structural form resulting from → hermeticity of all → world coordinates. 

Gravitational reality barrier: This defines a micromare and a  macromare boundary distance (relative to a gravitational center), between which the gravitational field exists as a real quantity. 

Resonance basis: A dimensionless numerical factor that depends solely on the set of quantum numbers of an invariant fundamental pattern and marks the beginning of the spectrum of resonances with respect to the invariant mass term of the fundamental pattern in question. $(2,337)$ 

Resonance order: These are positive integers that describe the possible resonances in the resonance spectrum of a fundamental pattern term, such that the resonance order 0 corresponds to the mass term of the fundamental pattern. The resonance orders are bounded above by a positive integer, which, like the order itself, depends solely on the set of quantum numbers of the ground state in question. $(2,329)$

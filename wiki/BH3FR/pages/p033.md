The event structures of spacetime are thus always equivalent to phenomenological energy densities. The consequence of this equivalence is the occurrence of real tensors and the unity of fields and field sources. Forming the matrix trace of this equivalence principle leads to a modified phenomenological energy density tensor, which now also contains the non-Hermitian metric fundamental tensor as a general tensorial interaction potential (as a consequence of the geodesic equation)  and is likewise not a pseudotensor.

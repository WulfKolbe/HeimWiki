Due to the half-integer nature in (88), it proved expedient to use the  double spin quantum number $Q$ for the spatial spin and the double spin quantum number $P$ for the imaginary straton spin component (isomorphism spin). In this notation, the different  nature of bosons and fermions in (91) becomes particularly clear. With regard to the bounds, the double spatial spin is bounded at its  lower limit by 1/2 for the spinor and by the scalar term 0 for the tensor term of the  boson. In contrast, for the double isomorphism spin, there is the closed integer interval (90a) between the value 0 and an upper bound, which is the configuration number (72) increased by 1.

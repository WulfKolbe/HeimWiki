The Kowski space $R_{-4}$, whose coordinates are $x_{1}, x_{2}, x_{3}$, ict, all appear to lie on their currently known fields. There is a corresponding Lorentz transformation in both $R_{-4}$ and $R_{+4}$. Both spaces are embedded in a six-dimensional space $R_{6}$. According to general relativity, gravitational waves should propagate at the speed of light $c$. However, the theoretical derivation of this statement assumed a Minkowski space $R_{-4}$, so it is not surprising that the propagation speed again appears to be the limiting speed $c$  . Experimental measurements of the propagation speed of gravitational field disturbances could provide clarity here, but this has not yet been possible due to the peculiar nature of gravity.

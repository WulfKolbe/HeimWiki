Since the phenomenological energy density tensor in the world tensor (macroscopic approximation of (19)) is Hermitian, the conservation principle of energy is expressed by the absence of sources, i.e., the vanishing of the vector divergence of this tensor field. On the other hand, the energy density tensor can always be interpreted as an iteration of a unified force field tensor, which must then also be divergence-free. However, the spacetime vector divergence of its spacetime section corresponds to phenomenological, electrically neutral and electrically charged matter flows, which, when the divergence-free nature of the unified force field tensor in component form is incorporated, leads to semantic statements about the two transcoordinates. Thus, $x_{5}$ appears to be a quantification of states of organization, which in a specific special case can also be expressed by negative entropy. For this reason, $x_{5}$ designated as an “entelechial” coordinate, which constantly updates the organizational states of spatial structures, while the coordinate designated as “aeonic,” $x_{6}$, controls the temporal direction of updating. The interaction  (34) of these two hidden dimensions of the world demonstrates  how the integral cosmic motion of all spatial world structures is actualized (34a), so that the formulation of the cosmological  problem thereby undergoes further concretization. 

The fundamental empirical natural constants express in their numerical values the respective system of measurement used for the measurement, whose units of measurement are the units of the concepts of mass, length, and time, which are supplemented by the electrical units volt or ampere. Due to (29), the electrical unit ampere was chosen in the discussed paper. On the other hand, constant factors arise directly from theoretical derivations, which have been designated as capacitor constants and can also be used as units of measurement. Thus, the calibration factor (Planck mass) appearing in the derivation of (27) is just as much a capacitance constant as the metron (15) or the elementary charge field (29).

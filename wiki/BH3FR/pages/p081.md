Base Signature: The indexing of the covariant part in a → fundamental condenser. 

Binary Field: Those → fundamental condensers in which two different elements of the ⟶ correlator are interrelated. 

Chronon: The indivisible element of time resulting from the existence of the → world metron. $(2,27)$ 

Distributor number: The quantum number of the → structural distributor, which is identical to the empirical strangeness number. $(2,285)$ 

Doublet number: A newly introduced quantum number that explains the occurrence of multiple isospin doublets. $(2,283)$ 

Unit selector: A → selector whose effect always yields the value 1 as a  number. 

Endogenous spin: A vector whose direction is determined by the direction of rotation of a → world metron and points into the interior of the cells of the → world tensorium bounded by these metrons. 

Endogenous world: A → world structured from cells oriented in the sense of the → endogenous spin. 

Entelechial dimension: A hidden world dimension orthogonal to physical spacetime, which, like the → eonic dimension, is imaginary. $(2,21)$ 

Event, latent: A → world point outside the spacetime subspace of the → world. 

Event, manifest: A → world point within the subspace of spacetime. 

Eschatological Sphere Trinity: The trinity of monometric spheres (spatial) that delimits the → Aeon as the temporal interval defining spacetime, as the final event of spacetime. $(2,62)$ 

Exogenous spin: A direction of rotation opposite to that of the → endogenous spin. 

Exogenous world: A → world structured in the sense of the → exogenous spin, analogous to the → endogenous world.

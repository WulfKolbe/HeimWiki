Fundamental condenser: The → selector of the degree of a metronic → condensation. 

Fundamental selector: A tensorial selector whose effect generates a fundamental tensor and is always the iteration of two different or identical → lattice nuclei. 

Fundamental Sphere: The inner sphere of a → cosmogonic or → eschatological spherical trinity. $(2,58)$ 
Functional selector: A higher-order → selector that is functionally dependent on  other → selectors. 

Futural potency: The totality of possible  controls, relative to an event, originating from the → Aeonic dimension of actualizations into  later events. $(2,24)$ 

Lattice, metronic: The coordinate lattice determined by the → Metron, as opposed to the infinitesimal coordinate grid. 

Lattice core: A tensorial → selector that, as the core of a metronic integral operator, describes the state of a metronic → condensation. 

Lattice selector: A → selector that, in the Euclidean or pseudo-Euclidean case, describes the finite division of the straight-line, equidistant → lattice determined by the → metron. 

Gravitational boundary: A finite distance from the gravitational center where the gravitational field vector changes sign, such that this boundary is to be understood as the boundary of the attractive gravitational field. 

Graviton: Hypothetical quanta of the gravitational field, the existence of which is suggested by a → Hermitian form. 

Basic flow progression: Temporal progression of → synmetronic condensation stages, which, as dynamic → prototropes, form the → flukton. (2,  186)

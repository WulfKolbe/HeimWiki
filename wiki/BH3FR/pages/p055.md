This solution to the synmetronic fundamental problem, described by (67) and (68), shows that the synmetronic condensation stages (58), as condenser maxima, structure a compositional condensation stage (19), whereby these compositional condensation stages describe the four forms of hermetry of material elementary structures. The internal synmetronic structurings of the forms of hermetry are thus correlating condenser systems that depend solely on those structural indexations (base and contrast signatures), which—following the action of the respective sieve chain on the correlator (53)—characterize the corresponding form of hermetry. A synmetronics of these hermetic forms consists of a structural description of these internal correlations according to (68) and (68b). The simplest structural scheme is the pseudobimetry of the self-condensations $a$, described by (69) and (69a). A formally identical description is possible for the pseudohexametry (timelike and spacelike) of the time condensations $b$ or the complex spatial condensations $c$, although the physical manifestation is completely different, which is due solely to the difference in algebraic character.These forms of hermeticity $b$ and $c$ are therefore jointly described by the internal correlation structures (70) and (70a). The highest complexity apparently lies  in the case of total hermetry, that is, the eneametry of complex spacetime condensations $d$ in the form of the internal correlation schemes (71) and (71a). 

These schemes of internal correlation structures clearly show that the synmetronic correlator (53), in its four versions (corresponding to the four forms of hermetry), already represents the term selector we are seeking.

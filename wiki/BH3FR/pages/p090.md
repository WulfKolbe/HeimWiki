Metron number: The sequential number of the individual metrons. 
Mundal entelechy: The integral component of all structures of physical space (the universe) in the ⟶ entelechial world dimension. (2,  67) 
Zero selector: A special → constant selector in which the  constant has the value 0, such that the effect of this zero selector  always yields 0. 

Operator, metronic: A computational rule that takes into account the → metron and can always be interpreted as a → selector. 

Orthoconjunctor: These are the → conjunctors through which → fluctones with → flucton spins in the ortho configuration are connected. $(2,202)$ 
Orthoconjunctive: The → conjunctive corresponding to the → orthoconjunctor. $(2,202)$ 
Paraconjunctive: The → conjunctive of a → paraconjunctor. $(2,202)$ 
Paraconjunctor: A → conjunctor that connects → fluctones with → fluctone spins in para-alignment. $(2,202)$ 

Partial structure: In the case of → polymetry, this is described in each instance by an element of the → correlator; however, → fundamental condensers ( → binary fields) are also referred to as such partial structures. 

Partial selector: A → functional selector that depends on → partial structures. 

Polymetry: The interrelation of the → fundamental selectors of the → correlator with respect to a → composition field, when the → correlator consists of more than one element. 

Ponderability: The ability to be weighed as a consequence of the existence of a rest mass. 

Protofield: The Hermitian part of a → synmetronic fundamental tensor. $(2,82)$ 

Protoselector: The → functional selector of a → protofield. (2,

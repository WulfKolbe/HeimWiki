If the terms of a resonance spectrum are plotted as a diagram against the  resonance orders, a staircase curve results; that is, initially, the mass rises with the resonance order up to a critical value of the resonance order, only to drop to a lower value at the next higher integer. Subsequently, the mass rises again beyond the previous maximum, only to fall once more. This is the graphical representation of the general structural principle (107), which repeats itself within the framework of these periodicities. The first mass term in such a trough is then lower in mass but denser in terms of its protosimplex structure. It is conceivable that, during this compression process, the mass difference during protosimplex compression between the maximum term of the rise (protosimplex run) and the lower term of the next resonance order in the compression process is radiated as an imaginary $b$-hermetry of a discrete photon wavelength (116).

Furthermore, the transfer time must be at least one period  of the cyclic condenser flux in the integral flux aggregate (composed of the $G$ partial aggregates) (117).

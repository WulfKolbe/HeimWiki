Time Helicity: The parallelism or antiparallelism—expressed as a quantum number—of the direction of spin of an integral flux aggregate of a → coupling structure, generally with respect to the time dimension. $(2,225)$ 

Time Condensation: This is an imaginary → hermetic form in which the three imaginary → world dimensions form → condensation levels. 

Central Zone: The central → configuration zone of highest density and  cubically increasing occupancy with → protosimplexes within a  term of → complex Hermeticism. $(2,261)$ 

State Selector: A Hermitian → functional selector that is the metronic analogue of the state operator. If the state selector acts on a → field selector that generates a convergent metronic state function, then the eigenvalues form a discrete  point spectrum analogous to quantum states, because an abstract  function space exists for both the state selector and the relevant → field selector. For example, the space condenser is such a → state selector whose eigenvalues form a discrete point spectrum as condensation levels, while the action of the → fundamental condenser provides the metronic state function. The states described by the → space condenser in the → world selector are thus metric states of the ⟶ world tensor itself, which occur in discrete → condensation levels and form a point spectrum. Thus, this → world tensor appears as a six-dimensional carrier space of the relevant abstract function space.

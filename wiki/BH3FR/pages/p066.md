The density drops steeply from the maximum in the central zone through the internal and meso zones to the minimum in the external zone. In the special case of invariant ground states, the occupation parameters must depend in some way on typical quantum numbers of the ground state, so that these parameters—and thus also the masses of the ground state terms—are also invariants. Furthermore, in schemes (98b) through (98e), each occupation parameter splits into a time-constant component of a framework structure—which depends only on $k$—and the time-varying occupation parameter.

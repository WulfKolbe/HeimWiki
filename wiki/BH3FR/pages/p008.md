As already explained in the preface, this introduction is intended, on the one hand, to facilitate the reading of *Elementary Structures of Matter* and, on the other hand, to serve as a brief overview of Heim’s theory. 

In his article “Field Theories Currently Under Discussion,” Walter Dröscher—who, together with Burkhard Heim and Andreas Resch, played a key role in shaping this introduction—describes the place of Heim’s theory within the framework of modern physics. In doing so, he deliberately refrains from providing references to the individual theories, as the relevant works may be assumed to be familiar to the reader. 

Burkhard Heim’s commentaries on “Elementary Structures of Matter” provide a highly concise summary of Volumes 1 and 2. The numbers in parentheses inserted into the text correspond to the  numbering of derived reference equations from these volumes,  which are also listed in the formula collection of this introduction for reference. 

In many of these reference equations, factors or sums of $Y_{j}$ with the running index $j \geqq 1$ appear, which merely represent uncertainty factors. According to the current state of empirical knowledge, the following applies to all $Y_{k}=1$, it is conceivable, however, that at a later stage of empirical research, deviations from the numerical  theoretical data may arise, requiring corrections $Y_{j} \neq 1$ to compensate for the discrepancies, so that these $Y_{j}$ may be empirically observed where a refinement of the theoretical framework would be necessary. Uncertainty factors $Y_{k}$  are included in those mathematical relationships that  have not yet been fully clarified.

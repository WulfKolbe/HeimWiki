The second domain of validity permits approximated solutions in the case of high  metron numbers, such that the third domain of validity, with a vanishing → metron (area approaches 0), yields infinitesimal  solutions of a micromar nature, and the fourth domain of validity, according to the correspondence principle, marks the transition into the macromare field continuum. 

Hermetry: A non-Euclidean structured subspace whose semantics has been interpreted according to physical principles. 

Hermetric characteristic: The set of → singular screen fields,  that is, the set of those → fundamental condensers whose  → base and counter-signatures are identical and are determined solely by the indexing of a → lattice core as a → structural unit. (2,  187) 

Hermetric form: Possible forms of → hermetry within the  → world tensorium. 

Hermetric structure: The hermetic structuring of → world structures. 

Hyperselector: A → selector that describes the coordinate division of geodesic coordinates of a non-Euclidean structure, as determined by the → Metron, and differs from the → lattice selector if such a structure exists. 

Hyperstructure: A space entirely determined by the → Metron, which is defined as a → structural tensorium by a → fine-structure selector. 

Imaginary condensation: A metronic → condensation that is defined only in a range of imaginary coordinates. 

Imponderability: The inability to weigh free field energy, characterized by a vanishing rest mass. 

Internal Zone: The → configuration zone of a term of → complex Hermitian geometry whose → protosimplex occupancy increases quadratically and which lies between the → central zone and the → mesozone. $(2,261)$

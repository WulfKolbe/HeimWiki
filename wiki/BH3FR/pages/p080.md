# IV. GLOSSARY OF TERMS FOR  “ELEMENTARY STRUCTURES OF MATTER,” VOLUMES 1 AND 2

 
[The notation (2, x) indicates that the term in question is defined on page x of Volume 2.] 

Aeon: World time, eternal duration, spacetime 
Aeonic dimension: A hidden, imaginary world dimension from  which the direction of temporal actualization during the → aeon (the definitional interval of physical time) is controlled. $(2,21)$ 
Aeonic mundal potency of being: The → Hermetry in the → aeonic  dimension, in which no → condensation stages are formed. $(2,24)$ 
Activation selector: The anti-Hermitian component of a → synmetronic → fundamental selector. $(2,83)$ 
Antihermetry: The absence of a metric structure in a defined subspace, which then possesses Euclidean or pseudo-Euclidean properties. 
Anticonjunction: A → conjunction related to negative → temporal helicity. $(2,220)$ 
Apeiron: A possible region of the → world outside the definition interval of the subspace of physical spacetime. $(2,67)$ 
Architectural unit, semantic: Subspaces of the → world that differ in meaning and are not interchangeable. 
Argument selector: A → selector on which a → functional selector depends. 

Baryoneutrino: Provisional designation for the electrically neutral  complement to the electron, which, however, has only a formal existence. $(2,13)$ 
Basic rise: The increase in → protosimplex occupancies in a term of a → complex hermetic form in the case of a time-constant → ground state as the framework structure. $(2,325)$

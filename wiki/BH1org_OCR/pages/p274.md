Consequently, an initial development stage of $R_{3}$ has been completed for $n=3$, so that $D, d, \sqrt{\tau}$ and $\sqrt{\tau^{\prime}}$—as functions of $n>1$—vary over time along with $n(t)$. If we set  $\pi D^{2}=n \tau$ and $\pi d^{2}=n \tau^{\prime}$, then $(D d)^{2}=\pi n E^{2} / e^{2}$ follows, or with the constants $\pi E^{2} / e^{2}=\underline{\alpha}$ and $\pi^{2} \underline{\alpha}=\beta$, then $(D d)^{2}=\underline{\alpha} n$ also follows with $\tau \tau^{\prime}=\beta / n$. In fact, at the beginning of cosmic motion, a threefold actualization of the cosmogonic spherical trinity $T_{K}$ would be possible in the steps $n\left(t_{1}=0\right)=1$, as well as $n\left(t_{2}\right)=1+1=2$ and $n\left(t_{3}\right)=1+1+1=3$, which in turn can be expressed by a cardinal number complex, namely $|K|=\{3 ; 2 ; 1\}$ which proves to be analogous to the cardinal number complex of the hermetic $R_{6}$ structure. If $D d=\sqrt{\underline{\alpha} n}$ is derived backward using $n \rightarrow 1$,  then for $t=0$ with $1 \leqq k \leqq 3$, the statement $D_{k} d_{k}=$ holds for $n=1$, but the following applies to all $n \geqq 2$ up to the current state $t=T$  the progression $D(n) \rightarrow D_{T}$ and $d(n) \rightarrow D^{\prime}(T)=d_{0}$, whereby the diameters $D_{T}$ and $d_{0}$ exist separately from one another; that is, the possibility of a surface $D_{T} d_{0}=D_{0}^{2}=$ const is not apparent. 

The spherical trinities $\left\{D_{1}, D_{2}, D_{3}\right\},\left\{d_{1}, d_{2}, d_{3}\right\}$ at $t=0$ and  $\left\{d_{1}, d_{2}, d_{3}\right\}\left\{D_{1}, D_{2}, D_{3}\right\}$ at $t=\theta$ are obviously the $R_{3}$-projections of unknown $R_{6}$ structures, which are to be understood as corner events of  spacetime. If we set $\left\{D_{1}, D_{2}, D_{3}\right\} \hat{=} T_{K}$ for the cosmogonic and $\left\{d_{1}, d_{2}, d_{3}\right\} \hat{=} T_{E}$ for the eschatological spherical trinity of $R_{4} \equiv R_{4}^{+}$, then at some $x_{5}$ distance, a $R_{4}^{-}$ must exist according to  possible $R_{4}^{-}$ with $R_{3}^{-} \equiv R_{3}^{+}$ according to (5) and (5a), but $\cos \left(\bar{x}_{4}^{+}, \bar{x}_{4}^{-}\right)=-1$  exists according to (92), which, except for the corner events $t=0$ and $t=\theta$  is disjoint from $R_{4}^{+}$. With the abbreviation $T_{\bar{K} E}^{ \pm}=T_{K E}\left(R_{4}^{ \pm}\right)$, it then follows—due to the projective nature and the non-disjoint property of these corner events $T_{K}^{ \pm} \rightleftarrows T_{E}^{\mp}$ and $T_{K}^{ \pm} \neq T_{E}^{ \pm}$, so that in the $R_{4}^{ \pm}$ the  antiparallel time arrows $T_{K}^{ \pm} \rightarrow T_{E}^{ \pm}$ exist. Such a time arrow  therefore goes back to the structure of the $R_{6}$ par excellence, because the  asymmetry of the spherical trinities $T_{K}$ and $T_{E}$ exists. Equation (49) must therefore be supplemented by $T_{K}^{+} \rightarrow T_{E}^{+} \hat{=} T_{K}^{-} \rightarrow T_{E}^{-} \hat{=} T_{K}^{+}$,  thereby closing the aonic-temporal circle of the world.

Since the $\Gamma_{k m}^{i}$ appear only as mixed-variant 3rd-order tensor components with respect to regular affinities, this also holds for the micromare functions $\left\{\begin{array}{cc}i \\ k & m\end{array}\right\}$, so that here the general invariance  under the Poincaré group is restricted, whereas, on the other hand,  $\widehat{\}}$ appears in (8) as the state function of the metric structural state. 

A heuristic investigation of this situation must start from the  phenomenological energy density tensor $T_{i k}$ of the macromare approximation $R_{i k}-\frac{1}{2} g_{i k} R=\alpha T_{i k}$ of (8a). The canonical energy density tensors in the spaces $R_{ \pm 4}$ and $R_{4}$ are always iterations of field tensors. Thus, it stands to reason that $T_{i k}$ is also the iteration of a uniform field tensor  $M_{i k}$ of the $R_{6}$ state in the macromolecular range. It would then be  $T_{i k}=\sum_{v=1}^{6} M_{i v} M_{v k}$, so that it must be a matter of determining the $M_{i k}$ from this system, since the phenomenological $T_{i k}$ are known. Since $T_{i k}=T_{k i}^{*}$ holds, it can only be $M_{i k}= \pm M_{k i}^{*}$, because only in this way does the iteration in general form lead to a Hermitian tensor. If, for brevity, the tensors are written as square matrices of type 6 according to $\left(T_{i k}\right)_{6}=\hat{T}$ and $\left(M_{i k}\right)_{6}=\hat{M}$, then the determination of $\hat{M}$ amounts to determining $\hat{M}=\sqrt{\hat{T}}$ in the iteration that now appears as $\hat{T}=\hat{M}^{2}$. First, $\hat{T}=\hat{T}^{x}$ and $\hat{M}= \pm \hat{M}^{x}$, as well as the regularity $\operatorname{rg} \hat{T}=\operatorname{rg} \hat{M}=6$ with $\operatorname{def} \hat{T}=\operatorname{def} \hat{M}=0$ can be established as a consequence of the tensor character.

Namely, if a coordination selector can be oriented by $\bar{e}_{i}$,  then there must also be general oriented functional selectors  $\bar{C}_{i}=\bar{e}_{i} C_{i}$ , where the selector laws $C_{i} ; n=\varphi_{i}\left(n_{k}\right)_{1}^{L}$ are intended to represent metronic scalar components. Using $\sum_{i=1}^{L} \bar{e}_{i} \varphi_{i}=\bar{\varphi}$ and  $\sum_{i=1}^{L} \bar{e}_{i} C_{i}=\bar{C}$, a general metronic vector field  over the $L$-dimensional metronic argument using the oriented functional selector $\bar{C}$, as described by $\bar{\varphi}=\bar{C} ; n$. This metronic vector, in turn, can be interpreted as a metronic tensor of the first order. If $T_{i_{1} \cdots i_{m}}$ is the component of some  metronic tensor $m \bar{T}={ }^{[m]}\left[T_{i_{1}} \cdots i_{m}\right]_{L}$ of $m \geqq 1$ (tensor rank), then, according to the concept of the metronic vector, this component is composed, as shown in $T_{i_{1} \cdots i_{m}}=\prod_{k=1}^{m} \varphi_{i_{k}}$, from metronic vector components, from which $m \leqq L$ is immediately apparent. On the other hand, $\varphi_{i_{k}}=C_{i_{k}} ; n$ and $\prod_{k=1}^{m} \varphi_{i_{k}}=\prod_{k=1}^{m} C_{i_{k}} ; n=\left(\prod_{k=1}^{m} C_{i_{k}}\right) ; n$ always hold, so that in this way a tensor functional selector $m \bar{C}={ }^{[m]}\left[\prod_{k=1}^{m} C_{i_{k}}\right]_{L}$ of degree $m \leqq L$ has been defined, which, according to ${ }^{m} \bar{T}={ }^{m} \bar{C} ; n$, yields a metronic tensor of degree $m$ through  its action on the indeterminate sequence of positive integers $n$, whereby metronic scalar functions result for $m=0$, which were already discussed in the preceding section.

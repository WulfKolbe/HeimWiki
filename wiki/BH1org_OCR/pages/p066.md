Based on relations (5) through (5b), it is now possible to transfer the $R_{4}$construction of the timelike front and rear cones, known from the theory of relativity, to the $R_{6}$. Here, too, with respect to $x_{4}$, the $R_{4}$subspace (relative to a  manifest world point), a double-shell hyperbolic space appears, each enclosed by a conical asymptotic space, such that  all (factually past) world points lying earlier with respect to the current reference world point and all (potentially future) world points are either points in these double-shell hyperbolic spaces or points in the conic asymptote spaces. The world points of these asymptote spaces are always points on world lines that possess the character of geodesic zero lines. Whereas in the pseudo-Euclidean case these double-shell hyperbolic spaces and the associated conical spaces are $R_{4}$ , this symmetry of the subspaces in $x_{5}$ and $x_{6}$ is no longer necessarily given. An  analysis of this construction of $R_{6}$ shows that the constancy of $\hat{C}$  requires at least $\beta=$ const or, more generally, $\vec{Y}=$ const. The invariance of the possible world tensors with respect to the regular affinities of the orthogonal affinity matrix $\hat{C}=$ const can therefore only apply to worldlines that satisfy at least $v \sim u$ (i.e., $\beta=$ const) or $\vec{Y}=$ const.

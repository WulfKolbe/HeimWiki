In this formulation, the $d \vec{z}_{p}^{ \pm}$ appear as the resultants of the corresponding vectorial coordinate differentials. In $\vec{z}_{p}^{ \pm}=\vec{\varepsilon}_{p}^{ \pm} z_{p}^{ \pm}$, the resulting orientations $\vec{\varepsilon}_{p}^{ \pm}$do not necessarily form a normalized orthogonal system, and the transformations $\vec{z}_{p}^{ \pm}\left(x_{1}, \ldots, x_{4}\right)$ belong to subgroups (gauge-invariant: -, non-gauge-invariant: +)  of the global Poincaré group in $R_{4}$. As vectorial line elements  of the $z_{p}^{ \pm}$, we then obtain $d \vec{s}_{ \pm}=\sum_{p=1}^{4} d \vec{z}_{p}^{ \pm}$ with $\vec{z}_{p}^{ \pm}=\vec{\varepsilon}_{p}^{ \pm} z_{p}^{ \pm}$. 

Since the structures are metric deviations from the pseudo-Euclidean nature of empty spacetime, a distinction must be made between co- and contravariant indices.

In general, $\varphi$ is not defined solely over a simple tensor $n$. Rather, $1 \leqq i \leqq L<\infty$ tensors $n_{i}$ in the integer intervals $\kappa_{i} \leqq n_{i} \leqq N_{i}<\infty$ with $\kappa_{i} \geqq 1$ a $L$-dimensional  metronic lattice in a $R_{L}$, such that $\varphi=\varphi\left(n_{1} \ldots n_{I}\right)$ holds,  for which, for brevity, $\varphi\left(n_{1} \ldots n_{L}\right)=\varphi\left(n_{i}\right)_{1}^{L}$ will be set  in what follows. In this case, the $\varphi$ slope can also be examined, and  in particular, $\partial_{i} \varphi=\varphi-\varphi\left(\ldots ., n_{i}-1, \ldots\right)$ holds, while for the total  change, $\partial=\sum_{i=1}^{L} \partial_{i}$ can be derived. 
When forming the total change $\partial^{K}$ with an integer $K \geqq 1$, the $\partial_{i}$ appear in iterations; however, the sequence of these  steps always satisfies $\left(\partial_{i} \times \partial_{k}\right)_{-}=0$, by analogy with partial infinitesimal  differentiation.

For the trace $i=k$, however, the following holds:  $R_{. k m p}^{k}=\Gamma_{k p, m}^{k}-\Gamma_{k m, p}^{k}+\Gamma_{m s}^{k} \Gamma_{k p}^{s}-\Gamma_{p s}^{k} \Gamma_{k m}^{s}=A_{m p} \rightarrow C_{p} \varphi_{k m}^{k}=$  $=\lambda_{p}(k, m) \varphi_{k m}^{k}$, where the antisymmetric transposition $A_{m p}=$  $=-A_{p m}$ with $A_{m m}=0$. This behavior of the last trace can be traced back to the antisymmetry of $R_{. k m p}^{i .}$ in $m$ and $p$; for $R_{. k m m}^{i .}=0$ also holds. If, according to the correspondence principle, $C_{p} \varphi_{k m}^{i} \rightarrow R_{. k m p}^{i}$ the algebraic properties of the curviest track are transferable to $C_{p} \varphi_{k m}^{i}$ , then $C_{m} \varphi_{k m}^{i}=0$ and, consequently, $\lambda_{m}(k, m) \varphi_{k m}^{i}=0$ would also have to hold. The $\varphi_{k m}^{i}$ vanish only in the case of Euclidean geometry or when referring to geodesic coordinates, but in general, $\varphi_{k m}^{i} \neq 0$ holds if this metric function represents the deviation of the structure from the Euclidean space. The 64 possible eigenvalue spectra $\lambda_{p}(k, m)$ for each covariant indexing, on the other hand, are discrete eigenvalue spectra of structural levels  (equivalent to energetic states) and thus as yet unknown number-theoretic functions of integers. It is entirely conceivable that, despite $\varphi_{k m}^{i} \neq 0$, some of these spectra remain empty in principle, such that $\lambda_{m}(k, m) \varphi_{k m}^{i}=0$ is generally satisfied by $\lambda_{m}(k, m)=0$.

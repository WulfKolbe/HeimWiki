In the case of elementary corpuscles, on the other hand, $m_{0}>0$ always holds, so that, according to our terminology, these particles possess the property of ponderability. 

When we speak of elementary structures of matter, these  may not only refer to ponderable elementary particles; rather, the imponderable photons  and hypothetical gravitons also appear to us as such elementary structures, so that  it seems justified to us to introduce the term “elementary matter field quantum” ( $M q$ ) as a generic term for both ponderable and imponderable elementary structures. This concept of the $M q$ inevitably broadens the problem framework already outlined in the preliminary remarks. 

The key must be to describe the totality of all $M q$ in a unified manner; for a unified theory of the material world can  only be a unified theory of these $M q$. A corresponding structural theory may be suitable for such a description. A method must also be developed to determine how the spectrum of ponderable $M q$ can be separated and how a set of fundamental symmetries that is as complete as possible can be constructed, whose invariance requirements underpin a general relativistic basic dynamics of the  elementary particles. 

We consider it entirely conceivable that these fundamental symmetries of the basic dynamics could turn out to be higher-order symmetries.

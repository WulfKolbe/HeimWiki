To date, it has not been possible empirically to detect even a qualitative trace of transverse gravitational waves from gravitational radiation (WEBER and WHEELER). Furthermore, despite the gravitational-dynamic experiment (*) through (*b), the gravitational field appears in all empirical potential measurements to be a non-gauge-invariant field, because, in principle, $\sigma-\sigma_{(0)} \neq 0$ and $\operatorname{div} \vec{\mu} \neq 0$ remain. 

On the other hand, however, temporal potential disturbances of the field must certainly exist, which are actually observable as tidal effects. For these reasons, $\beta>0$ is assumed in what follows; however,  the following construction remains unchanged even if $\beta<0$ were to be true.

Similarly, $\left(\mathbf{d}_{\mathbf{1}}\right)$ can be expressed in a $\hat{A}_{-}$form in the $R_{-4}$ by an anti-Hermitian field tensor $F_{k m}=-F_{m k}^{*}$ of the electromagnetic field in $R_{-4}$ by a vector divergence of the tensor field, which is proportional to an electric quaternion current. There also exists a dual tensor for $F_{k m}$. 

In fact, the spacetime constructions $R_{ \pm 4}$ with $x_{+4}=\omega t$ as well as $x_{-4}=\mathrm{ict}$ and common $R_{3}$ coordinates or the  matrices $\hat{A}_{ \pm}$ are merely fictitious auxiliary constructions; for a spacetime  $R_{+4}$, in which only gravitational field structures exist, lacks physical reality just as much as a spacetime that is determined solely by gravity-free $M q$. In real spacetime $R_{4}$, the $M q$ appear as sources of gravitational fields, and the corresponding gravitational fields always appear as a single unit. The $R_{ \pm 4}$ cannot be regarded as tangential spacetimes to $R_{4}$; rather, there appears to be a  “entanglement” to exist here, such that the unit defined in $R_{3}$—consisting of a field source and a gravitational field—exists both in a $R_{-4}$ (with the  $M q$ as the field source) and, with respect to the gravitational field, in the  $R_{+4}$. It must therefore be a matter of first constructing this true $R_{4}$ from a synthesis.

If the variants $i=\tilde{i}$ are antihermetic, then this relation describes a linear combination of the contravariant antihermetic condenser components with the hermetic ones (since $s$ runs along the  hermetic coordinates). 
The assumption of $i=\tilde{i}$ is valid; for $\left[\begin{array}{c}\tilde{i} \\ k l\end{array}\right]=\gamma^{\tilde{i}} \underline{s}\left[s_{s k l}\right] \neq 0$  is a linear combination of the purely covariant condenser components, since for the antihermetic fundamental selectors  $\gamma^{\underline{\tilde{i}} \underline{s}}=$ const holds with respect to the hermetic coordinates. If, on the other hand, $i$ is also hermetic, then the selector equation obviously describes the structure of the condensation stage in question, and the coefficients $b_{i}^{(k l)}$ are defined by the eigenvalue ratios.  The selector equation can therefore be multiplied by $b_{i}^{(k l)}$ and summed over 
$1 \leqq i \leqq q$. These linear combinations of the covariant condenser components, in turn, define covariant selectors $\phi_{k l}=b_{i}^{(k l)}\left[\begin{array}{cc}i & i \\ k & l\end{array}\right]$. Since, furthermore, $b_{i}^{(k l)} b_{s}^{(k l)}\left[\begin{array}{cc}i \\ k & l\end{array}\right] ;()\left[\begin{array}{cc}s \\ k & l\end{array}\right]=$  $=\left(b_{i}^{(k l)}\left[\begin{array}{cc}i & \\ k & l\end{array}\right]\right) ;()\left(b_{s}^{(k l)}\left[\begin{array}{cc}s \\ k & l\end{array}\right]\right)=\phi_{k l}^{2}$, the selector equation becomes $\left((a(k, l)-1) \underline{\partial}_{l}-\sum_{m \neq l} \underline{\partial}_{m}\right) ; \phi_{k l}+\phi_{k l}^{2}=\lambda(k, l) \phi_{k l}$ in this way. With the effect $\phi_{k l} ; n=\varphi_{k l}$, this selector equation becomes the partial metron differential $\left((a(k, l)-1) \underline{\partial}_{l}-\sum_{m \neq l} \underline{\partial}_{m}\right) ; \varphi_{k l}+\varphi_{k l}^{2}=\lambda(k, l) \varphi_{k l}$.  The Hermitian problem, precisely formulated in this way, can be integrated metronically once after a few transformations. Using the normalized orthogonal system of the $q$ hermetic coordinates $\left(\bar{e}_{i} \bar{e}_{k}\right)_{q}=\hat{E}$ and $\bar{a}_{k l}=\frac{\bar{e}_{l}}{\alpha_{l}}(a(k, l)-1)-\sum_{m \neq l} \bar{e}_{m} / \alpha_{m}$,

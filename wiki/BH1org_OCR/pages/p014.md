The tensor field is denoted by $\overline{\operatorname{div}}_{n}$, and the tensor divergence, which extends the tensor degree, is denoted by $\widehat{\operatorname{div}}_{n}$. 

However, the theorems of infinitesimal calculus no longer apply,  because the $R_{n}$ is structured by geometric fundamental units $\tau>0$, then the symbols defined in Chapter III are used. 

In all physical discussions, the International System of Units is used, so that the underedived empirical natural constants are the influence constant $\varepsilon_{0}$, the induction constant $\mu_{0}$, as well as  Newton’s gravitational constant $\gamma$ and the action quantum  $h=2 \pi \hbar$. To avoid ambiguity, the units of numerical values are specified—in [] if necessary—after the magnitude.

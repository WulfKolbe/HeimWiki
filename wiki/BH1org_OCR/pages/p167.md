If the geodesic lines forming the metronic grid are  taken as parameter functions of the geodesic system $x^{\underline{i}}$ from $R_{N(0)}$ , then these grid lines satisfy the system of equations $\ddot{x}^{i}+\Gamma_{k l}^{i} \dot{x}^{k} \dot{x}^{l}=0$. If $p$ is the parameter, then the same applies to the metric numbers $n^{\underline{i}}(p)$ in $R_{N(0)}$, where $p$ must also appear metronized. Accordingly, $\ddot{x}^{\underline{i}}=\alpha_{i} \widehat{\partial}_{p}^{2} n^{\underline{i}}$ and $\dot{x}^{\underline{i}}=\alpha_{i} \widehat{\partial}_{p} n^{\underline{i}}$ hold, while the translation field component results in a condenser effect 
$\Gamma_{k l}^{i} \rightarrow\left[\begin{array}{c}i \\ k l\end{array}\right] \stackrel{(c d)}{(a b)} ; n$. This means that for the metronic lattice, the equation $\partial_{p}^{2} n^{\underline{i}}+\frac{\alpha_{k} \alpha_{l}}{\alpha_{i}} \partial_{p} n^{k} \partial_{p} n^{\underline{l}}\left[\begin{array}{c}i \\ k l\end{array}\right] \stackrel{(c d)}{(a b)} ; n=0$ holds, specifically with respect to the hyperstructure $C_{0}$ of the $R_{N(0)}$. If, on the other hand, we consider the hyperstructure $C_{\xi}$ of the $R_{N}$ , then the infinitesimal $\ddot{\xi}^{i}=0$ with $\Gamma_{k l}^{i}=0$ holds, which, when substituted into $\Gamma_{k l}^{i} \rightarrow\left[\begin{array}{c}i \\ k l\end{array}\right] \frac{(c d)}{(a b)} ; n$ 
yields $\left[\begin{array}{c}i \\ k l\end{array}\right] \stackrel{(c d)}{(a b)} ; n=0$, so that $\left[\begin{array}{c}i \\ k l\end{array}\right]\left(\begin{array}{l}c d \\ (a b)\end{array}+=0\right.$ becomes identical to the zero selector. With respect to $C_{\xi}$, $\left[\begin{array}{c}\widehat{c d} \\ -a b\end{array}\right]_{(\xi)}=\widehat{0}$ therefore holds, whereas this condenser, relative to $C_{0}$ or any other system, remains distinct from the zero schema.

If,  nevertheless, these $L$ fine-structure selectors $c_{i}$ are nevertheless required as functional selectors  of the $p$-dimensional subgrid, they can also be traced back to the $N$ lattice selectors. To do this, the domain $\Omega_{N}$ of ${ }^{2} \bar{\gamma} ; n$ is projected onto the simple tensors $R_{p}$, which is $N>p$ (the $R_{p}^{(i)}$ are subspaces here) and yields the regions $\Omega_{i}$ in these subspaces as projections. According to the continuous connection condition for all $\tau$, the metrons are geodetically bounded by the $\xi^{\underline{l}}$ of the corresponding  $R_{p}^{(i)}$, such that for a volume difference 
$\Delta V_{i}=\prod_{l=1}^{p} \Delta \xi_{(i)}^{l}=\Delta \int_{V_{i}} \prod_{l=1}^{p} d \xi_{(i)}^{l}$ in $1 \leqq i \leqq L$ follows. Metronization yields $\lim \Delta V_{i}=\partial V_{i}$, and the metron condition requires  $\tilde{\partial} V_{i}=\tau$ in all simple tensors. Substituting this metronic limit relation yields $\int_{\tau} \prod_{l=1}^{p} d \xi_{(i)}^{l}=\tau$, and this relation can be integrated metronically along $1 \leqq \nu \leqq n_{i}$.

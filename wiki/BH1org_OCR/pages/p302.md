This cosmogonic relationship will demonstrate, on the one hand, how the cosmic motion of the $R_{3}$ is to be understood, and that, on the other hand, this constant updating establishes a polymetry of all world structures, the existence of which could already be surmised through the heuristic conclusion leading to (9). Applying this polymetry to (19) then explicitly provides a polymetric description of the hermetic forms $a$ through $d$, such that, by separating the imaginary $a$ and $b$ from the complex $c$ and $d$, the pseudo-continuum in (27) can be eliminated. Subsequently, the properties of the c- and d-terms are analyzed, and it is shown that the fundamental symmetry encompasses only a very small set of invariance conditions, which, however, must hold exactly, while a number of higher-order symmetries can also be derived, though these can be broken.

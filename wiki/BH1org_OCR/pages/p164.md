The functional selector fundamentally characterizes the change in position of each metronic degree of condensation, such that this condenser, or rather, fundamental condenser, describes the metronic condensation state of the hyperstructure of $R_{N}$ with respect to $R_{N(0)}$ in the most universal form. First, the covariant form $\Gamma_{i k l}(\tau)$ must be described, whereby in the most general case ${ }^{2} \bar{g}_{(a b)} \neq^{2} \bar{g}_{(a h)}$ and the corresponding fundamental selector should be an off-diagonal element of $\hat{\gamma}$ with the lattice kernels ${ }^{2} \bar{a}$ and ${ }^{2} \bar{b}$  (both non-Hermitian). Under these universal conditions ${ }^{2} \bar{g}_{(a b)}={ }^{2} \bar{\gamma}_{(a b)} ; n$ and ${ }^{2} \bar{\gamma}_{(a b)}=s p\left({ }^{2} \bar{a} \times{ }^{2} \bar{b}\right) \neq{ }^{2} \bar{\gamma}_{(a b)}^{x}$, covariant metronization becomes possible. 

In the symmetric or Hermitian special case $g_{i k}=g_{k i}$ or  $g_{i k}=g_{\mathrm{ki}}^{*}$, the $\Gamma_{i k m}$ can be uniquely represented in the familiar way by  $2 \Gamma_{i k m}=\frac{\partial g_{i m}}{\partial x^{k}}+\frac{\partial g_{k i}}{\partial x^{m}}-\frac{\partial g_{k m}}{\partial x^{i}}$. However, this explicit description is impossible in the general case of a non-Hermitian Cartan geometry; for an unambiguous relationship between the three-point symbols and the $g_{i k} \neq g_{k i}^{*}$ cannot be found without further additional assumptions regarding the $g_{i k}$ (D. K. sen, Fields and / or Particles). However, in the general case of this Cartan geometry, the transition  $g_{i k} \rightarrow g_{+i k}=g_{+k i}^{*}$ must yield the representation of covariant Hermitian three-point symbols. Furthermore, these $\Gamma_{i k m}$ must vanish for $g_{i k} \neq g_{k i}^{*}$ when  referring to geodesic coordinates. Since in this context $g_{i k}=$ becomes constant with respect to all coordinates and then  $\Gamma_{i k m}=0$ holds, the non-Hermitian three-point symbols can only depend in some way on the partial derivatives of the components $g_{i k}$. If, in general, $g_{i k}^{(a b)}=\gamma_{i k}^{(a b)} ; n$ holds with ${ }^{2} \bar{\gamma}_{(a b)} \neq{ }^{2} \bar{\gamma}_{(a b)}^{x}$  for $\tau>0$, then the non-Hermitian three-point symbols, according to $\Gamma_{i k m}^{(a b)} \rightarrow \Gamma_{i k m}^{(a b)}(\tau>0)$, become metronic functions which, regardless of their nature, are expressed according to $\Gamma_{i k m}^{(a b)}(\tau>0)=\left[_{i k m}\right]_{(a b)} ; n$ by non-Hermitian functional selectors $[i k m]_{(a b)}$

The Euclidean approximation of $R_{4}$ is ambiguous; since there is the possibility $R_{4} \rightarrow R_{ \pm 4}$, and it must be decided which of these projection possibilities applies to the zero-line process $\psi$;  since the physical interpretation of $\psi$ depends on this. Since the $R_{ \pm 4}$ differ only in $x_{4} \sim t$, the ambiguity is avoided by $x_{4}=C t$ , whereby $C=i c$ corresponds to $R_{-4}$, but $C=\omega$ the $R_{+4}$ according to $(* \mathbf{b})$ with $\beta>0$. In the second-order total spacetime equation for $\psi$, the quadratic term describes the  interactions between identical zero-line processes in $R_{4}$, so that, for an interpretation of $\psi$, it suffices to neglect this quadratic term $(d \psi / d r)^{2} \rightarrow 0$.

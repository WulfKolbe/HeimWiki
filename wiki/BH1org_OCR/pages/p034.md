The $x_{k}$ and $1 \leqq k \leqq 4$ are to form a right-handed Cartesian coordinate system in the following, to which the $R_{4}$ is referenced. The real coordinates $x_{1}, x_{2}$ and $x_{3}$ of the $R_{3}$ are supplemented by $x_{4}=$ ict (not interchangeable with the $R_{3}$coordinates) to form this Cartesian coordinate system $C$, whereby the normalized orthogonal system $\vec{e}_{i} \vec{e}_{k}=\delta_{i k}$ of unit vectors $\vec{e}_{k}$, in accordance with $\vec{x}_{k}=\vec{e}_{k} x_{k}$, orients these coordinates. If the $R_{4}$ is of a pseudo-Euclidean nature,  then its network of geodesic lines becomes identical to the $x_{k}$. This means that the events—as elements of physical processes, i.e., the points of the $R_{4}$—are distributed completely uniformly, and therefore no event structures can be distinguished. If, therefore, the Cartesian system $C$ is geodesic with respect to the $R_{4}$—geodetically, this means that the $R_{4}$ is empty and the general matter tensor $T_{i k}$ does not exist. If, on the other hand, a uniform material structure $T_{i k} \neq T_{k i}^{*}$ is given, then distinguishable event structures exist that differ from the empty $R_{4}$ and can be understood as deformations of the uniform event manifolds of the empty $R_{4}$. $T_{i k}$ describes the gravitational field source and its gravitational field uniformly in $R_{3}$, so that in the case of such a deformation of the event structures, the $R_{ \pm 4}$ (as auxiliary constructions) in $R_{4}$ "intertwine";  that is, these auxiliary constructions have a common $R_{3}$,  but different time counts $x_{ \pm 4}$, which, however, can always be mapped to the $x_{4}=$ ict of the  $R_{4}$. Thus, if a $M q$ exists in the $R_{4}$,  then this $M q$ as a non-Euclidean deformed—and thus distinguishable—event structure that can be related to the empty (i.e., pseudo-Euclidean) $R_{4}$ and thus can be related to the coordinates $x_{k}$. Since such event structures must also be understood as centers of interactions, and each of these interactions is characterized by typical invariance properties (symmetries), the metric character of the spacetime structure of the $M q$  is shaped by the invariance properties of the individual interactions. According to empirical evidence, there are at least four interactions, so  that their total number $n$ must lie within the interval $4 \leqq n<\infty$.

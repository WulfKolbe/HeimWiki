In $R_{3}$, so-called “antigravity”—induced by negative or imaginary ponderable $m_{0}$—cannot exist. However, $R_{-}$ shows that  in the astrophysical realm, there can indeed be extremely high mass densities  of degenerate matter, although the question of the  existence of so-called “black holes” may remain open for the time being.  It should also be noted that $\tau$ from (15) should not be interpreted in the sense of a “world ether.” Rather, the key must be to synthesize the consequences (8) and (15) from (2) into a unified description. However, because of (15b), a formal methodology must first be developed under the assumption $\tau>0$.

In equation (3), the right-hand side consists of spatial energy densities  $\varepsilon_{k m}^{(p)}$, which, according to $\lambda_{(p)}(k, m) \varphi_{k m}^{(p)} \sim \varepsilon_{k m}^{(p)}$, , from which an interpretation of metric structural levels can be derived. According to (c) and (2), the $R_{k m}$ are determined by the spacetime densities of the action quanta or the spatial densities of quantum energy levels due to $C_{p} \varphi_{k m}^{p} \rightarrow R_{k m}$ (macroscopic approximation), so that in the microscopic region these densities $\varepsilon_{k m}^{(p)}$ of quantum-like energy levels, in the sense of discrete steps, continuously alter the metric properties  of the $R_{4}$. In (3), the indices run independently of one another through the numbers 1 through 4 of the $R_{4}$ dimensions, so that (3)  contains a total of 64 operator equations for an equal number of point spectra  $\lambda_{p}(k, m)$. The additional relation (3a), which arises due to non-Hermiticity, would comprise 32 equations, but $\varphi_{k(k)}^{(k)}=\varphi_{(k) k}^{(k)}$ is completely evident, which leads to these four relations appearing twice and reduces the number of the 32 relations (3a) to 28. Since the $\lambda_{p}$ now determine the discontinuous structural field of spacetime with the property (3a), the 28 relations (3a) must be substituted into the 64 relations (3). The result of such a substitution is characterized by the fact that, in principle, 28 of the 64 point spectra remain empty, which must be attributable to the nature of the non-Hermitian spacetime structure itself. Consequently, there remain $64-28=36$ discontinuous energy densities $\lambda_{(p)} \varphi_{k m}^{(p)} \sim \varepsilon_{k m}^{(p)} \neq 0$  due to $\lambda_{p}(k, m) \neq 0$ the 36 remaining point spectra.

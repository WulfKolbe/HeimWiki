In the derivation of the $R_{6}$signature $(+++---)$, it was shown that, as a consequence of the micro- and macro-marian stabilities of the world, only three real coordinates are possible. This fact corresponds to  all elementary lengths $\delta x_{i}$ with $1 \leqq i \leqq 12$ of the $R_{12}$. 

From the hermetic $R_{6}$ structure, it follows that for the volumes $\Delta x_{1} \cdot \Delta x_{2} \cdot \Delta x_{3} \sim(\delta l)^{\alpha_{3}}, \Delta x_{4} \sim(\delta l)^{\alpha_{1}}$ and $\Delta x_{5} \cdot \Delta x_{6} \sim(\delta l)^{\alpha_{2}}$ with $\alpha_{k}=k \leqq 3$, so that for the set of exponents, $E=\left\{\boldsymbol{\alpha}_{1}, \boldsymbol{\alpha}_{2}, \boldsymbol{\alpha}_{3}\right\}=\{1,2,3\}$ can be set. With these $\boldsymbol{\alpha}_{k}$, the primordial elements of the set $U=\left\{H_{1}, H_{2}, H_{3}\right\}$ can therefore be  expressed according to  $\alpha_{k} H_{k}=1$ , which determine the $g_{k}$ from the spherical diameters at the temporal origin of the world. If $o_{j}$ is again a general operation, then $x o_{j} x=\boldsymbol{\alpha}_{i}$ with $\boldsymbol{\alpha}_{i}=0$ up to $\alpha_{i}=3$ correspond to an extension $U \rightarrow U^{\prime \prime}$. For reasons of symmetry, the relations $x o_{j} x=\alpha_{i}$ must satisfy a symmetry with respect to the cardinal number complex $K=\{1 ; 2 ; 3\}$. 

In what follows, we will first determine the corresponding set of operations from the power set  $P(M)=\{\{a\},\{b\},\{a, b\}\}$.  In $P(M)$, the subsets $T_{\alpha}(M)=\{\{a, b\}\}$ as well as $T_{\beta}(M)=\{\{a\},\{a, b\}\}$ and $T_{\gamma}(M)=\{\{a\},\{b\},\{a, b\}\}$ are obviously contained.

Furthermore, no singularities in the form of points of infinity may appear in these mappings, and the coordinate transformations involved must be continuous (at least in the macroscopic regime).  This means that the permitted transformations $x_{k}^{\prime}\left(x_{1} \ldots x_{6}\right)$ in the mapping of world structures must also be transformations of the global Poincaré group. The world structures are therefore, in principle, invariant with respect to this transformation group. Since the world structures to be described are metric deformations of event manifolds of a non-Euclidean nature, a distinction must again be made between covariant and contravariant quantities. For brevity, the summation rule $A_{k} A^{k}$, extended over $1 \leqq k \leqq 6$, is used again, whereby the grouping of the indices precludes this summation.

In what follows, metronic vectors are indicated by underlining. 

Every metronomic region $n_{i}$ must, in a geometric interpretation, have the dimensioning $p$ of $\tau$ , i.e., the metronization of the $R_{p}$  with $p \leqq L$ implies a metronization of the $1 \leqq i \leqq L$ coordinates $x_{i}$  of the $R_{L}$, so that the $L$ metronic regions $n_{i}$ reflect these coordinate metronizations. But if this is the case, then—regardless of how the metronic region is geometrically oriented— each sequence $n_{i}$ of metron numbers must be assigned a vector orientation $\bar{e}_{i}$ with $\left|\bar{e}_{i}\right|=1$  , which suggests an extension of the coordination selector to an oriented coordination selector $\bar{Z}(i)=\bar{e}_{i}()_{i}$,  whereby the $L$ individual orientations $\bar{e}_{i}$ need not necessarily  be orthogonal to one another; that is, the quadratic matrix of the  type $L$ of the orientations $\left(\bar{e}_{i} \bar{e}_{k}\right)_{L}=\hat{A}$ can generally be $\hat{A} \neq \hat{E}$  , where $\hat{A}\left(x_{i}\right)_{1}^{L}$ holds with $x_{i}\left(n_{i}\right)$.

If permutability is allowed only within these subsets and there are $u$ real elements of the set that are $2 v$ complex conjugate to one another, then the group $S_{u} \times\left(S_{v} \times S_{v}\right)$ follows.  During the open time interval $0<t<v$ (chronon according to (35a))  the groups $S_{u}$ and $S_{v}$ remain unchanged, so that a group  $\left[S_{u} \times\left(S_{v} \times S_{v}\right)\right] \times\left[S_{u} \times\left(S_{v} \times S_{v}\right)\right]$, arising from $\pm q$, exists, but  for $t \geqq \vartheta$ there exists a group $\left[S_{w} \times\left(S_{u} \times S_{u}\right)\right] \times\left[S_{w} \times\left(S_{u} \times S_{u}\right)\right]$.

The $L$-fold metronic tensors in the metric $R_{N}$ described in this way are still subject to the constraint of primitive structuring, i.e., the $L$ simple tensors each consist  only of a sequence of metrons that are adapted to one another in accordance with the requirement of  continuous connection. Even in the case of a non-Euclidean $R_{p}$, the simple, primitively structured tensorium consists only of a sequence of geodetically bounded metrons in the form of a geodetic hypersurface strip. In actual tensors, however, hyperstructures must be present, so that the simple tensors exhibit fine structures in the form of metronic lattices, which are bounded by geodesic lattices (determined by the metric structure). A corresponding extension of the concept of the metronic tensor must therefore still be carried out within the framework of metron theory.

If a linearly acting state selector $\Gamma=L^{\prime}$ is present, such that $L^{\prime} ; \psi=\lambda \psi$ is a homogeneous selector equation of the 1st , then even if there is inhomogeneity, the eigenfunctions of $L^{\prime} ; \varphi_{\nu}=\lambda_{\nu} \varphi_{\nu}$ must form the actual state function $\varphi$ in the sense of a linear combination $\varphi=\sum_{v} c_{v} \varphi_{v}$, where the $c_{v}=$ are constants.

The concept of “condensation” is not only relative in nature but also metaphorical. If the factor $K_{k} \geqq 1$ is introduced into $\partial_{l} N_{k} \geqq \delta_{l k}$,  so that $\partial_{l} N_{k}=K_{k} \delta_{k l}$, then $K_{l}$ can be regarded as a measure of the degree of condensation, because $K_{k}=1$ denotes the absence  and $K_{k}>1$ the presence of condensation along $x_{k}$. It is $K_{k}=\sum_{l=1}^{N} \partial_{l} N_{k}=\partial N_{k}$ and because of $\sum_{i=1}^{N} \partial_{i} n_{k}=\partial_{n}=1$.  If $\sum_{\mathrm{k}=1}^{\mathrm{N}} \partial N_{k}=\partial \sum_{k=1}^{N} N_{k}=\partial \underline{N}$ is set, then either $\partial \underline{N}=\sum_{k=1}^{N} K_{k} \partial n_{k}=\bar{K} \partial \bar{n}$ or $\underline{N}=\boldsymbol{S} \bar{K} \partial \bar{n}$ follows. This relationship  $\underline{N}=\overline{S K} \overline{\boldsymbol{\gamma}} \bar{n}, \quad \bar{n}=\sum_{k=1}^{N} \bar{e}_{k} Z(k) ; n$

From the representation $\partial_{l} N_{k}=K_{k} \delta_{k l}$, a relationship between $\bar{K}$ and  the lattice core follows directly. Namely, whenever the metronic lattice for ${ }^{2} \bar{\gamma} \neq{ }^{2} \bar{E}$ is related to that of ${ }^{2} \bar{\gamma}={ }^{2} \bar{E}$,  $\partial_{l} N_{k}=\partial_{x l} \xi_{k}\left(x_{m}\right)_{1}^{N}=\frac{1}{\alpha_{l}} \partial_{l} \xi_{k}$, which, when compared with $\partial_{l} N_{k}=K_{k} \delta_{k l}$, yields $\partial_{l} \xi_{k}=\alpha_{l} K_{k} \delta_{k l}=\alpha_{l} K_{k} \partial_{l} n_{k}$ or, after summation, $\partial \xi_{k}=K_{k} \sum_{l=1}^{N} \alpha_{l} \partial_{l} n_{k}=K_{k} \partial x_{k} \quad$.

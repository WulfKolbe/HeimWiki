If, according to (19), there are condensation stages $\bar{\lambda} \neq \overline{0}$, then these $\bar{\lambda}$ do not necessarily need to be defined in all coordinates of the $R_{6}$. Rather, the spectrum $\bar{\lambda}$ can refer to any $k$  dimensional subspace $V_{k}$ with $1 \leqq k \leqq 6$, such that  in this $V_{k}$ the fundamental selector of the hyperstructure differs from the tensorial unit selector ${ }^{2} \bar{\gamma} ; n \neq{ }^{2} \bar{E}$, while $6-k \geqq 0$  coordinates remain pseudo-Euclidean, so that outside the  $k$ dimensions, the $V_{k}$ lattice and hyperselectors coincide. We must first investigate which of these $Z_{1}$ possibilities actually describe micromare world structures and what the semantics of these $\bar{\lambda}$ are like. Such a semantic interpretation of the $\bar{\lambda}$ in the possible $V_{k}$ metric structures obviously requires forms of a “hermeneutics of possible world geometry” within the $V_{k}$  (referred to briefly as forms of hermeneutics), such that this hermeneutics is ultimately oriented toward empirical physical facts. If, therefore, such a hermetry $\bar{\lambda}\left(V_{k}\right)$ exists in $k$ world dimensions, then, for the sake of brevity, the $6-k$ of $\bar{\lambda}$ be described as anti-hermetic.

If the mapping law $\Delta s_{8} \rightarrow R_{4}$ is to be examined, then it must be taken into account that, according to (3c), an energy density tensor exists in $R_{6}$ , but not in $R_{8}$. Furthermore, the semantics of the coordinates $x_{7}, x_{8}$ can be attributed a similar organizational nature to  $x_{5}$ and $x_{6}$. Because of this relationship and the hermetic structure of the set of all $R_{6}$coordinates, the transformation steps $\left(x_{7}, x_{8}\right) \rightarrow\left(x_{5}, x_{6}\right) \rightarrow x_{4} \rightarrow\left(x_{1}, x_{2}, x_{3}\right)$ are therefore to be expected. The hermetization of $x_{4}$ as a consequence of the changes in $\left(x_{7}, x_{8}\right)$ and $\left(x_{5}, x_{6}\right)$. 

As already shown in IV, 3, a mapping of the Hermitian form $a$ into $R_{4}$ is possible if $a$ can be represented by a geodesic null line. Since the mapping from the $R_{12}$ is performed via the organizational coordinates in the steps  $\left(x_{7}, x_{8}\right) \rightarrow\left(x_{5}, x_{6}\right) \rightarrow x_{4}\left(x_{4}^{ \pm}\right) \rightarrow R_{3}$, the following hold: $\sum_{k=5}^{8} \Delta s_{k}^{2}=0$ and  $\Delta s_{4}^{2}+\Delta s_{5}^{2}+\Delta s_{6}^{2}=0$. The transition $x_{4}^{-} \rightarrow R_{3}$ in $R_{-4}$ characterizes  the mapping of $b$hermetics as time condensation in the $R_{3}$, so that  $\sum_{k=1}^{4} \Delta s_{k}^{2}=0$ applies only to this $b$ hermetics (photons). From these quadratic forms, it follows that $-\left(\Delta s_{7}^{2}+\Delta s_{8}^{2}\right)+\Delta s_{4}^{2}=0$, which, due to $\Delta s_{7} \ll \Delta s_{8}$, leads to the approximation $\Delta s_{4}^{2}-\Delta s_{8}^{2}=0$ or $\Delta s_{8}= \pm \Delta s_{4}$, respectively. Consequently, in the interval $-i k \delta l_{v} \leqq \Delta s_{8} \leqq i k \delta l_{v}$, we can substitute, which yields $-i k \delta l_{v} \leqq \Delta s_{4} \leqq i k \delta l_{v}$. Furthermore, $\Delta s_{8}=0$ is the most likely value, for which $\Delta s_{4}=0$ would then have to be set. Thus, if $x_{4}^{(0)}=i c t_{0}$ is given at a certain point in time, the overlap $x_{4}=i c t_{0}+\Delta s_{4}$ occurs, which is identical to the actual duration. Because of $x_{4}=i c t$, the following holds: $\Delta s_{4}=i c\left(t-t_{0}\right)$, where $i c t_{0}$ represents, as the mean value, so to speak, a “rest value” that can only be specified with an “uncertainty” $\Delta s_{4}$.

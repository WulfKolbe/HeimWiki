The coefficients of this homogeneous quadratic differential form  $g_{i k}^{(\beta)}$ or $g_{i k}$ are tensor components, specifically with respect to  invariance under the global Poincaré group. These  tensors are also field functions of the $x_{k}$ of the $C$ reference frame of a empty $R_{4}$. Here, $g_{i k}\left(x_{1}, x_{2}, x_{3}, x_{4}\right)$ is the field of the metric fundamental tensor, which characterizes the allowed event structures of the $R_{4}$ in invariant form, corresponding to the material structure  that is phenomenologically represented by $T_{i k} \neq T_{k i}$. The  symmetry of $g_{i k}^{(1)}=g_{k i}^{(1)}$ and $g_{i k}^{(3)}=g_{k i}^{(3)}$ becomes immediately evident in this representation, whereas $g_{i k}^{(2)} \neq g_{k i}^{(2) k}$ proves to be asymmetric, so that $g_{i k} \neq g_{k i}$ is also an asymmetric fundamental tensor.

Consequently, in the $R_{4}$ the non-Hermitian fundamental tensor of a general Cartan geometry is a consequence of a  spacetime geometrization of general interaction fields  of the $M q$.

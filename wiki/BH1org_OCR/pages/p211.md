The eigenvalues of the linear condenser aggregate $\psi_{k l}$ are the $\lambda_{k l}$, which are always algebraically given by components $\lambda_{m}(k, l)$. This means that the discrete eigenvalues of such condenser aggregates are always correlations of components of the metronic condensation stages from (19), which lie in discrete point spectra. Finally, according to (20a), $\lambda_{k l}<\infty$ always implies $a(k, l)-q \neq 0$ for $\lambda(k, l)<\infty$. 

Since $\psi_{k l} ; n$ takes the value 1 at the extremum and $\left(\lambda_{k l} \mu ; n\right)_{\text {ext }}=$  is $=\alpha+i \beta$, it follows that for the extrema, $e^{-\alpha} e^{-i \beta}=e^{-\alpha}(\cos \beta-i \sin \beta)=0$ holds,  which, due to $e^{-\alpha}>0$, holds only for $\cos \beta-i \sin \beta=0$ or $\cos \beta=0$ and  $\sin \beta=0$ is attainable for $\alpha<\infty$. The imaginary sequences of the nth terms of the metro series thus allow for four eigenvalue spectra in principle,  namely $\beta_{+}^{( \pm)}= \pm \frac{\pi}{2}\left(2 n_{+}+1\right)$ from $\cos \beta_{+}=0$ and $\beta_{-}^{( \pm)}= \pm \pi n_{-}$  from $\sin \beta_{-}=0$. These spectra, in turn, can be interpreted—due to the integer nature of the $n_{ \pm}$—as selectors $\varepsilon_{ \pm}^{( \pm)} ; n=\beta_{ \pm}^{( \pm)}$, namely $\varepsilon_{+}^{( \pm)}= \pm \frac{\pi}{2}\left(2()_{+}+E\right)$ and $\varepsilon_{-}^{( \pm)}= \pm \pi()_{-}$. Since, according to $n_{+} \equiv n_{-}$, the positive integers can be traversed in the same direction, the following complementarities result:

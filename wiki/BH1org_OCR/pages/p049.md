The $\varphi_{k m}^{p}$ should not be interpreted as probabilities;  for the $C_{p}$ act, due to $C_{p} \varphi_{k m}^{p} R_{k m}$, as functional operators in the sense of nonlinear combinations, so that the solutions do not superimpose additively as structural functions of the metric state of spacetime—which, however, is the prerequisite for the quantum-theoretical probability interpretation. The $C_{p}$ thus describe nonlinear metric states of a non-Hermitian spacetime per se,  which occur in metric levels $\lambda_{p}(k, m)$. On the other hand, these $\lambda_{p}(k, m)$ are equivalent to the energy masses of those material elementary structures that appear as spacetime deformations in the sense of the empirical $M q$. Due to the nonlinearity of spacetime states, the theoretical $M q$masses, there will be no uncertainty principles of quantum theory, but rather discrete masses consistent with the results of empirical high-energy physics. It is conceivable that the quantization difficulties in general relativity may have their cause in this fact; for in the transition of the nonlinear microstates  of a non-Hermitian spacetime into the macroscopic continuum, neglecting principle (c), equation (2) yields the system  (1), which, assuming the separation of the gravitational field and the field source as well as a Riemannian spacetime geometry, approximately yields the fundamental equations of general relativity. 

Since the sum $C_{p} \varphi_{k m}^{p}=\lambda_{p} \varphi_{k m}^{p}$ reaches $R_{k m}=R_{. k m p}^{p}$ on both sides in the approximation to the macroscopic continuum,  it stands to reason that to view this sum as the matrix trace of a superordinate principle of the fourth tensor degree, namely $C_{p} \varphi_{k m}^{i}-\lambda_{p} \varphi_{k m}^{i}=Q_{. k m p}^{i}$ with $Q_{. k m p}^{p}=0$.  Here, $Q_{. k m p}^{i} \neq 0$ would contradict the integer property $N_{i k}$ from $\omega_{i k}=h N_{i k}$ of principle (c), so that $Q_{. k m p}^{i}=0$ and thus $C_{p} \varphi_{k m}^{i}=$  $=\lambda_{p}(k, m) \varphi_{k m}^{i}$ must be required. The contradiction with (c) arises whenever $Q_{. k m p}^{i} \neq 0$ establishes the Hermitianity of $C_{p}$

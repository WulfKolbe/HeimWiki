If $n=\operatorname{const}(t)$ occurs over any time interval, then $n$ describes a neutron that is stable within this time interval and transitions into another when the boundary of the time interval is crossed. At $n=0$, no mass value $m$ is defined at all, while the maximum value lies at $n=1$. $n<0$ is not possible due to $\operatorname{Im}(m)=0$, especially since $m$ decreases more and more as $n$ increases.

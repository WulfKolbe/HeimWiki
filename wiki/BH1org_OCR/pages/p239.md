In this  domain of validity of $\hat{C}$, it is impossible to reach a geodetic zero line due to $0 \leqq \beta<1$. The complex condensations can therefore, in the third domain of validity with $\beta<1$, only approach the spacetime asymptotic cone but never reach it.

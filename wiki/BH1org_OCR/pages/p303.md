Regardless of this cell structure, according to Chapter II, the statics of a field are determined by the steady state of the dynamic equilibrium of microfluctuations in the $R_{3}$. The deformation of the cell structure is caused by the external  reduced charge field $e_{r}=\varepsilon_{ \pm} \sqrt{\eta}$ of the $p$ and $e^{-}$ in the $H$atom, but is described by a vector function $\bar{\varphi}(n)$ of the metron number $n$.

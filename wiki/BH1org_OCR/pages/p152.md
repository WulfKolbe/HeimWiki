c) In this step, the metric structure of the $R_{N}$ must be determined. The $1 \leqq k \leqq N$ coordinates $y_{k}$ of the $R_{N}$ obtain their  orientations $\bar{e}_{k}$ in such a way that $d \bar{s}=\sum_{k=1}^{N} \bar{e}_{k} d y_{k} \quad$ becomes possible, where the infinitesimal metric $d s^{2}=\sum_{i k=1}^{N} \bar{e}_{i} \bar{e}_{k} d y_{i} d y_{k} \quad$ can be formed. Here, the $y_{k}=y_{k}\left(x^{l}\right)_{1}^{N}$ are transformed into Cartesian contravariant $x^{k}$, and the behavior of $\left(\vec{e}_{i} \vec{e}_{k}\right)_{\lambda}$ is investigated,  which yields $d s^{2}=g_{i k} d x^{i} d x^{k}$—that is, ${ }^{2} g\left(x^{k}\right)_{1}^{N}$—relative to the $x^{k}$. If ${ }^{2} g$ of $R_{N}$ is known in this way, then from the $1 \leqq i \leqq N$ differential laws $\ddot{x}^{i}+\Gamma_{k l}^{i} \dot{x}^{k} \dot{x}^{l}=0$, the $N$ sets of geodesic lines can be determined, which, as a lattice of the metric structure, themselves become a coordinate system $\xi^{k}$. Because of $\ddot{\xi}^{k}=0$, the  transformations $x^{l}=x^{l}\left(\xi^{k}\right)_{1}^{N} \mathrm{zu} \quad \Gamma_{k l}^{i}=0$, that is, ${ }^{2} g=\mathrm{const}$, must be consistent with respect to $\xi^{k}$. However, if ${ }^{2} \bar{g}=$ is constant, then $g=\left|g_{i k}\right|_{N}=$ must also be constant. 

Since $g$ is identical to the square of the functional determinant 
$|g|=\left(\frac{\partial\left(x^{k}\right)_{1}^{N}}{\partial\left(\xi^{l}\right)_{1}^{N}}\right)^{2}$, $g=$ const also implies that $\frac{\partial\left(x^{k}\right)_{1}^{N}}{\partial\left(\xi^{l}\right)_{1}^{N}}=$ is const, from which $x^{k}\left(\xi^{l}\right)_{1}^{N}$ or, conversely, $\xi^{l}\left(x^{k}\right)_{1}^{N}$ can be determined.  This establishes the metrical structure of $R_{N}$.

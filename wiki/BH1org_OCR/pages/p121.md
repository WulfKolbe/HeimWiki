The concept of homogeneity is also applicable to a class of metronic functions. The function $\varphi$ is said to be homogeneous of integer  degree $h \geqq 1$ if $\varphi\left(t, n_{i}\right)_{1}^{L}=t^{h} \varphi\left(n_{i}\right)_{1}^{L}$ holds for some number $t \neq 0$. Here, $t \neq 0$ can take on any numerical value, but a metronic relation $\sum_{i=1}^{n} x_{i} \frac{\partial f}{\partial x_{i}}=h f$  with a metronic relation completely analogous to $f\left(t, x_{i}\right)_{1}^{n}=t^{h} f\left(x_{i}\right)_{1}^{n}$ is possible if $t=n \geqq 1$ also becomes an integer as the metronic parameter.

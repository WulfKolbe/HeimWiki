A general selector theory is possible that is such that the theory of metronic functions is contained within this general selector theory. In principle, a distinction must be made between coordination selectors and functional selectors. Not only $\phi$, but also $C_{k}$ are such functional selectors, although $\phi$ depends on several argument selectors as a combined functional selector, whereas $C_{k}$ are simple, i.e., non-combined, functional selectors. Based on this definition of a selector, it is evident that there must also be a zero selector and a unit selector, $0 ; n=0$ and $E ; n=1$, respectively. Likewise, it follows from the concept of a selector that all selectors must satisfy the associative and distributive laws with respect to addition and multiplication, whereas in general the commutative law applies only with respect to addition.

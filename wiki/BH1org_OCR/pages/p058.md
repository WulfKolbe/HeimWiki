In the $R_{4}$ subspace, the following holds for the functional determinant (in particular, for the permitted coordinate transformations): $w=\sqrt{-g}$, where the algebraic character of this functional determinant is determined by the metric determinant $g$. The $R_{6}$ is now merely the dimensional extension of spacetime, so that the algebraic character of the functional determinant must be preserved in this extension; for even in the $R_{6}$ world, invariance of admissible coordinate transformations under the global Poincaré group must be required.  For this reason, the signature options $b$ and $c$ are ruled out from the outset; that is, one must choose between the alternatives $a$ and $d$. 

According to the empirical principle (b), the physical world is governed by stabilities; in particular, the macroscopic gravitational field is such that gravitating bodies can move stably along closed Keplerian orbits (which, as a very good approximation, are conic sections), whereas in the micromacroscopic realm, the basis of the material world is characterized by the existence of stable ground states of the shell electrons in atomic structures.

On the one hand, the following remarks arose only during the revision of this volume; on the other hand, however, they relate to the cosmology chapter (Chapter V) of Volume II. This anticipation is possible because both volumes form a unified whole, such that Volume II remains unreadable without prior study of this volume. The following section can therefore be understood as an anticipated supplement to Chapter V in  Volume II.

# Equation M8

$$
\begin{aligned} & \partial_{\varphi} f=\frac{1}{\partial \varphi}(f(\varphi)-f(\varphi-\partial \varphi)), \text { also } \\ & f=f(\varphi), \quad \partial_{\varphi} f \partial \varphi=f(\varphi)-f(\varphi-\partial \varphi) \end{aligned}
$$

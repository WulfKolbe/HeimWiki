# Equation BH1org_OCR_EQ0067

$$
\frac{d p}{d x}=\lim _{x_{1} \rightarrow x} \frac{p\left(x_{1}\right)-p(x)}{x_{1}-x}=\lim _{h \rightarrow 0} \frac{1}{h}(p(x+h)-p(x))=\lim _{\Delta x \rightarrow 0} \frac{\Delta p}{\Delta x}
$$

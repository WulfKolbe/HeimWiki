# Equation BH1org_OCR_EQ0145

$$
\begin{aligned} & \frac{1}{p}(\kappa)_{l} ; p=\frac{1}{\alpha_{l}} \frac{1}{p} \partial_{l} p-\left[\begin{array}{c} s \\ l s \end{array}\right](\kappa)+; n, \text { und } \frac{1}{\alpha_{m}} \partial_{m} \frac{1}{p}(\kappa)_{l} ; p= \\ & =\frac{1}{\alpha_{l} \alpha_{m}} \partial_{m} \frac{1}{p} \partial_{l} p-\frac{1}{\alpha_{m}} \partial_{m}\left[\begin{array}{cc} s \\ l s \end{array}\right]_{+} ; n, \text { also } \\ & \frac{1}{\alpha_{m}} \partial_{m} \frac{1}{p}(\kappa)_{l} ; p-\frac{1}{\alpha_{l}} \partial_{l} \frac{1}{p}(\kappa)_{m} ; p=\frac{1}{\alpha_{l}} \partial_{l}\left[{ }_{m} s\right]_{+} ; n-\frac{1}{\alpha_{m}} \partial_{m}\left[{ }_{l} s\right]_{+} ; n, \end{aligned}
$$

# Equation BH1org_OCR_EQ0102

$$
=\sum_{i, l=1}^{L} \partial_{l} \partial_{i}\left(\partial_{i}()_{l}-\partial_{l}()_{i}\right)=\sum_{i, l=1}^{L} \partial_{i}^{2} \partial_{l}()_{l}-\sum_{i, l=1}^{L} \partial_{l}^{2} \partial_{i}()_{i}=0
$$

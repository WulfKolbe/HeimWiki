# Equation 20a

$$
\begin{aligned} & \Lambda_{k l}=\alpha_{l}(a(k, l)-1)^{-1}-\sum_{m \neq 1} \alpha_{m}, \quad(a(k, l)-q) \cdot \lambda_{k l}=\lambda(k, l), \\ & \left(\lambda(k, l), a(k, l), b_{i}^{(k l)}\right)=\sum_{m=1}^{q}\left(\lambda_{m}(k, l) \cdot a_{m}^{(k l)}, b_{m i}^{(k l)}\right), \\ & b_{m i}^{(k l)}=\left(\frac{a_{l i}}{a_{l k}}-\frac{a_{m i}}{a_{m k}}\right) \frac{a_{k m}}{a_{k l}}, \quad a_{m}^{(k l)}=\frac{a_{k m}}{a_{k l}}, \quad a_{m l} \lambda_{m}(m, l)=-\lambda_{l}(m, m), \\ & \lambda_{m}(\tilde{k}, l)=\lambda_{m}(k, \tilde{l})=\lambda_{m}(\tilde{k}, \tilde{l})=\lambda_{\tilde{m}}(k, l)=0, \end{aligned}
$$

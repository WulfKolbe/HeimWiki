# Equation BH1org_OCR_EQ0182

$$
\begin{aligned} & \bar{\psi}=\sum_{k, l=1}^{q} \lambda(k, l) \bar{b}_{(k l)}^{-1} \psi_{k l}=\sum_{k, l=1}^{q} \bar{e}_{i}\left[\begin{array}{c} i \\ k l \end{array}\right] \text { oder } \psi_{s}=\bar{e}_{s} \bar{\psi}= \\ & =\sum_{k, l=1}^{q} \bar{e}_{s} \bar{e}_{i}\left[\begin{array}{c} i \\ k l \end{array}\right]=\sum_{k, l=1}^{q} \delta_{i s}\left[\begin{array}{cc} i \\ k & l \end{array}\right], \text { beziehungsweise } \psi_{i}=\sum_{k, l=1}^{q}\left[\begin{array}{cc} i \\ k & l \end{array}\right], \end{aligned}
$$

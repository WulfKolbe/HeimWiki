# Equation 17

$$
\begin{aligned} & \Gamma ; \psi=\lambda \psi, \quad \varphi=\psi ; n, \quad \underset{\Omega}{\boldsymbol{S}}\left(\varphi^{*} \Gamma ; \varphi-\varphi(\Gamma ; \varphi)^{*}\right) \delta \Omega=0, \\ & \underset{\Omega}{S} \varphi \varphi^{*} \delta \Omega<\infty, \quad \lambda=\lambda^{*} . \end{aligned}
$$

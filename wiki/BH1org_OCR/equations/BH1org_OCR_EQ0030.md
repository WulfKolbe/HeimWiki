# Equation BH1org_OCR_EQ0030

$$
\begin{aligned} & d \vec{\eta}_{p}=\frac{\partial \vec{\eta}_{p}}{\partial x^{k}} d x^{k}, \text { so daß für die Metrik } \\ & d s^{2}=\sum_{p, q=1}^{6} \frac{\partial \vec{\eta}_{p}}{\partial x^{i}} \frac{\partial \vec{\eta}_{q}^{*}}{\partial x^{k}} d x^{i} d x^{k}=g_{i k} d x^{i} d x^{k}=g^{i k} d x_{i} d x_{k} \text { gilt. } \end{aligned}
$$

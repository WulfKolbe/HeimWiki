# Equation M20а

$$
\begin{aligned} & \varphi\left(x^{\underline{k}}\right)_{1}^{N} \rightarrow \phi ; n, \quad \frac{\partial \varphi}{\partial x^{k}} \rightarrow\left(\frac{\partial_{k} \phi}{\partial C^{\underline{k}}}\right) ; n, \quad d \varphi \rightarrow\left(\sum_{k=1}^{N} \partial_{\left(C^{\underline{k}}\right)} \phi\right) ; n, \\ & \partial C^{\underline{k}}=\sum_{i=1}^{N} \partial_{i} C^{\underline{k}}, \quad \partial_{i} n^{\underline{l}}=\delta_{i l}, \quad \int \varphi d \psi \rightarrow \boldsymbol{S} \phi ; n \breve{\partial}_{\psi} \end{aligned}
$$

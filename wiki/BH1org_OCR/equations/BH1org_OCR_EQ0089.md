# Equation BH1org_OCR_EQ0089

$$
\begin{aligned} & \partial_{n} \varphi=\sum_{i=1}^{L} \partial_{n_{i}} \varphi \partial_{n} \eta_{i}=\sum_{i=1}^{L} n_{i} \partial_{n_{i}} \varphi\left(\eta_{i}\right)_{1}^{L} \text { wegen } \\ & \partial_{n} \eta_{i}=n n_{i}-(n-1) n_{i}=n_{i} \cdot \text {. Andererseits ist aber } \\ & \partial_{n} \varphi=\left(n^{h}-(n-1)^{h}\right) \varphi\left(n_{i}\right)_{1}^{L}, \text { was wegen der binomischen Entwicklung } \\ & (n-1)^{h}=(-1)^{h} \sum_{v=0}^{h}(-1)^{v}\binom{h}{v} n^{v} \text { also } n^{h}-(n-1)^{h}= \\ & \sum_{v=0}^{h-1}(-1)^{h+v+1}\binom{h}{v}^{v} \text { zu } \partial_{n} \varphi=(-1)^{h+1} \sum_{v=0}^{h-1}(-1)^{v}\binom{h}{v} n^{v} \varphi\left(n_{i}\right)_{1}^{L} \end{aligned}
$$

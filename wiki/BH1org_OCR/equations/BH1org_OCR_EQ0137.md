# Equation M26.

$$
\begin{array}{ll} {\left[\begin{array}{c} \widehat{c d} \\ -a b \end{array}\right] \neq\left[\begin{array}{c} \widehat{c d} \\ +a \bar{b} \end{array}\right], \quad 2 \bar{c} \neq 2 \bar{d}, \quad\left[\begin{array}{c} \widehat{c d} \\ -a b \end{array}\right] \neq\left[\begin{array}{c} \widehat{c d} \\ -a b \end{array}\right]^{x}, \quad 2 \bar{\gamma}_{(a b)} \neq 2 \bar{\gamma}_{(a b)}^{x},} \\ {\left[\begin{array}{c} \widehat{c d} \\ -a b \end{array}\right]=\left[\begin{array}{c} \widehat{c d} \\ -a b \\ - \end{array}\right]_{+}+\left[\begin{array}{c} \widehat{c d} \\ -a \end{array}\right]_{-}, \quad\left[\begin{array}{c} \widehat{c d} \\ -a \end{array}\right]_{ \pm}= \pm\left[\begin{array}{c} \widehat{c d} \\ -a \end{array}\right]_{ \pm}^{x}} \end{array}
$$

# Equation M4.

$$
\begin{aligned} & \underset{n_{1}}{\stackrel{v}{\boldsymbol{S}}+\underset{v+1}{n_{2}}=\underset{n_{1}}{n_{2}}} \quad \underset{n_{1}+1}{\underline{\boldsymbol{S}}_{1}}=0, \quad \underset{n_{1}}{\boldsymbol{S} \varphi} \underset{n_{2}}{n_{1}}=\varphi\left(n_{1}\right), \\ & n_{2} \\ & \underset{n_{1}}{\boldsymbol{S}}+\underset{n_{2}}{\boldsymbol{S}}=\underset{n_{1}}{\boldsymbol{S}}+\underset{n_{2}}{\boldsymbol{S}} \end{aligned}
$$

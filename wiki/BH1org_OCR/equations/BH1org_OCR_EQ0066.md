# Equation M1.

$$
\begin{aligned} & y=f(x), \quad \lim _{\Delta F_{v} \rightarrow \tau} \sum_{v=1}^{n} \tilde{y}_{v} \Delta x_{v}=n \tau, \quad \Delta F_{v}=\tilde{y}_{v} \Delta x_{v}=\int_{x_{v-1}}^{x_{v}} y d x \\ & \sum_{v=1}^{n} \int_{v-1}^{v}=\int_{0}^{n}, \quad \int_{x_{0}}^{x_{n}} f(x) d x=n \tau, \quad x_{n}=x(n), \\ & y_{n}=f(n) \end{aligned}
$$

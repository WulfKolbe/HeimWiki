# Equation BH1org_OCR_EQ0172

$$
\begin{aligned} & (a(k, l)-1) \underline{\partial}_{l}-\sum_{m \neq l}^{q} \underline{\partial}_{m}=\bar{a}_{k l} \sum_{s=1}^{q} \bar{e}_{s} \breve{\partial}_{s}=\bar{a}_{k l} \mathrm{GRAD}_{q}, \text { also } \\ & \bar{a}_{k l} \mathrm{GRAD}_{q} \varphi_{k l}=\lambda(k, l) \varphi_{k l}-\varphi_{k l}^{2}=\frac{1}{4} \lambda^{2}(k, l) \cdot\left(1-\left(\frac{2 \varphi_{k l}}{\lambda(k, l)}-1\right)^{2}\right), \end{aligned}
$$

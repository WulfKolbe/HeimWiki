# Equation M9a

$$
\begin{aligned} & =\left(\varphi-\varphi\left(\ldots, \psi_{j}-\partial \psi_{j}, \ldots\right)\right)\left(\partial \psi_{j}\right)^{-1} \text {, also zusammengefaßt in } \\ & \varphi=\varphi\left(\psi_{1}, \ldots, \psi_{\lambda}\right), \quad \psi_{j}=\psi_{j}\left(n_{1} \ldots n_{L}\right), \quad \partial \varphi=\sum_{j=1}^{\lambda} \partial_{\psi j} \varphi \partial \psi_{j}, \\ & \partial_{\psi j} \varphi=\left(\varphi-\varphi\left(\ldots, \psi_{j}-\partial \psi_{j}, \ldots\right)\right)\left(\partial \psi_{j}\right)^{-1} \end{aligned}
$$

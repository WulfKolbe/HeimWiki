# Structural units

**German:** *Struktureinheiten*

These are the [synmetronic](../concepts/Synmetronics.md) tensorial lattice nuclei, whose iterations form the elements of the [synmetronic](../concepts/Synmetronics.md) correlator. [(vol. 2, p. 80)](../sources/bh2.md)

# Superposition conjunctive

**German:** *Superpositionskonjunktiv*

If a [correspondence](../concepts/Correspondence.md) occurs through the superposition of screen fields, then the superposition conjunctive is present as the conjunctive of this screen field correspondence.[(vol. 2, p. 233)](../sources/bh2.md)

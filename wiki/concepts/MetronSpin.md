# Metron spin

**German:** *Metronspin*

A vector determined by the direction of rotation of a two-dimensional [metron](../concepts/Metron.md) (normal to the metron surface).

<!--okf:source lang="de"-->
> **Original (German)**
>
> Vektor, vom Umlaufsinn eines zweidimensionalen → Me trons bestimmt (normal zur Metronfläche).

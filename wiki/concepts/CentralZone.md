# Central Zone

**German:** *Zentralzone*

The central [configuration zone](../concepts/ConfigurationZone.md) of highest density and cubically increasing occupation with [protosimplexes](../concepts/Protosimplex.md) within a term of complex Hermetry. [(vol. 2, p. 261)](../sources/bh2.md)

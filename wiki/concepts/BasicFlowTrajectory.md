# Basic flow trajectory

**German:** *Grundflußverlauf*

Temporal progression of [synmetronic](../concepts/Synmetronics.md) condensation stages, which, as dynamic [prototropes](../concepts/Prototropes.md), form the [flukton](../concepts/Flukton.md). [(vol. 2, p. 186)](../sources/bh2.md)

# Baryon neutrino

**German:** *Baryneutrino*

Provisional designation for the electrically neutral complement to the electron, which, however, has only a formal existence. [(vol. 2, p. 13)](../sources/bh2.md)

<!--okf:source lang="de"-->
> **Original (German)**

# Prototrope combinate

**German:** *Prototropenkombinat*

A correlative combination of [prototropes](../concepts/Prototropes.md) corresponding to the [coupling structure](../concepts/CouplingStructure.md), which, as maxima of the sources of [correspondence](../concepts/Correspondence.md) fields, structure the compositional condensation stage. [(vol. 2, p. 191)](../sources/bh2.md)

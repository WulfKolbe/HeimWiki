# Chronon

**German:** *Chronon*

The indivisible element of time resulting from the existence of the [world metron](../concepts/WorldMetron.md). [(vol. 2, p. 27)](../sources/bh2.md)

<!--okf:source lang="de"-->
> **Original (German)**

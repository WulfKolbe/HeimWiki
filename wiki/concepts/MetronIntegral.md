# Metron integral

**German:** *Metronintegral*

A summation operation of [metron differentials](../concepts/MetronDifferential.md) that is analogous to infinitesimal integration.

<!--okf:source lang="de"-->
> **Original (German)**
>
> Summationsoperation von → Metrondifferentialen, die der infinitesimalen Integration analog ist.

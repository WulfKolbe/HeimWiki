# Structural condenser

**German:** *Strukturkondensor*

The [functional selector](../concepts/FunctionalSelector.md) that generates the [structural compressor](../concepts/StructuralCompressor.md) and acts on a [fundamental condenser](../concepts/FundamentalCondenser.md).

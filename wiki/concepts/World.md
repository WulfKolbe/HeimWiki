# World

**German:** *Welt*

The totality of all points in a six-dimensional space, of which spacetime is a four-dimensional subspace. In addition to the three real, commutative coordinates of the physical space (universe)—which is compact with respect to the rotation group—the world is spanned by three further non-commutative imaginary coordinates, such that its signature is (+ + + - - -).

# Eschatological Sphere Trinity

**German:** *Eschatologische Sphärentrinität*

The trinity of monometronic spheres (spatial) that delimits the eon as the temporal interval defining spacetime, as the final event of spacetime. [(vol. 2, p. 62)](../sources/bh2.md)

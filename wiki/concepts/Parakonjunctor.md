# Parakonjunctor

**German:** *Parakonjunktor*

A [conjunctor](../concepts/Conjunctor.md) that connects fluctones with fluctone spins in para-alignment. [(vol. 2, p. 202)](../sources/bh2.md)

<!--okf:source lang="de"-->
> **Original (German)**
>
> Ein → Konjunktor, der → Fluktonen mit → Flukton spins in Paraeinstellung verbindet. $(2,202)$

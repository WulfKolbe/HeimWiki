# Protosimplex transfer

**German:** *Protosimplextransfer*

The shift of [protosimplexes](../concepts/Protosimplex.md) from the [mesozone](../concepts/Mesozone.md) into the [external zone](../concepts/ExternalZone.md) as a result of linearity. [(vol. 2, p. 341)](../sources/bh2.md)

> **Original (German)**

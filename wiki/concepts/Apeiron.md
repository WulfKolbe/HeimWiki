# Apeiron

**German:** *Apeiron*

Possible region of the [world](../concepts/World.md) outside the definition interval of the subspace of physical spacetime. [(vol. 2, p. 67)](../sources/bh2.md) Architectural unit, semantic: Subspaces of the [world](../concepts/World.md) that differ in meaning and are not interchangeable.

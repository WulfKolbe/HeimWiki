# World Flukton

**German:** *Weltflukton*

A [flukton](../concepts/Flukton.md) whose basic flow paths extend into all [structural units](../concepts/StructuralUnits.md) of the [world](../concepts/World.md) and thus characterize the eneametry of [spacetime condensations](../concepts/SpacetimeCondensation.md). [(vol. 2, p. 189)](../sources/bh2.md)

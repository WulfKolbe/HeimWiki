# Protosimplex generator

**German:** *Protosimplexgenerator*

A quantity formed multiplicatively from the [structural power](../concepts/StructuralPower.md), the [base rise](../concepts/BaseRise.md), and an excitation function (dependent on the [resonance orders](../concepts/ResonanceOrder.md)), through which the occupancy of the configuration zones with [protosimplexes](../concepts/Protosimplex.md) can be determined. [(vol. 2, p. 324)](../sources/bh2.md)

# Lattice selector

**German:** *Gitterselektor*

A [selector](../concepts/Selector.md) that, in the Euclidean or pseudo-Euclidean case, describes the finite division of the straight-line, equidistant lattice determined by the [metron](../concepts/Metron.md).

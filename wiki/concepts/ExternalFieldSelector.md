# External-field selector

**German:** *Fremdfeldselektor*

The [fundamental condenser](../concepts/FundamentalCondenser.md) supplemented by the [correlation](../concepts/Correlation.md) tensor, in which the external field is then expressed by the [correlation](../concepts/Correlation.md) tensor. [(vol. 2, p. 108)](../sources/bh2.md)

<!--okf:source lang="de"-->
> **Original (German)**
>
> Der durch den → Korrelationstensor ergänzte → Fundamentalkondensor, in welchem dann das Fremdfeld durch den → Korrelationstensor ausgedrückt wird. $(2,108)$

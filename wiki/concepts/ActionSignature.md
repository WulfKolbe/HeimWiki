# Action signature

**German:** *Wirkungssignatur*

This is a signature that indicates which lattice kernel in the contravariant [fundamental selector](../concepts/FundamentalSelector.md) causes the mixed-variant [binary field](../concepts/BinaryField.md) of the relevant [fundamental condenser](../concepts/FundamentalCondenser.md).

# Conjunctor

**German:** *Konjunktor*

The integral [selector](../concepts/Selector.md) that explicitly describes the [correlation](../concepts/Correlation.md) of [protosimplexes](../concepts/Protosimplex.md) caused by a conjunctive. [(vol. 2, p. 202)](../sources/bh2.md) Conjunctor, prototropic: The conjunctor of only one flucton. [(vol. 2, p. 196)](../sources/bh2.md)

# Composite selector

**German:** *Verbundselektor*

A tensorial selector that specifies the correlation between a contravariant and a covariant acting element of the [correlator](../concepts/Correlator.md) in the [binary field](../concepts/BinaryField.md) of a [fundamental condenser](../concepts/FundamentalCondenser.md), when a [polymetry](../concepts/Polymetry.md) is present and the law of variance step change no longer applies. The composite selector thereby constructs the [coupling tensor](../concepts/CouplingTensor.md).

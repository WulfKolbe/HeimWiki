# Aeonic dimension

**German:** *Äonische Dimension*

A hidden, imaginary dimension of the world from which the temporal direction of actualization during the [aeon](../concepts/Aeon.md) (the definitional interval of physical time) is controlled. [(vol. 2, p. 21)](../sources/bh2.md)

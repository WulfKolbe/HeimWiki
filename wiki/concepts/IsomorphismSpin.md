# Isomorphism spin

**German:** *Isomorphiespin*

A spin component of a [world structure](../concepts/WorldStructure.md) as a Hermitian form in the imaginary subspace of the [world](../concepts/World.md), which is identical to the empirical isospin. [(vol. 2, p. 213)](../sources/bh2.md)

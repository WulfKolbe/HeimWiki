# Condenser flux

**German:** *Kondensorfluß*

The temporal flux of the structural state of a [fundamental condenser](../concepts/FundamentalCondenser.md), which occurs cyclically in the form of basic fluxes of a fluctonic nature between the [correlation](../concepts/Correlation.md) and [correspondence](../concepts/Correspondence.md) maxima. [(vol. 2, p. 156)](../sources/bh2.md)

<!--okf:source lang="de"-->
> **Original (German)**

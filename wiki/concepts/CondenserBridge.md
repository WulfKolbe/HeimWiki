# Condenser bridge

**German:** *Kondensorbrücke*

Partial [coupling group](../concepts/CouplingGroup.md) consisting of hermetic and corresponding antihermetic [fundamental condensers](../concepts/FundamentalCondenser.md). [(vol. 2, p. 122)](../sources/bh2.md)

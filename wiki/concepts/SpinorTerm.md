# Spinor term

**German:** *Spinorterm*

Terms of complex hermitian fields with half-integer quantum numbers of the [space spin](../concepts/SpaceSpin.md) (fermions). [(vol. 2, p. 216)](../sources/bh2.md) Spin selector: [Field selector](../concepts/FieldSelector.md) of the [metron spin](../concepts/MetronSpin.md).

<!--okf:source lang="de"-->
> **Original (German)**
>
> Terme → komplexer Hermetrie mit halbzahliger Quanten zahl des → Raumspins (Fermionen). $(2,216)$ Spinselektor: → Feldselektor des → Metronspins.

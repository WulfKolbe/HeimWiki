# Entelechial dimension

**German:** *Entelechale Dimension*

A hidden world dimension orthogonal to physical spacetime, which, like the eonic dimension, is imaginary. [(vol. 2, p. 21)](../sources/bh2.md) Event, latent: A [world point](../concepts/WorldPoint.md) outside the spacetime subspace of the [world](../concepts/World.md). Event, manifest: A [world point](../concepts/WorldPoint.md) within the subspace of spacetime.

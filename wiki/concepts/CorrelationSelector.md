# Correlation selector

**German:** *Korrelationsselektor*

A scalar selector that describes the [correlation](../concepts/Correlation.md) between two elements of the [synmetronic](../concepts/Synmetronics.md) correlator. [(vol. 2, p. 119)](../sources/bh2.md)

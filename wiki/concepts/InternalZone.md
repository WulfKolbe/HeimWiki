# Internal Zone

**German:** *Internzone*

The [configuration zone](../concepts/ConfigurationZone.md) of a term of complex Hermitian geometry whose [protosimplex](../concepts/Protosimplex.md) occupation increases quadratically and which lies between the [central zone](../concepts/CentralZone.md) and the [mesozone](../concepts/Mesozone.md). [(vol. 2, p. 261)](../sources/bh2.md)

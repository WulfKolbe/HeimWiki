# Conjunctor isomerism

**German:** *Konjunktorisomerie*

The isomers of a coupling structure that are possible due to the various arrangements of [prototrope](../concepts/Prototropes.md) conjunctors within that structure. [(vol. 2, p. 198)](../sources/bh2.md)

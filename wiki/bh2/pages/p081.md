The development of this somewhat speculative-seeming relationship ultimately boiled down to using (39) in conjunction with (32) to analyze the change $\dot{m}_{L}<0$ in relation to the cosmic age, and from this to infer the final state $\Lambda$ of the expansive $R_{4}$hemisphere in the form $m(\Lambda)<m_{L}$,  such that $\Lambda$ can be expressed in terms of some earlier $R_{3}$state $D<\Lambda$ and $\tau>\tau(\Lambda)$. Here, $2 R_{+}$ was interpreted from (14) as the $R_{3}$ maximum diameter $\Lambda$.

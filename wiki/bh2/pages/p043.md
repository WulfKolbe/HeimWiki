If $D(t)$ holds, then one can attempt to find the ratio of empirical elementary constants that varies in the highest order with $D$. If this order is $n$ and $\eta$ is the corresponding ratio, then $\eta \sim D^{n}$ must hold. If it is still possible to apply suitable constant factors $A$ to $\eta$ such that $\eta A=y \sim D^{n}$ becomes a ratio of physically measurable quantities, then, after taking the logarithm, $\ln y=n \ln D+$ is constant, and after differentiation with respect to time, $\dot{y} / y=n \dot{D} / D$. Thus, if $\dot{y} \neq 0$ can be verified, then $\dot{D} \neq 0$ must also remain constant; that is, a possible measurement of $\dot{y}$ would show whether $D$ of $R_{3}$ changes or not in the course of  cosmic motion.

Whether the world structure is such that a world polymetry in the sense of III. exists is obviously a question of general cosmology. Even if the existence of such a world polymetry cannot yet be directly proven, it nevertheless seems sensible to first determine the lower bound of the partial spectrum of complex condensation levels—that is, the minimal complex condensation. If this is to be done, moreover, with a view to a general cosmological analysis, then the general cosmological background phenomenon of gravity must be discussed once again.

The expansion $\dot{D}>0$ of $R_{3}$ would have to manifest itself in a general receding motion of the $R_{3}$ structures, which depends on the $R_{3}$ distance $s$ between the object and the observation site, and must manifest itself as a redshift of the spectral lines of emitted photons with wavelength $\lambda$  as the Doppler effect $\lambda z=\delta \lambda$. In fact, such a redshift in the spectra of extragalactic spiral nebulae is observed as the Hubble shift, which, with $B=$ const within the observational range of the empirical linear distance dependence $z \approx B s$ and is dispersion-free, but exhibits certain empirical anomalies.  If this general redshift of all spectral lines is interpreted as the Doppler effect $z=v / c$ of the recession velocity $v$, then  the relationship $v \approx H s$ would follow from the Hubble constant $H=c B$. If this speculative interpretation is accepted,  then the cosmological problem (41) could be solved using $A=H$,  because $H$ is spectroscopically measurable.

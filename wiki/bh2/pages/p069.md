It follows that the imaginary solutions of equation (47) must be excluded from the analysis, while the graphically determined zeros $\eta_{1}, \eta_{2}$ and $\eta_{3}$ are relevant as real solutions of (47). 

From the numerically determinable magnitudes of these three real solutions, $D_{0}$ can be determined. It turns out that for each of the $1 \leqq j \leqq 3$ real solutions $\eta_{j}$, a different $D_{0}$value $d_{j}$ exists, specifically in the sequence $D_{2}>D_{1}>D_{3}$. The origin event of the $R_{4}$ with $t=0$ and $R_{3}\left(D_{0}\right)$ is thus characterized by three concentric monometric spheres, whereby the largest of these spheres, $D_{2}=D_{p}$ encloses $D_{2}=D_{p}$ as the protosphere of this proto-universe, such that this protosphere $D_{p}$, upon its actualization, triggers the initiation of cosmic motion. The monometronic sphere $D_{3}$ with the smallest diameter evidently plays a fundamental but unknown role in the actualization of the $R_{3}$ , so that this monometronic sphere $D_{3}$ should be designated as the fundamental sphere $D_{f}=D_{3}$. Accordingly, the solution $D_{1}$ lying between these concentric spheres is designated the mesosphere  $D_{1}=D_{m}$.

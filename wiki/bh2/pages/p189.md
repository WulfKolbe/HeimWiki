From metronic  extreme value theory, the extreme value condition $\partial[3]=\widehat{0}$ or $0=\partial e^{\varphi}=e^{\varphi}\left(\underline{\lambda}_{k l}-a \alpha\right) \partial \mu$ follows for the curve in $R_{3}$, which can only be satisfied by $\alpha=\frac{\lambda_{k l}}{a}=A$ due to $e^{\varphi}>0$ and $\delta \mu>0$. Using the transformation $u=\frac{w-b}{\sqrt{E-b^{2}}}$, this condition becomes $\frac{u(u+C)}{E-u^{2}}=A$.

Special thanks are due to Müller and, above all, Dipl. Ing. Walter Dröscher for their valuable suggestions and painstaking proofreading. Last but not least, I owe my thanks to the author for his trust and to Mrs. Gerda Heim for her invaluable efforts in compiling the manuscript of her husband, who was virtually blind. 

I therefore hope that, due to its uniqueness, this volume will attract the well-deserved interest of experts in the field and help broaden our understanding of the world. 

Innsbruck, September 17, 1984  Andreas Resch

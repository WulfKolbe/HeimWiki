Protosimplex equivalents for the energy measure $4 \mu \alpha_{+} c^{2}$ must be found, and $\beta_{j}$ must also be an integer. If $G_{j}$ is increased by just one unit at $\beta_{j}=1$ by just one unit, a restructuring occurs because the density of the zone $j-1$ is higher than that of the zone $j$ and this higher density of a configuration in $j-1$ has a higher probability than the lower density of the configuration in $j$. Thus, in $\beta_{j}=0$, $j$ collapses with $G_{j}=0$, while in $j-1$ the digit $n_{j-1} \rightarrow 1+n_{j-1}$ increases by 1. The zones $j \geqq 2$ can thus, when excited via $f$, reach the population maxima $\beta_{j}=1$ due to the band widths $\beta_{j}>0$ occupancy maxima $\beta_{j}=1$ and then collapse at $\beta_{j}=0$,  whereby the lower-lying zone $j-1$ is increased by 1 in its occupation number. In this dynamic process, only the occupation  of the central zone $j=1$ can rise continuously; however, this rise is not  unlimited, because $n_{1} \rightarrow \infty$ would also result in divergence $G_{1} \rightarrow \infty$ as well as  $M(N) \rightarrow \infty$ with $N \rightarrow \infty$. However, the masses $M \rightarrow \infty$ represent infinite self-energy potentials, which, for physical reasons, must be ruled out as non-existent. For this reason, there must be an upper bound $\left(n_{1}\right)_{\text {max }}=L_{1}<\infty$ for the central zone; however, according to (107), this also implies upper bounds for the zone occupancies $\left(n_{j}\right)_{\text {max }}=L_{j}<\infty$ with $j \geqq 2$ as well as an upper bound for the excitation $N_{\max }=L_{N}<\infty$ in the $P_{4}$lattice, so that the lines of the excitation spectra are always bounded and all $M(N)$ of the $c$ and $d$ terms remain finite.

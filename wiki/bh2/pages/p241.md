# 5. Correspondence Fields and the Prototropic Structure  of the Universe.

 

The compositional condenser terms $\bar{\lambda}(x)$ of a hermetic form $x$  are, according to IV., obviously identical to the empirical elementary matter field quanta $M q$. According to VI. and VII., 1. through 3., these  $M q$ are determined synmetrically by dynamic properties and are  therefore in any case capable of external interactions in the sense of correspondences. 

In summary, it can be stated that elementary cyclic condenser flows $N^{ \pm}() N^{\mp}$ pass through the internal pseudo-Euclidean correlation maxima $N^{-}$(condenser minima) through internal conjunctives  $-(\mu)-$ (relative to the lattice core region ${ }^{2} \bar{\kappa}_{(\mu)}$ with $\mu \leqq 3$ ) integrally correlate the coupling structure determining $\bar{\lambda}$, such that this coupling structure, during a temporal stability interval $t_{2}-t_{1}>0$, in the undisturbed case is represented by a cyclic integral condenser flux, which during this stability interval is determined as a steady state by a dynamic equilibrium. According to Section VII, 3, this coupling structure is superimposed by a general integral straton spin, which arises from the correlation of all cyclic condenser fluxes. 

According to this picture, there must therefore be two different classes of possible  correspondences, namely the spatial spin correspondences $R k$  and the structural correspondences $S k$.

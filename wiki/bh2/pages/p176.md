# 1. The enantiomeric flow aggregates of the coupling structures. ${ }^{(1)}$

 

If the cyclic flux aggregates—which apparently exist in the terms of (27) and are correlated with one another—are to be analyzed as a consequence of metronic  field activations in order to arrive at a general  theory of the internal structure of these terms (which must yield the separation of complex terms from (27) as a result of this correlation theory of cyclic condenser fluxes), then a preliminary compilation of all cyclic flux aggregates that are at all possible in the coupling structures becomes indispensable. First of all, it follows directly that the flux classes $v=1$ and $v=2$ can occur only in the case of doubly or singly degenerate coupling structures (with respect to the condenser in question). For $v=1$, only a doubly degenerate coupling structure can be possible. With $a \equiv \mathrm{I} h, b \equiv \mathrm{II} h$, as well as $\alpha \equiv \mathrm{I} a$ and $\beta \equiv \mathrm{I} a$, it becomes apparent that the $\binom{6}{1}$ possibilities $v=1$ are always characterized by $\omega=1$,  but are reduced to only four actual $F_{1}$. Consequently, $(j) 1$ remains for $1 \leqq j \leqq 4$. The $\binom{6}{2}=15$ possibilities $v=2$ require a simple degeneracy of the coupling structure.

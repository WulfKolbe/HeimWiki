A system of signature-isomeric fundamental flows forms a flucton, and  from this it immediately becomes evident that, for each flucton $(-k)$ in a Un space of the $R_{6}$, there are always two fundamental flows for $1 \leqq k \leqq 5$ that have degenerated into shield fields, so that for $k \leqq 5$ the $(-k)$ always occur in conjunction with the shield fields ( $+k$ ) in the respective subspaces, because the fundamental flows must be understood dynamically as flows of metric structure, and shield fields are static in nature due to the identity of basis and contrasignature. These $(+k)$ apparently enclose the $(-k)$ as synmetronic condensation stages, so that the prototropes $k \leqq 5$ already constitute the simplest structural systems ( $\pm k$ ). Such a simplest synmetronic system $( \pm k)$ will be referred to hereinafter as a protosimplex. There can therefore be only five types of these protosimplexes; for there is no shield field associated with the world flukton $(-6)$ and no flukton associated with $(+7)$. The coupling structures from Section VI, 4. for the possible forms of hermetry $a$ through $d$ provide schemas of coupling groups that define condenser bridges as well as distributions of condenser sources and condenser sinks.

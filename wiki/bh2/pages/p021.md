The metric of these cells is determined by the requirement of integers and the spherical nature of the symmetric—that is, undisturbed—condensation. Because of this possible interpretation of $n$ as metron numbers, the entire metronic hyperstructure of the condensation can be related to a simple Euclidean tensor, because the spectral function depends only on a running metron number—which is, however, the characteristic feature of simple metronic tensors.

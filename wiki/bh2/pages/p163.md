Only the condensations $a$ can be transformed further using a geodetic system with respect to (11). With respect to such a system $C_{(11)}$, all physical effects that originate from ${ }^{2} \bar{\gamma}_{(11)}$ no longer exist. Since these are always gravitational processes, the gravitational effect (gravity) appears as an apparent effect dependent on the reference frame, which is why the geodesic motion of free fall proceeds without any forces, even though the source of the gravitational field remains unchanged. These gravitational field sources can, in principle, only be condensations of the classes $b, c$ or $d$.

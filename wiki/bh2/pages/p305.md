Using this relationship, it is now possible to investigate how the $1 \leqq j \leqq 4$ configuration zones structure the quasi-corpuscular $b_{\lambda}$ as subconstituents. According to (98d), $G_{j}\left(Q_{j}\right)=$  = const $(t)$ always holds for $n_{j}=0$ of the temporally constant framework structure. In general, in this case, $4 G_{1}=Q_{1}^{2}\left(1+Q_{1}\right)^{2}$ holds, as well as $6 G_{2}=$  $=Q_{2}\left(2 Q_{2}^{2}+3 Q_{2}+1\right)$, as well as $2 G_{3}=Q_{3}\left(1+Q_{3}\right)$ and $G_{4}=Q_{4}$ for the $j \leqq 4$ protosimplex fillings of the configuration zones with  $n_{j}=0$. However, according to (98b), for the $Q_{j}$ in the case $k=1$, numerically  $Q_{1}=Q_{2}=3$ as well as $Q_{3}=2$ and $Q_{4}=1$ in the case $k=1$, that is, $G_{1}=36, G_{2}=14, G_{3}=3$ and $G_{4}=1$. On the other hand, there are quasicorpuscular regions $G=k+1=2$ for these mesons. Condition (103) is apparently satisfied by $G_{1}$ of the central zone, but not by $G_{2}$ through $G_{4}$ of the internal, meso, and external zones, whereas $G_{2}+G_{3}+G_{4}=18$ does. If we denote $Z(n), Z(m), Z(p)$ and $Z(\sigma)$ as the occupation numbers of the four configuration zones, then $G=2$ mesonic regions would be composed, due to $k=1$, in the form $b_{1}=Z(n)$ and $b_{2}=Z(m)+Z(p)+Z(\sigma)$. For the baryonic region $k=2$, according to (98b), the corresponding numerical values are $Q_{1}=24, Q_{2}=31, Q_{3}=34$ and $Q_{4}=15$. However, this means that the configurations $G_{1}, G_{2}$ and $G_{3}$ all satisfy (103) for $n_{j}=0$, but $G_{4}$ does not.

If we accept (52), then $\Lambda$ can be numerically determined using the present values $\tau(T)$  according to (15) and $D_{T} \approx 6,03 \cdot 10^{125}[\mathrm{~m}]$. This yields $\Lambda \approx 1,562 \cdot 10^{126}[\mathrm{~m}]$, i.e., $\Lambda \approx 2,6 D_{T}$. 

Similarly, using (51a), the current age of the universe $T$ can be reduced to  $D_{T}$, whose approximation followed from (37) according to (37b). If we set the cosmogonic protosphere $D_{1}=D_{p}$ for $t_{1}=0$ and $t=T$ with $D_{T} \gg D_{P}$ in (51a), then $2 \beta C T=D_{T}^{\beta}-D_{p}^{\beta} \approx D_{T}^{\beta}$ follows as a very good approximation. From the chronon $\vartheta$ in (35a) and the geometrization (36) with (39), we obtain $C \approx 44,64 \cdot 10^{64}\left[m^{\beta} S^{-1}\right]$, whereas $11 \beta=16$ follows from (51a) and (51).  Thus, $T$ can evolve from $D_{T}$ to $T \approx 1,72 \cdot 10^{115}[s]$ and $T_{A}$ until the final state $\Lambda$ of the expanding hemisphere is reached at $T_{A} \approx 2,754 \cdot 10^{117}[s]$, i.e., at $T_{A} \approx 160 T$. This means that, with respect to $T_{A}$, the cosmic motion in the current cosmic epoch $T$ has so far spent only about 0.6% of its time structure in the expansive $R_{4}$—whereas the domain $\Lambda \mathrm{im}$ of the maximum expansion state of $R_{3}$ has only been realized as approximately $38 \%$ more expansive as a  result of cosmic motion in the sense of $D_{T}$.  For the eon as the temporal definition interval of the $R_{4}$ subspace of the  world, the numerical result is thus $\Theta=2 T_{A} \approx 320 T$. 

Given the magnitude of the current age of the world  $T \approx 1,72 \cdot 10^{115}[s]$ it can be noted that the cosmogony of matter could not possibly have begun with the key event $t=0$ of $R_{4}$. If, nevertheless, one assumes that the beginning of this cosmogony of matter coincides with $t=0$, then one must also assume  that during the entire interval $0 \leqq t \leqq T$ stellar systems (composed of condensed matter) synthesized higher-order nuclides from $H$ as a result of infrastellar thermonuclear combustion processes. However, this would mean that, in the current state of $R_{3}$, due to the order of magnitude of $T$, there should be no hydrogen left at all. This, however, contradicts all empirical evidence, so the assumption that the cosmogony of matter began with $t=0$ must be incorrect.

If the kernel ${ }^{2} \bar{\kappa}_{(\mu)}$ is combined with $\mu \leqq 3$, then the superposition conjunction could symbolize the ser component of the screen field correspondence by $(+p)_{X}-[\hat{\mu}]-(+q)_{Y}$ , although $\hat{\mu}$ is not represented as a matrix in connection with the conjunction symbol. This superposition conjunction of the screen field correspondence in $\mu$ now acts (if the mentioned boundary conditions  are satisfied) both in $X$ and in $Y$ as a disturbance of the stationary  correlation state $( \pm p)-(\mu)-( \pm q)$ in such a way that the cyclicity  of the condenser flux in $\mu$ is canceled out. This would result in the decay of the corresponding $M q$; however, this does not occur if the correlation structure of the perturbing term is modified in the same way, and the from $X$ and $Y$ satisfy an adaptation condition in $\mu$. In this case, a structurally oriented correspondence conjunction $X \rightarrow Y$ form as a result of the preceding screen field correspondence, which is to be symbolized by $( \pm p)_{X}-(\mu\rangle-( \pm q)_{Y}$. This symbol  means that, as a result of the disturbance and the satisfied boundary conditions,  a capacitor flux in the direction of $X \rightarrow Y$ is initiated in the region $\mu$.  The $S k$ between $X$ and $Y$ is given when, in response to the oriented correspondence conjunction $X \rightarrow Y$ a corresponding  conjunctive $Y \rightarrow X$, symbolized by the inversion  $( \pm p)_{X}-\langle\mu)-( \pm q)_{Y}$, is caused. Finally, superimposing both conjunctives results in the simultaneous conjunctive $( \pm p)_{X}-(\hat{\mu})-( \pm q)_{Y} \hat{=}$  $\hat{=}( \pm p)_{X}-(\mu\rangle-( \pm q)_{Y}+( \pm p)_{X}-\langle\mu)-( \pm q)_{Y}$, then, as a consequence of the $S k$ arising from $X$ and $Y$ a higher-order flow aggregate has emerged,  which, due to $-(\hat{\mu})-$, is in turn cyclic in nature and is in a  new steady state of dynamic equilibrium, characterized by a new temporal stability interval. In  this way, $S k$ generates, from $X$ and $Y$, generates a correspondence system $\Lambda(X, Y)$, whose constituents are the matter field quanta $X$ and $Y$.

According to VI, the $\bar{\lambda}_{(\mu v)}^{(\kappa \lambda)}=\underline{\bar{\lambda}}$ are the synmetronic condenser terms of the corresponding fundamental condensers, which structure the compositional $\bar{\lambda}$. The following always holds: $\underline{\bar{\lambda}} \perp \vec{Y}=\vec{v}+i \vec{w}$, where, through an appropriate choice of coordinates, $\vec{v}=\overrightarrow{0}$—that is, $\vec{Y}=i \vec{w}$—becomes possible. Since in this composite motion $\vec{Y}=i \overrightarrow{\mathcal{W}}$ of $R_{3}$ with $w^{2}=c^{2}+\dot{\varepsilon}^{2}+\dot{\eta}^{2}$, $\dot{\varepsilon}=\dot{\eta}=0$ is always attainable in the case of time-invariant $x_{5}$ and $x_{6}$,  $i \overrightarrow{\mathcal{w}}=\dot{\vec{x}}_{4}$ and thus $\vec{Y} \| \vec{x}_{4}$ or $\underline{\lambda} \perp \vec{x}_{4}$ can be set without limiting the generality. On the other hand, however,  the $\bar{\lambda}$—as symmetronic condensation levels—determine, through their composition  by conjunctive laws, the orientation of the ${ }_{x}^{p}(\mu)_{ \pm}^{q}$, which, according to the preceding discussion, in turn determine the orientation of the total spin, whose $R_{3}$ projection appears as the straton spin $\bar{s}_{x}$. This means, however, that the orientation of $\bar{s}_{x}$ is directly related to the temporal $R_{4}$ structure $\bar{\lambda} \perp \vec{x}_{4}$, and this structure is expressed, according to (5a), in the condition $\cos \alpha_{4} \neq 0$ of the world geometry with respect to $x_{4}$. According to this relation (5a), however, the following holds for the spacetime world $R_{4}^{+}$ and its paraspaces $R_{3}^{+k}$ layered in $x_{5}$ with $k \geqq 0$ and $R_{3}^{+0} \equiv R_{3} \quad$ satisfy the condition $\cos \alpha_{4}>0$. However, since $\cos \alpha_{4} \neq 0$ also allows for $\cos \alpha_{4}<0$ with an antiparallel time coordinate, $R_{6}$ the existence of an anti-spacetime $R_{4}^{-}$ with anti-spaces $R_{3}^{-k}$ must also be assumed, although this cannot be directly proven. However, if this anti-spacetime $R_{4}^{-}$ exists, then—relative to $R_{4}^{+}$  in addition to the flukton and conjunctor spins of the flux aggregates and  in addition to the straton spin $\bar{s}_{x}^{+} \equiv \bar{s}_{x}\left(R_{4}^{+}\right)$, the corresponding enantiomeric structures must exist as antistructures with the antistraton spin  $\bar{s}_{x}^{-} \equiv \bar{s}_{x}\left(R_{4}^{-}\right)$whose spin configurations are oriented in a mirror-symmetric manner  relative to $R_{4}^{+}$ such that they appear as normal structures  relative to $R_{4}^{-}$.

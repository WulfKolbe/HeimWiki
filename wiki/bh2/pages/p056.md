At a distance of $s_{0}^{\prime} \gg 0$ from the gravitational center of the $C^{\prime}$ (here, too, let’s call it $s_{0}^{\prime}<\varrho$ '), there is also a point $S^{\prime}$ from which photons of energy $E=h v$, that is, with wavelength $\lambda v=c$, are emitted. Let the  distance between $\varrho(C)$ and $\varrho^{\prime}\left(C^{\prime}\right)$ be $s \gg 2 \varrho$, whereas $\varrho \approx \varrho^{\prime}$ (at least on the same order of magnitude). The straight-line path of the photon emitted at $S^{\prime}$ to the receiving point $S$ is therefore $\varrho^{\prime}-s_{0}^{\prime}+s+\varrho-s_{0}$.

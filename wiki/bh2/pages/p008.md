Heim’s theory takes into account the trend—which has been growing particularly in recent times—of attributing more physical properties to space itself. Whether physics can ultimately be understood solely through the geometry of a multidimensional space remains to be seen. The approaches to fundamental physical phenomena presented in the two volumes could, however, at least serve as a proposal for further development in physics, in order to move closer to—or even achieve—the long-sought goal of a unified physical worldview.

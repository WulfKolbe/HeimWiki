With this representation, it is now possible (by analogy with (28)) to phenomenologically describe static internal correlation potentials of the protosimplexes that determine the charge field at any distance $r$ from the  metric center of the simplex.  In general, for the phenomenological influence  of static charge field components $x$ and $y$, the static internal set $4 \pi \varepsilon_{0} V_{x y}=e_{x} e_{y} f(r)$ must hold in the physical $R_{3}$. 

On the basis of these phenomenologically expressed synmetronic protosimplex conjunctors, it is now possible, on the one hand, to determine the upper bound of the natural numbers $k>0$, and, on the other hand, to determine the $\alpha_{j}$ and $\operatorname{der} j \leqq 4$ configuration zones. 

If $E(N, q)$ is the energy content of the external effect of a protosimplex,  but $E_{k}(N, q, k)$ is that of the internal effect with some value  $k>0$, then these two energies must relate to each other  like the static internal potentials of charge field components;

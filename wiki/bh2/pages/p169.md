A direct consequence of (74a) is the empirical energy principle and all conservation principles derivable from it. The deeper cause of compressor isostasis, in addition to Equation (74), is the existence of a condenser flow (75), which stems from the coupling structures—that is, from the internal symmetronic correlations of the structural units. Equation (75) thus enables an explicit analysis of these correlations. Before classifying the possible systems of condenser flows, a preliminary investigation of the fundamental properties of all condenser flows caused by field activation is necessary. 

Every system $Q$ consisting of condenser sources and sinks can cause a condenser flow if the existence condition (75) for $N_{+}(\alpha, \beta, \kappa, \lambda) N_{-}$ is satisfied.

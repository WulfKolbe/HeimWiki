In fact, however, these spectral lines also exhibit a redshift, so that this provides empirical support for the postulate of latent acceleration $g>0$ in $r>\varrho$. In contrast, redshifts will occur for stellar infragalactic objects, but not because $z=0$ would apply in principle here, but rather because in (45) the distances $s$ are so small that the appropriate $z$ value  cannot be determined spectroscopically, and because, furthermore, this  redshift can be compensated for by $g<0$ due to the small infragalactic distances. ${ }^{(11,12)}$ If, nevertheless, one assumes the model of $R_{3}$ expansion $v=H s$, then it appears possible to make a statement about the cosmogony of matter; for then the expansion movement $v$ would have to have begun $t_{x}>10^{10}$ years ago in a primeval explosion resulting from a singularity.

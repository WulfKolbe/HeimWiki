If one takes $P=1$ for the empirical leptons interpreted by (2) and (3), then one of these leptons would have to appear as a negatively charged pseudo-singlet (3) and the other together with a complementary neutral component.  If there were a neutral electron $e_{0}$ or a neutral $\mu$ meson $\mu^{0}$,  then one would expect their masses to differ only slightly from those of the charged components.

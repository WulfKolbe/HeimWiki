Using the internal potentials $V_{x y}$, the analysis can be continued, whereby it is essential to first determine the $\alpha_{j}$ with $j \leqq 4$  . The component $M_{6}$ from $M=\sum_{i=1}^{6} M_{i}$ and the $M_{5}$  determining spin function $F_{S}$ are apparently not attributable to the internal protosimplex structure of the configuration zones; rather, they must be an expression of the conjunctor spin structure of this configurative metric framework. In the tentative determination  of $\alpha_{j}$, one can assume that the $\mu_{+} \alpha_{j}=\mu_{j}$  elementary units of mass in the zone $j$ are $n+Q_{n}=m+Q_{m}=$  $=p+Q_{p}=\sigma+Q_{\sigma}=1$, such that $\mu_{5}$ and $\mu_{6}$ represent mass units of the contributions $F_{S}=1$ and $q=1$ in the mass spectrum  $M(c, d)$. In what follows, I describe $V_{\varepsilon}$ the phenomenological correlation potential for $\varepsilon_{ \pm}^{2}$ and $V_{e}$ that for $e_{ \pm}^{2}$, while  the remaining potentials are indexed by $V_{x y}$, where here as well $V_{C}$  stands for $e_{C}^{2}$. In general, it can be stated that $\mu_{j}=\mu_{+} \alpha_{j}$ and $1 \leqq j \leqq 4$—due to the prototropic nature of these masses—can depend only on $q$ and $k$ in $\eta_{q k}$ according to $\mu_{j}\left(\eta_{q k}\right)$, but  in no case on the time-varying configurations $n_{j}(t)=n_{j}$  of the zones, because in the event of such a dependence, the $\mu_{j}$ themselves would become  time functions. However, due to the unitary nature of the prototropes $\mu_{j}$ as inertial contributions, this is impossible. Since $\mu_{+}$ appears as a calibration factor (specifically, in the sense of a derived natural constant), the $\alpha_{j}=\alpha_{j}\left(\eta_{q k}\right)$ must be for $j \neq 4$. Only in the case of $j=4$ is there the sole possibility of $\alpha_{4}=1$, because in the case of $j=4$, the “massive” internal structure transitions into the external point-like configuration, which couples the interaction potential of possible correspondences into the external $R_{3}$.

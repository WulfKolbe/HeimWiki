This fact arises from the fact that, in the case of only one partial structure, a metronic hyperstructure with the hyperselector $\bar{\psi}_{(v)}=\boldsymbol{S}^{2} \bar{\kappa}_{(v)}() \partial \bar{x}$ also exists $\mathrm{mu} \beta$. Only when at least two activated protostructures  correlate can a Hermitian  composition field with discrete structural levels form.

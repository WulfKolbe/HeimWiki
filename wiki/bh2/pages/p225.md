$+\frac{a}{2}(a-1)$ yields this result when the methods from Section III, 1 are applied. In the Metron integral, $a=1$ holds because $1 \leqq \nu \leqq I$ applies to the definition interval of the multiplet components. On the other hand, however, since $a=1$ holds due to $-s \leqq s_{v} \leqq+s$, $\underset{a=1}{I} s_{v} \partial v=\sum_{v=1}^{I} s_{v}=0$ also holds,  which, when substituted with $a=1$, yields $0=(s+1) I-(I+1) I / 2=$  $=I s-(I-1) I / 2$, thus yielding $I=2 s+1=P+1$ with $2 s=P$ for the number of spin-isomorphically transformable $\bar{\lambda}$ terms of such a multiplet.

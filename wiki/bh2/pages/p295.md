For the following  investigations, it is convenient to rewrite the interval $1 \leqq y \leqq P+1$ of the  multiplet components in $0 \leqq x \leqq P$ so that $q_{x}$ symbolizes the  charge quantum number with sign of the multiplet component $x$. The distribution of the $d$ and $c$ hermitian matrices in a given multiplet of spin isomorphisms can be interpreted as a $R_{3}$-projection  of $( \pm(3,5))$ and $(-6)$, such that each spin isomorphism is assigned such a degenerate mapping. In the $R_{3}$  , the component $x$ of the multiplet must then appear as a property of the  map $q_{x}= \pm\left|q_{x}\right|$ with $\left|q_{x}\right| \geqq 0$. This property of the $d$- or $c$-structure of $x$ could then be expressed linearly in terms of the fundamentally imaginary part of $\bar{\sigma}$ from (89), i.e., $s_{x}=P_{x} / 2$ of this component $x$. Thus, for $q_{x}$, the approximation $q_{x}=s_{x} A+B$ could be made, whereby, to determine the actual electric charge, $q_{x}= \pm q$ the quantum number $q_{x}$ must be multiplied by $\left|e_{ \pm}\right|$ from (29). In this approach, at least $B=B(\varepsilon)$ must depend on the time structure of $R_{4}^{+}$ or $R_{4}^{-}$, that is, on the time helicity $\varepsilon$ from (93). In $q_{x}$, one can always set $s_{x}=s-x$ or $2 s_{x}=P-2 x$, i.e., $2 q_{x}=(P-2 x) A+2 B$. Since the multiplet components are given by $s$ from (89) in the $R_{3}$ projected degenerate isomorphisms of a stratonic space-spin structure $J$ of the corresponding  $R_{6}$ condensation, they can be regarded as different states of the  $R_{3}$-projection of this synmetronic (i.e., polymetric) $R_{6}$ structure, such that in this $R_{3}$ projection, the properties $q_{x} \neq 0$ of the $d$structure or $q_{x}=0$ of the  $c$ structure appear. However, the stability of the multiplet components need not be required; for since these  components are spin isomorphisms—that is, different states of the $R_{3}$average of the symmetronic $R_{6}$ structure, these multiplet components must generally be transmutable into one another.

In $x_{4}=$ const $\quad$ and their projections into $R_{3}$ fundamentally change their state in the temporal-spatial domains only while preserving the law of compressional isostasy and the constancy of syn-metronic field activators—that is, within the framework of the fundamental symmetry conditions in $R_{6}$. Furthermore, this law of isostasy, in addition to the constancy of the compression state within a closed world volume, also demonstrates the principle of to reduce local compressor densities to minimum values and, in subsequent regions, to level out the constant compressor state within the closed world volume across subsequent event structures.  Physically, this means, in the macroscopic realm, the subsequent  attainment of maximum probability and entropy, but also, in the  microscopic realm, the stability of the lowest possible energy levels. The atomistic quantum structure, in turn, appears as a consequence of the discrete condenser stages and the imaginary character of $s_{(2,3)}$ in correlation with the real unit $s_{(1)}$. The fundamental physical principles known only empirically—namely, the conservation principles (a), the entropy principle (b), and the quantum principle (c) from I., thus appear as consequences of the compositional world selector (19), just as the three-dimensionality of the physical universe and its prototropic structure do.

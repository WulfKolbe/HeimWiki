Semantic architectural units are a $R_{3}$ condensation, which either defines the ponderability of the $c$ and $d$ structures, or as the pseudostratonic spatial sphere of influence of the imaginary condensations. This explains why the $a$ - and $b$ hermetries can become effective in $R_{3}$ at all,  and the empirically observed transmutation of imaginary forms into complex  forms becomes possible. On the other hand, these imaginary forms of hermeticism would then also have to possess the pseudostratonic properties of a spatial spin.

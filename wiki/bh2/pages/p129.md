To describe the specific correlations in the internal structures of the compositional condensation stages of the possible Hermite forms $a$ through $d$ the explicit representation of the specific correlation tensor ${ }^{2} \underline{Q}={ }^{2} \bar{Q}_{(\mu \nu)}^{(\kappa \lambda)}$ is essential when the contraction $\underline{T}$ from (63) is used again. 

To eliminate the correlation tensor, the particular  solution to the synmetronic fundamental problem in the form of  equation (63) appears particularly suitable. However, since (63) was developed approximately in the second metronic validity region, this approximation region should also be assumed for the following analysis. 

With $\underline{\psi}_{k l}=\psi_{k l}^{\underline{\alpha}}$, (63) becomes $\left[\begin{array}{c}i \\ k l\end{array}\right]_{(\mu \nu)}^{(\kappa \lambda)}+\underline{Q}_{m}^{\underline{i}}\left[\begin{array}{cc}m \\ k l\end{array}\right]_{(\mu \nu)}^{(\kappa \lambda)}=\underline{C}_{k l}^{\underline{i}} \underline{\psi}_{k l}$, and this becomes $\partial \gamma_{+k l}^{(\mu v)}=2 \underline{q}_{k l} \underline{\psi}_{k l} \partial \mu$. Since ${ }^{2} \underline{Q} \neq{ }^{2} \overline{0}$ remains unchanged, $\underline{q}_{k l}=\underline{C}_{k l} \sum_{s}\left(\sum_{i}\left(\gamma_{(\kappa \lambda)}^{\underline{i} s}+\underline{Q}_{m}^{\underline{i}} \gamma_{(\kappa \lambda)}^{m} \frac{s}{\lambda}\right)^{-1}\right)=\underline{C}_{k l} \sum_{s} \gamma_{s s}^{(\kappa \lambda)}\left(E+\underline{Q}_{s}\right)^{-1}$ holds,  if the summation is performed over the $q$ Hermitian indices and $\underline{C}_{k l}=\sum_{i} \underline{C}_{k l}^{i}$ as well as $\underline{Q}_{s}=\sum_{i} \underline{\mathrm{Q}}_{\mathrm{s}}^{\mathrm{i}}$, or the scalar selector $\underline{Q}=\sum_{S} \underline{Q}_{S}$ is used. Thus, $\partial \gamma_{+k l}^{(\mu \nu)}=$ follows  $=2 \sum_{s} \gamma_{s s}^{(\kappa \lambda)}\left(E+\underline{Q}_{s}\right)^{-1} ;() \underline{C}_{k l} \underline{\psi}_{k l} \partial \mu$, or, after multiplication by $\delta_{k l}$ and summation, if $p_{(\mu v)}^{(\kappa \lambda)}=\sum_{l=1}^{q} \underline{C}_{l l} \underline{\Psi}_{l l}$ is used for reduction, $2 \sum_{s=1}^{q} \gamma_{s s}^{(\kappa \lambda)}\left(E+Q_{(\mu \nu) s}^{(\kappa \lambda)}\right)^{-1} p_{(\mu \nu)}^{(\kappa \lambda)} \partial \mu=\partial \underline{p}^{2} \bar{\gamma}_{(\mu \nu)}=$

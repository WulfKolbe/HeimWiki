It is assumed that the $\sigma$ term in the protosimplex generator is such that $\exp \left(-A Q_{4}\right)_{k=2}=1 / e$ holds. However, due to (98b), $\exp \left(-A Q_{4}\right)_{k=1}>1 / e$ would then be required, so that $\left(Q_{4} A\right)_{k=2}=1$,  and thus $A(2)=1 / 15$ and $A(1)>1 / 15$ fail. If $z \geqq 1$ a positive integer, then, by $Q_{4}=1$, $\left(Q_{4} A\right)_{k=1}=$ could also be set for $k=1$  $=z / 15<1$ could be set, so that the possible $z$ values lie in the integer interval $1 \leqq z<15$. On the other hand, $15 / z$ should be an integer according to $15 M O D z=0$, which limits $z$ to the values $z=1$ as well as $z=3$ and $z=5$. Here, $z=1$ is also ruled out because then $Q_{4} A$ would be identical for the two possible $k$ values. The decision between $z=3$ and $z=5$ could be made heuristically; since the constant $\xi$ determined, as a limit, the course of the approximation of the (+7) field according to (79b) and (79c). However, it is $(2 \xi-1)^{2}=5$, according to (96b), so that heuristically the choice $z=5$—and thus $\left(Q_{4} A\right)_{k=1}=\frac{1}{3}$—is suggested. Thus, for the two possible $k$ values, $\left(Q_{4} A\right)_{k=1}=1 / 3$ and $\left(Q_{4} A\right)_{k=2}=1$, so that these two relations can be unified in their simplest form by $3 Q_{4} A(k)=2 k-1$.

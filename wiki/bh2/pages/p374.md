For an electromagnetic field quantity $\psi$ with $\operatorname{div} \vec{C}=\operatorname{div} \vec{D}=0$, the transverse wave equation divgrad $\psi \sim \ddot{\psi}$, which establishes the electromagnetic principle of relativity. Thus, in a further approximation, the entire empirical domain of electrostatics and magnetostatics is implied by this approximation, just as is the empirical domain of electrodynamics, optics, and ray optics. 

Thus, (19), together with the cosmogonic consequence (58), appears to imply the entire scope of established contemporary physical knowledge in the form of approximations, while the exact solution (112), together with (102), makes sense of the relatively large number of ponderable elementary structures of matter (as corpuscular particles with rest mass) discovered in the context of high-energy physics and numerically reproduces quite well the properties of these ponderable matter field quanta that have actually been measured. However, the structural theory presented here may suggest a revision of the currently discussed cosmological model of an expanding universe with the Urexplosion as its initial event just as much as it suggests a revision of the quark model, which is also currently under discussion. Thus, the Big Bang model ultimately rests solely on the anticipated interpretation of the cosmological redshift of extragalactic spiral nebula spectra as a Doppler effect and the $3 K$ background radiation. However, this interpretation presupposes that there are no anomalies in this redshift—anomalies that can nevertheless be detected using modern spectroscopic methods and are quantitatively correctly reproduced by (45)—while the $3 K$radiation can also be interpreted differently (according to V.). In the microphysical realm, a certain analogy to the quark model arises;  for in every elementary particle there always appear $G=k+1$ structural units, which, however—unlike in the quark model—appear only as quasi-corpuscular subconstituents. Since the fourfold contouring of all elementary structures through the configuration zones $j \leqq 4$  generally applies, these subconstituents can only be quasi-corpuscular  structural elements that cannot appear freely on their own.

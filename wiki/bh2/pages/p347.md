An examination of the empirical resonance masses first reveals that $f(N)$ must certainly consist of two terms. It should be noted here that the CERN measurement data ${ }^{(4)}$ from 1974 were used as empirical data. The first term $X_{B}(N)$  can vary only insignificantly with $N$ and should indicate where the level of the first resonance term lies above $M_{x}$ $(v)_{x}$, while the second summand $X_{R}(N)$ corresponds to the monotonically increasing function $N \geqq 0$.

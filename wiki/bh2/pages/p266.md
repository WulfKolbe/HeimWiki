This real part is a selector that depends on the dynamic state of the internal correlative coupling structure and, in its general form (in the case of interactions with other condenser terms), depends in a still unknown manner on the $R_{6}$ coordinates. If, on the other hand, one assumes that the internal  correlation state of this coupling structure (of the term under consideration) is not disturbed, but rather that the basic dynamics of these internal  correlating condenser flows are characterized by the steady state of an  undisturbed dynamic equilibrium, then this real part must, according to

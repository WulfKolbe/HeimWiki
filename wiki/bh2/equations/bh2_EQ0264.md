# Equation 98b,

$$
\begin{aligned} & N_{(j)}(t)=n_{j}(t)+Q_{j}, \quad(j \leqq 4) \hat{=}(n, m, p, \sigma), \\ & Q_{n}=3 \cdot 2^{s-2}, \quad Q_{m}=2^{s}-1, \quad Q_{p}=2^{s}+2(-1)^{k}, \\ & Q_{\sigma}=2^{s-1}-1, \quad s=k^{2}+1=\operatorname{const}(t) \end{aligned}
$$

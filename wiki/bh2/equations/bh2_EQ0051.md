# Equation bh2_EQ0051

$$
\begin{aligned} & \left(1-\frac{s}{\varrho}\right)^{2} \approx \frac{s^{2}}{\varrho^{2}}, \text { also } \\ & \varphi^{\prime}\left(s_{0}^{\prime}\right)-\varphi\left(s_{0}\right) \approx \gamma \frac{M_{0}^{\prime}}{s_{0}^{\prime}}\left(1-\frac{s_{0}^{\prime}}{\varrho^{\prime}}\right)^{2}-\gamma \frac{M_{0}}{s_{0}}\left(1-\frac{s_{0}}{\varrho}\right)^{2} \end{aligned}
$$

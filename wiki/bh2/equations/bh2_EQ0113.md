# Equation bh2_EQ0113

$$
\begin{aligned} & \sum_{m=1}^{q} \underline{a}_{m}(k, l)=\underline{a}(k, l), \text { sowie } \sum_{m=1}^{q} \underline{b}_{m s}(k, l)=\underline{b}_{s}(k, l) \text { und } \\ & \sum_{m=1}^{q} \underline{\lambda}_{m}(k, l)=\underline{\lambda}(k, l) \text { zur integrierbaren Fassung } \\ & \left((\underline{a}(k, l)-1) \underline{\partial}_{l}-\sum_{m \neq l} \underline{\partial}_{m}\right) ; F_{k l}^{\underline{i}}+\underline{b}_{s}(k, l)\left[\begin{array}{c} s \\ k l \end{array}\right] ;() F_{k l}^{\underline{i}}=\underline{\lambda}(k, l) F_{k l}^{\underline{i}} \end{aligned}
$$

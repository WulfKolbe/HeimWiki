# Equation bh2_EQ0003

$$
\alpha_{q}\left(\frac{m_{q}}{\mu}\right)^{4}=\frac{2 n_{N}}{\left(2 n_{N}-1\right)^{2}} \approx \frac{1}{2 n_{N}}, \text { also } 2 n_{N} \approx \frac{1}{\alpha_{q}}\left(\frac{\mu}{m_{q}}\right)^{4}
$$

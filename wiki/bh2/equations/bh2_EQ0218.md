# Equation 89

$$
\begin{aligned} & \bar{\sigma}(x)=i\left(\bar{e}_{t} s+\bar{e}_{r} J P_{a}\right), \quad \bar{e}_{t} \perp \bar{e}_{r}, \quad 2 s=P \geqq 0, \\ & 2 J=Q \geqq 0, \quad P_{a}=\alpha_{p}(-1)^{J}, \quad \alpha_{p}= \pm 1 \end{aligned}
$$

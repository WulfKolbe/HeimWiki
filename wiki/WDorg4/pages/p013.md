First, the approaches currently under discussion for the mathematical description of fundamental units of matter and their interactions are presented, and the empirical phenomenon of these interactions is outlined—interactions that are known to be described by measured, dimensionless numbers, which have thus far been accepted as empirical facts, but have not yet been derived from the perspective of a higher level of analysis. 
The approach to this, in addition to [1, Eq. 3d], is based on the fact that the coordinates of the material world $\mathrm{R}_{6}$, due to the solution manifolds of  [1, Eq. 19], i.e., the four forms of hermeticism a through d, and that this coordinate structuring continues into the hyperspace $\mathrm{R}_{12}$ of the world. A consideration of the subspace structure of this hyperspace $\mathrm{R}_{12}$ suggested an investigation of mappings of the subspaces into one another,  which inevitably leads to a dynamics of the events in $\mathbf{R}_{12}$, that is, to a  hyperspace dynamics.

First, this chapter examines general representations of the subspaces of $\mathrm{R}_{12}$ in time and space (i.e., the quantified categories of human perception). The $1 \leq \mathrm{k} \leq 12$ coordinates $\mathrm{x}_{\mathrm{k}}$ of the $\mathrm{R}_{12}$  span the subspaces in the following form: 
$\mathrm{R}_{3}\left(\mathrm{x}_{1}, \mathrm{x}_{2}, \mathrm{x}_{3}\right)$ as physical space, whose dimensions are interchangeable and are algebraically real, while all other coordinates $\mathrm{k}>3$ are of an imaginary nature. The time structure $\mathrm{T}_{1}\left(\mathrm{x}_{4}\right)$ complements the $\mathrm{R}_{3}$ according to the special theory of relativity to form spacetime $\mathrm{R}_{4}$, which in turn is completed by the subspace of organizational coordinates $\mathrm{S}_{2}\left(\mathrm{x}_{5}, \mathrm{x}_{6}\right)$ to form the $\mathrm{R}_{6}$ of the material world. In the subspaces of an informational nature $\mathrm{I}_{2}\left(\mathrm{x}_{7}, \mathrm{x}_{8}\right)$  and the subspace of uninterpretable coordinates $\mathrm{G}_{4}\left(\mathrm{x}_{9}, \ldots, \mathrm{x}_{12}\right)$,  the concepts of matter or energy are no longer defined, so that $\mathrm{I}_{2}$ and $\mathrm{G}_{4}$ must be understood as a non-material background of the material world $\mathrm{R}_{6}$.

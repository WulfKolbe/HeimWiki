Due to the tensor nature of $\varphi_{\mathrm{km}}^{\mathrm{i}}$, a similar transition—namely, $\alpha\left(\mathrm{T}_{\mathrm{ik}}-\mathrm{g}_{\mathrm{ik}} \mathrm{T} / 2\right) \rightarrow \lambda_{\mathrm{p}}(\mathrm{k}, \mathrm{m}) \varphi_{\mathrm{km}}^{\mathrm{p}}$—can be carried out into the micro range. Due to general non-Hermitianity, the $\varphi_{\mathrm{km}}^{\mathrm{i}} \neq \varphi_{\mathrm{km}}^{\mathrm{i}^{\mathrm{x}}}$ cannot be explicitly expressed in terms of the $\mathrm{g}_{\mathrm{ik}} \neq \mathrm{g}_{\mathrm{ik}}^{\mathrm{x}}$, but they depend only on these $\mathrm{g}_{\text {ik }}$, so that they describe purely geometric $\mathrm{R}_{4}-\mathrm{Zu}-$ states. Appropriately chosen Kronecker elements, which are shifted by the tensor components $\varphi_{\mathrm{km}}^{\mathrm{i}}$ , together with nonlinear  operators expressed by the aforementioned tensor components, give rise to  the tensor $R_{k m p}^{p}=R_{k m}$ in the micro-range. For the transition to  the microdomain, this yields $\mathrm{R}_{\mathrm{km}} \rightarrow \mathrm{C}_{\mathrm{p}} \varphi_{\mathrm{km}}^{\mathrm{p}}$ as an operator representation  by the nonlinearly acting operators $\mathrm{C}_{\mathrm{p}}$, whereby the algebraic  character of $\mathrm{R}_{\mathrm{km}}$ in the micro-region is preserved because the summation convention for products of a co- and a contravariant factor with the same indexation applies to the tensor terms of both the macro-region and the micro-region. By superimposing Kronecker elements, the tensor $\varphi_{\mathrm{km}}^{\mathrm{p}}$ is isolated, so that ultimately the non-Hermitian relation $\mathrm{C}_{\mathrm{p}} \varphi_{\mathrm{km}}^{\mathrm{p}}=\lambda_{\mathrm{p}}(\mathrm{k}, \mathrm{m}) \varphi_{\mathrm{km}}^{\mathrm{p}}$ emerges as a system of nonlinear equations. Although the non-Hermitian property $\varphi_{\mathrm{km}}^{\mathrm{p}} \neq \varphi_{\mathrm{km}}^{\mathrm{p}}$ holds, the factors $\lambda_{p}(k, m)=\left(\lambda_{p}(k, m)\right)^{*}$ in the operator relation turn out to be real. The total indexing of these $\lambda_{\mathrm{p}}=\lambda_{\mathrm{p}}^{*}$ by (k, m) indicates their association with the covariant indexing of the tensor component $\varphi_{\mathrm{km}}^{\mathrm{p}}$. 

Radical geometrization thus leads, in the micro-scale, to the system $\left(C_{p}-\lambda_{p}(k, m)\right) \varphi_{k m}^{p}=0$, which can be interpreted, so to speak, as a system of nonlinear eigenvalue relations that tensorially describes metric structural states through nonlinear relations. Furthermore, it was shown in [Resch1989](../references/WDorg4_REF_Resch1989.md) that a geometric ultimate unit $\tau$ (referred to as a “metron”) exists, for which $8 \tau \mathrm{c}^{3}=3 \gamma \mathrm{~h}$ must be set, where h denotes the action quantum. Accordingly, in the undisturbed Euclidean  case, the $\mathrm{R}_{4}$ coordinates $\mathrm{x}_{\mathrm{i}}$ would always be integer multiples of the geometric  length element $\delta \mathrm{s}_{0}=\sqrt{\tau}$, where $\delta \mathrm{s}_{0}$ corresponds to the Planck length up to the factor $\sqrt{3 / 8}$. 

On the one hand, $\left(C_{p}-\lambda_{p}(k, m)\right) \varphi_{k m}^{p}=0$ is satisfied term by term, so that 
$\mathrm{C}_{(\mathrm{p})} \varphi_{\mathrm{km}}^{(\mathrm{p})}=\lambda_{(\mathrm{p})}(\mathrm{k}, \mathrm{m}) \varphi_{\mathrm{km}}^{(\mathrm{p})}$ holds, where (p) means that the summation convention has been lifted. On the other hand, during the transition to 
$\mathrm{R}_{\mathrm{km}} \rightarrow \mathrm{C}_{\mathrm{p}} \varphi_{\mathrm{km}}^{\mathrm{p}}$, the algebraic properties of the Ricci tensor are preserved.

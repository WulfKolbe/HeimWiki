Thus, however, $\varepsilon_{\mathrm{ik}}^{(\mathrm{m})} \neq 0$ holds for all 64 components, where these quantities are the elements of the eight-row square energy density matrix, which is to be interpreted as a tensor in a $\mathrm{R}_{8} \supset \mathrm{R}_{6}$.

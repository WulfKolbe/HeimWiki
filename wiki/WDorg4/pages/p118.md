The difference between the two basic coupling types, 1 and 2, is already reflected in the distribution of the coupling constants in the sets $\mathrm{w}_{0}$ through $\mathrm{w}_{5}$. Thus, the mediating Type 3, which is characterized by $\mathrm{S}_{2}$  of the $\mathrm{R}_{6}=\mathrm{R}_{4} \cup \mathrm{~S}_{2}$ of the material world, be geometrically incorporated into the transition region of the $\mathrm{R}_{4}$ in the manner described by $\mathrm{V}_{8}=\mathrm{G}_{4} \cup \mathrm{I}_{2} \cup \mathrm{~S}_{2}$, although it remains a question of hyperspace dynamics whether the Type 2 transformational couplings, thus extended, appear physically as an independent gravitational structure when accessing Type 1 energetic couplings of the $\mathrm{R}_{4}$ structures (via the time structure $\mathrm{T}_{1}$) physically appears as an independent gravitational structure, or whether probability fields, according to (9a), modify the energetic process. 

The Type 1 energetic coupling constants of $\mathrm{R}_{4}$ are the elements of the set $\mathrm{w}_{2}$, where the ponderability of Wq is determined by the elements  of the sets $\mathrm{w}_{1}$ and $\mathrm{w}_{4}$. The transformational coupling constants, on the other hand, are the elements of the set $\mathrm{w}_{3}$. The set $\mathrm{w}_{5}$ then contains the decayed energetic coupling constants that have been transmuted by transformational couplings, whereas $\mathbf{w}_{0}$ is the coupling constant of the unified field consisting of electromagnetic, weak, and strong coupling and thus also belongs to Type 1. If, for $\mathrm{j}=3$ and  $\mathrm{j}=5$ are added as $\underline{\mathrm{w}}_{\mathrm{j}}=\mathrm{w}_{\mathrm{j} \alpha}+\mathrm{w}_{\mathrm{j} \beta}$ and multiplied as $\underline{\mathrm{w}}_{\mathrm{j}}=\mathrm{w}_{\mathrm{j} \alpha} \mathrm{w}_{\mathrm{j} \beta}$ in $\mathrm{w}_{\mathrm{j}}$, then the sets of Type 1 energetic couplings can be summarized as $\mathrm{K}_{\mathrm{E}}$ and those of Type 2 transformational couplings as $\mathrm{K}_{\mathrm{T}}$.

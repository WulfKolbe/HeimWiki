Coordinate dependencies are specified in parentheses, $\mathrm{a}\left(\mathrm{x}_{5}, \mathrm{x}_{6}\right)$ as well as $\mathrm{b}\left(\mathrm{x}_{4}, \mathrm{x}_{5}, \mathrm{x}_{6}\right)$ and $\mathrm{c}\left(\mathrm{x}_{1} \ldots \mathrm{x}_{3}, \mathrm{x}_{5}, \mathrm{x}_{6}\right)$ and $\mathrm{d}\left(\mathrm{x}_{1}, \ldots, \mathrm{x}_{6}\right)$, which determines the structuring of the $\mathrm{R}_{6}$ coordinates in terms of hermetic subspaces of $\mathrm{R}_{6}=\mathrm{R}_{3}\left(\mathrm{x}_{1}, \mathrm{x}_{2}, \mathrm{x}_{3}\right) \cup \mathrm{T}_{1}\left(\mathrm{x}_{4}\right) \cup \mathrm{S}_{2}\left(\mathrm{x}_{5}, \mathrm{x}_{6}\right)$. These four forms of hermitian structure describe all elementary matter field quanta (Mq) structurally, whereby these Mq, in the ponderable case, appear as centers of interaction fields, so that the field and the field source always appear as a single entity. This means that the interaction fields determine a Hermitian form from the outset. On the other hand, $\mathrm{R}_{6} \subset \mathrm{R}_{12}=\mathrm{R}_{6} \cup \mathrm{I}_{2}\left(\mathrm{x}_{7}, \mathrm{x}_{8}\right) \cup \mathrm{G}_{4}\left(\mathrm{x}_{9}, \ldots, \mathrm{x}_{12}\right)$ proves to be so that the all-encompassing $\mathrm{R}_{12}$ is represented by the structured set of coordinates $\mathrm{K}=\left\{\left(\mathrm{x}_{1} \ldots \mathrm{x}_{3}\right),\left(\mathrm{x}_{4}\right),\left(\mathrm{x}_{5}, \mathrm{x}_{6}\right),\left(\mathrm{x}_{7}, \mathrm{x}_{8}\right),\left(\mathrm{x}_{9}, \ldots, \mathrm{x}_{12}\right)\right\}$ with the complex cardinal number $\mathrm{K}_{12}=\{3 ; 1 ; 2 ; 2 ; 4\}$. In this $\mathrm{K}_{12}$, the four Hermitian forms reappear in the partial cardinal number complex $\mathrm{K}_{6}=\{3 ; 1 ; 2\}$.  According to [1, Chap. IV], a( $\mathrm{x}_{5}, \mathrm{x}_{6}$ ) must appear as a graviton system at the intersection with spacetime, which always accompanies the structures b, c, and d, while the form $b$ determines the electromagnetic photon field as a time condensation, while c and d describe the ponderable structures of electrically neutral (c) or charged (d) elementary corpuscles. However, if the Hermetic form a can be represented as a gravitational field and the Hermetic form b as an electromagnetic interaction field when intersecting with $\mathrm{R}_{4}$,  then the Hermetic form d, which according to [Resch1989](../references/WDorg4_REF_Resch1989.md) and [Resch1984](../references/WDorg4_REF_Resch1984.md) describes an electric charge field, can still be associated with a weak interaction field. Finally, in addition to the gravitational interaction field (a), the electromagnetic interaction field (b), and the weak interaction field in the form (d), there will also be a strong interaction field determined by this Hermitian form c. In $\mathrm{K}_{12}$, the elements 3, 1, and 2 represent the subspace coordinates of a material world $\mathrm{R}_{6}$ and, consequently, three basic interactions whose structural combinations lead to the hermetic forms a through d. The cardinal number complex thus allows the basic interactions or basic hermetic forms to be specified in a concise form, such that the absence of an element is identical to the absence of the corresponding basic hermetic form or basic interaction. This means that certain time- and space-independent constants (16) can be assigned to each of the named hermetic forms.

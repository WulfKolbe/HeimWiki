In the image, a particle exists prior to the measurement process between the idea of an event and the event itself—or, in other words, between superimposed possibilities. After the measurement, one of these potentially contradictory tendencies is singled out in order to obtain the full status of reality, which characterizes the transition from future possibility to perfective facticity. This image of the dual world fits perfectly into the concept of hyperspace, as described in [Resch1989](../references/WDorg4_REF_Resch1989.md) and [Resch1984](../references/WDorg4_REF_Resch1984.md) as well as in this paper. Here, the subspace defined as the material world  $\mathrm{R}_{6} \subset \mathrm{R}_{12}$ appears as an energetically determined space whose non-Euclidean structures, as elementary structures (hermetic forms) of a material nature, can be fully  described in a $\mathrm{R}_{6}$ geometry, which corresponds to the representation of a particle in the corpuscular picture. On the other hand, $\mathrm{R}_{12}=\mathrm{R}_{6}\left(\mathrm{x}_{1}, \ldots, \mathrm{x}_{6}\right) \cup \mathrm{V}_{6}\left(\mathrm{x}_{7}, \ldots, \mathrm{x}_{12}\right)$ holds, where $\mathrm{R}_{6}=\mathrm{R}_{3} \cup \mathrm{~T}_{1} \cup \mathrm{~S}_{2}$ and  $\mathrm{V}_{6}=\mathrm{I}_{2} \cup \mathrm{G}_{4}$ hold, so that structures of $\mathrm{V}_{6} \subset \mathrm{R}_{12}$, when mapped into spacetime, must appear as projective fields, which would correspond to the wave picture. This latter mapping possibility was already mentioned in [1, Chap. IV, 5] in connection with the mapping process $\mathrm{I}_{2} \rightarrow \mathrm{R}_{6}$. Since the $\mathrm{G}_{4}$ always appears separate from the $\mathbf{R}_{8}=\mathbf{R}_{6} \cup \mathbf{I}_{2}$, access to this $\mathbf{G}_{4}$ to the $\mathrm{R}_{8}$ only through a previously discussed $\mathrm{R}_{\mathrm{n}}^{*}$ in the form $\mathrm{G}_{4}\left(\mathrm{x}_{9}, \ldots, \mathrm{x}_{12}\right) \rightarrow \mathrm{R}_{\mathrm{n}}^{*}\left(\mathrm{y}_{1}, \ldots, \mathrm{y}_{\mathrm{n}}\right) \rightarrow \mathrm{R}_{4}^{\prime}\left(\mathrm{x}_{1}, \ldots, \mathrm{x}_{4}\right)$ as an intermediary space. Here, $\mathrm{R}_{4}^{\prime}$ means that in this spacetime, the structures projected from $\mathrm{G}_{4}$ appear as $\mathrm{G}_{4}$ mapping fields. Thus, there are two spacetimes intertwined with one another: namely, the physical spacetime $\mathrm{R}_{4}^{\mathrm{p}}$ and a spacetime of transprojections (mapping fields) $\mathrm{R}_{4}^{\mathrm{a}}=\mathrm{R}_{4}^{\prime}$ as the final link in a chain of mappings originating from $\mathrm{G}_{4}$. The actually observable  spacetime $\mathrm{R}_{4}$ is thus the entanglement $\mathrm{R}_{4}=\mathrm{R}_{4}^{\mathrm{p}} * \mathrm{R}_{4}^{\mathrm{a}}$ of the spacetime of energy-matter structures according to [Resch1989](../references/WDorg4_REF_Resch1989.md) and [Resch1984](../references/WDorg4_REF_Resch1984.md) with the spacetime $\mathrm{R}_{4}^{\mathrm{a}}$ of the  mappings from $G_{4}$, which reach $R_{4}^{p}$ via the mediator space.

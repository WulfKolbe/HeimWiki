Following the publication of Burkhard Heim’s two-volume standard work, *Elementary Structures of Matter: A Unified Quantum Field Theory of Matter and Gravitation* (Vol. 1, $<sup>2</sup> 1989$ / Vol. 2, 1984), along with the companion volume by Burkhard Heim and Walter Dröscher: *Introduction to Burkhard Heim: Elementary Structures of Matter with a Glossary and Formula Index* (1985), these publications are now being reissued in the series “Burkhard Heim: A Unified Description of the World.” The third volume in this series is the extension of the geometrization program of Heim’s theory, which resulted from the collaboration between W. Dröscher and B. Heim.  As already indicated in Heim [Resch1989](../references/WDorg4_REF_Resch1989.md) and [Resch1984](../references/WDorg4_REF_Resch1984.md), according to W. Dröscher, an extension of the six-dimensional coordinate space to a coordinate space with eight or twelve dimensions could be derived, which makes it possible to obtain all known and as yet unknown interaction fields. 

Heim’s theory is, in fact, to be understood as a framework whose  fundamental property is the geometrization of the fundamental physical units. It exhibits the following basic characteristics: 
1. Distinction between three real (height, width, depth) and three imaginary (time, entelechy, eon) coordinates. The conventional space-time model—that is, the four-dimensional view of the world—is extended by two dimensions: the dimension $x_{5}$ (entelechy), which evaluates the states of organization that appear to be constantly actualizing in $\mathrm{x}_{4}$ (time), and the dimension $x_{6}$ (Aeon), which controls the ambiguous direction of actualization in $\mathrm{x}_{4}$. Accordingly, a distinction is made between manifest and latent events. 
2. Quantization of multidimensional space as a result of an indivisible geometric unit of area $\tau$, which is of the same order of magnitude as the  square of the Planck length. 
3. Novel cosmology and the resulting Hermitian manifold geometry. The Hermitian fundamental tensor lying in $\mathrm{R}_{6}$ is compositionally composed of the non-Hermitian fundamental tensors describing the manifold geometry.

These conversion constants not only cause the transformation of massless interaction quanta into ponderable ones, but may also involve the conversion of photons of very specific density and energy into vector gravitons, as well as the generation of interaction quanta with an extremely small and empirically unknown coupling constant from existing vector and tensor gravitons. The coupling constants of the electromagnetic, weak, and strong interactions are of the glide type, i.e., energy-dependent. 

Therefore, independent of the coupling constants, a set of elementary lengths $\delta \mathrm{s}_{\mathrm{i}}$ was determined using the previously employed algorithm of higher-order sets and associated  combination operations was determined using the algorithm previously employed, and these were assigned to the coupling constants, such that a dependence of the coupling constants of order i on an elementary length $\delta \mathrm{s}_{\mathrm{i}}$ arose. Since, according to the uncertainty principle $\delta \mathrm{s}_{\mathrm{i}}$, a specific energy value $\mathrm{E}_{\mathrm{i}}$ , the dependence of a coupling constant on a specific energy value $\mathrm{E}_{\mathrm{i}}$ was established. It was found that a portion of the energy values $\mathrm{E}_{\mathrm{i}}$ played a decisive role in the evolutionary history of the universe.  Further details can be found in Chapter III, sections 2–4, and Chapter IV, section 1.

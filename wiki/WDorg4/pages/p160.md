Undoubtedly, the preceding work, [Resch1989](../references/WDorg4_REF_Resch1989.md) and [Resch1984](../references/WDorg4_REF_Resch1984.md), opens up the possibility of a comprehensive framework for a picture of the world and its background; but it should be noted that such a picture, which emerges from  the works cited, can initially be nothing more than a sketch of the quantifiable  (i.e., physical) “shadow” of the real world and its background.

VIII] remain. As the space bubble continues to expand,  a continuous cooling process occurs until this dynamic process finally  comes to a halt. In this way, the radiation temperature can eventually approach absolute zero, so that electromagnetic radiation close to this absolute temperature emanates from the interior of the space bubbles of an elementary universe.

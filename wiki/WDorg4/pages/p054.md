The same $\mathrm{R}_{4}^{*}$ also existed for $\mathrm{t}=\mathrm{T}_{1}$, as shown in Figure $\mathrm{G}_{4}\left(\mathrm{x}_{9}, \ldots, \mathrm{x}_{12}\right) \rightarrow \mathrm{R}_{4}^{*}$. Because of $\operatorname{dim}\left(\mathrm{R}_{4}^{*}\right)=4$, the $\mathrm{R}_{4}^{*}$ can only be spanned by the vector components $\mathrm{b}_{2}^{\prime} \overline{\mathrm{e}}_{\mathrm{m}}$ or $\mathrm{b}_{5}^{\prime} \overline{\mathrm{e}}_{\mathrm{t}} \in \mathrm{R}_{4}^{*}$ according to (3) and (5) or (5a), because only these vector components are elements of the $\mathrm{R}_{4}^{*}$. At time $\mathrm{T}_{1}<\mathrm{T}$, there were minimal lengths that, according to [1, GI. 15] can be calculated from the metron $\tau$ according to $\delta \mathrm{s}_{0}=\sqrt{\tau}$. However, according to [2, Eq. 37 and Eq. 37a], both $\tau$ and the $\mathrm{R}_{3}$diameter $\mathrm{D}(\tau)$  depend on the current age of the universe, but due to $\mathrm{T}-\mathrm{T}_{1} \ll \mathrm{~T}$, this  dependence can be neglected in the range $\mathrm{T}-\mathrm{T}_{1}$ and thus, as a good approximation,  $\delta \mathrm{s}_{0}=$ can be assumed to be constant. 

If one of these elementary lengths $\delta \mathrm{s}_{0}$ is converted at time $\mathrm{T}_{1}$ by the intervention  $\mathrm{G}_{4}\left(\mathrm{x}_{9}, \ldots, \mathrm{x}_{12}\right) \rightarrow \mathrm{R}_{4}^{*}$ at time $\mathrm{T}_{1}$, then $\mathrm{b}_{2}^{\prime} \delta \mathrm{s}_{0}$ holds according to [1, Chap. IV, 4]. Since $\mathrm{b}_{5}^{\prime} \overline{\mathrm{e}}_{\mathrm{t}} \in \mathrm{R}_{4}^{*}$ is also true, $\mathrm{G}_{4} \rightarrow \mathrm{R}_{4}^{*}$ would also make $\mathrm{b}_{5}^{\prime} \delta \mathrm{s}_{0}$ possible; but $\mathrm{b}_{5}^{\prime} \delta \mathrm{s}_{0}>\delta \mathrm{s}_{0}$ holds. On the other hand, according to [1, Chap. IV, 4], $\delta \mathrm{s}_{1}=\delta \mathrm{s}_{0}\left(1-\left(\mathrm{M}_{0} / \mathrm{m}_{\mathrm{M}}\right)^{4}\right)$ also holds, where $\mathrm{m}_{\mathrm{M}}=\sqrt[4]{2} \cdot \sqrt{\mathrm{ch} / \gamma}$ is the maximon mass according to [1, Eq. 27a]. By comparison, $\mathrm{M}_{0}=\mathrm{m}_{\mathrm{M}} \sqrt[4]{\alpha}$ follows, which means that as a result of the interaction $\mathrm{G}_{4} \rightarrow \mathrm{R}_{4}^{*} \quad$ at time $\mathrm{T}_{1}$ a mass $\mathrm{M}_{0}$ is created in physical space; however, this mass cannot be temporally stable and, according to the principle of compressor isostasy from [2, Chapters VII and VIII], eventually decays via a decay chain into a larger number of protons as well as neutron and electron masses ( $\mathrm{m}_{\mathrm{p}}, \mathrm{m}_{\mathrm{n}}, \mathrm{m}_{\mathrm{e}}$ ) through $\gamma$ emission. In the nucleon doublet, however, $\mathrm{m}_{\mathrm{p}} \approx \mathrm{m}_{\mathrm{n}}>\mathrm{m}_{\mathrm{e}}$, so that in cosmological studies the average nucleon mass $\mathrm{m}_{\mathrm{N}}=\left(\mathrm{m}_{\mathrm{p}}+\mathrm{m}_{\mathrm{n}}\right) / 2$—which is temporally stable—can be used. According to structural studies of the terms of uniform particle spectra from [Resch1984](../references/WDorg4_REF_Resch1984.md), both mesons (configuration number $\mathrm{k}=1$ ) as well as baryons (configuration number $\mathrm{k}=2$ ) are described in the $\mathrm{R}_{6}$ of the world by aggregates  of cyclic condenser fluxes, which in physical space $\mathrm{k}+1$ exhibit partial flux aggregates as $\mathrm{R}_{3}$ components, which, together with the $\mathrm{R}_{3}$ discriminant field of the Straton, determines the ponderability of the mass terms. For  cosmic masses, the components of the nucleon doublet are obviously decisive, so that for the smallest, cosmologically relevant unit of mass  $\mathrm{m}_{\mathrm{a}}=\mathrm{m}_{\mathrm{N}} /(\mathrm{k}+1)=\mathrm{m}_{\mathrm{N}} / 3$, due to $\mathrm{k}=2$ , although $\mathrm{m}_{\mathrm{a}}$, as the average mass fraction of a $\mathrm{R}_{3}$partial flux cannot exist independently, since these partial fluxes are mutually dependent as quasi-corpuscular subconstituents.

Für  kosmische Massen sind offensichtlich die Komponenten des Nukleonendou bletts bestimmend, so daß für die kleinste, kosmisch relevante Masseneinheit  $\mathrm{m}_{\mathrm{a}}=\mathrm{m}_{\mathrm{N}} /(\mathrm{k}+1)=\mathrm{m}_{\mathrm{N}} / 3$ wegen $\mathrm{k}=2$ gesetzt werden kann, obgleich $\mathrm{m}_{\mathrm{a}}$ als  mittlerer Massenanteil eines $\mathrm{R}_{3}$-Partialflusses nicht frei existieren kann, da  diese Partialflüsse sich als quasikorpuskuläre Subkonstituenten wechselseitig  bedingen.

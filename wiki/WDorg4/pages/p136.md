$\mathrm{W}_{5} \approx \mathrm{w}_{4 \alpha}(\exp (-24 \pi))$, it is possible to assume the same linear relationship between $\underline{\underline{\mathrm{m}}}_{5}$ and $\mathrm{m}_{4 \alpha}$. IV, 4], at the time of the  material cosmogony $\mathrm{T}_{1}<\mathrm{T}$ (the present world age), an elementary length  $\delta \underline{\mathrm{s}}_{0}=0,2588965 \delta \mathrm{~s}_{0}$ with $\delta \mathrm{s}_{0}=\sqrt{\tau_{\mathrm{T}}}$, which determines a maximum distance 
$\mathrm{D}^{\prime}=\mathrm{D}_{\mathrm{T}} \sqrt{\tau^{\prime} / \tau_{\mathrm{T}}}=\mathrm{D}_{\mathrm{T}} \delta^{\prime} / \delta \mathrm{s}_{0}$. Numerically, the following hold true: 
$\delta^{\prime}=1,917964 \cdot 10^{-126} \mathrm{~m}$ as well as $\mathrm{D}_{\mathrm{T}}=6,025809 \cdot 10^{125} \mathrm{~m}$ and 
$\delta \mathrm{s}_{0}=2,48054 \cdot 10^{-35} \mathrm{~m}$ according to $[1,262-264]$, from which $\mathrm{D}^{\prime}=0,465918 \cdot 10^{35} \mathrm{~m}$ follows. $\mathrm{m}_{4 \alpha} \approx \mathrm{~m}_{5} \cdot \exp (24 \pi)$ holds, corresponds to an uncertainty distance of $\mathrm{m}_{4 \alpha} \cong 323,706 \mathrm{~m}$.

To discuss the electromagnetic coupling constant $\beta_{1}=\mathrm{w}_{21}$, one must first refer to the conditions of the origin of the world $\mathrm{t}=0$. Here, dimensionless elementary “lengths” $\mathrm{b}_{\mathrm{i}}$ with unit vectors $\overline{\mathrm{e}}_{\mathrm{r}}$  according to $\mathrm{b}_{1} \overline{\mathrm{e}}_{\mathrm{i}} \in \mathrm{R}_{1}^{*}$ as well as $\mathrm{b}_{2} \overline{\mathrm{e}}_{\mathrm{j}} \in \mathrm{R}_{2}^{*}$ and $\mathrm{b}_{3} \overline{\mathrm{e}}_{\mathrm{k}} \in \mathrm{R}_{3}^{*}$ (here, the indices j and k take the values $\mathrm{j}=1,2$ and $\mathrm{k}=1,2,3$ ), forming a total of three subspaces that span a space $\mathrm{R}_{6}^{*}=\mathrm{R}_{1}^{*} \cup \mathrm{R}_{2}^{*} \cup \mathrm{R}_{3}^{*}$. This $\mathrm{R}_{6}^{*}$ at $\mathrm{t}=0$ can be compared to the current $\mathrm{R}_{6}=\mathrm{R}_{3} \cup \mathrm{~T}_{1} \cup \mathrm{~S}_{2}$ and its element lengths.

Depends on the curvilinear coordinates $\mathrm{z}_{\mathrm{k}}(\mathrm{k}=1,2, \ldots, 8)$ of the curvilinear coordinates $\mathrm{x}_{\mathrm{i}}(\mathrm{i}=1, \ldots, 8)$ in the manner described in $\mathrm{z}_{\mathrm{k}}=\left.\mathrm{z}_{\mathrm{k}}\left(\mathrm{x}_{1} \ldots \mathrm{x}_{8}\right)\right|_{\mathrm{k}=1} ^{8}$. At every point of $\mathrm{R}_{8}$, a tangent space with Euclidean coordinates $\left.\xi_{p}\left(z_{1} \ldots z_{8}\right)\right|_{p=1} ^{8}$ can be constructed, so that ultimately the functional dependence 
$\xi_{\mathrm{p}}=\xi_{\mathrm{p}}\left(\mathrm{z}_{1}\left(\mathrm{x}_{1} \ldots \mathrm{x}_{8}\right) \ldots \mathrm{z}_{8}\left(\mathrm{x}_{1} \ldots \mathrm{x}_{8}\right)\right)$ arises. From $\mathrm{g}_{\mathrm{ik}}^{(\mu \mathrm{v})}$ with  $\mathrm{g}_{\mathrm{ik}}^{(\mu \nu)}=\left.\frac{\delta \xi_{p}}{\delta \mathrm{z}_{(\mu)}} \frac{\delta \mathrm{z}_{(\mu)}}{\delta \mathrm{x}_{\mathrm{i}}} \frac{\delta \xi_{p}}{\delta \mathrm{z}_{(\nu)}} \frac{\delta \mathrm{z}_{(\nu)}}{\delta \mathrm{x}_{\kappa}}\right|_{p, \mathrm{i}, \kappa, \mu, \nu=1} ^{8}$, one can then form $\Gamma$quantities can be formed, which represent not only the known interaction fields but also as yet unknown fields (the parentheses here denote the lifting of the summation convention). The transcoordinates lying beyond the $\mathrm{R}_{4}$ are mapped onto the $\mathrm{R}_{4}$ in this process. See Chapter IV, Section 2. 

If, instead of a $\mathrm{R}_{8}$ with the Euclidean coordinates $\mathrm{x}_{1} \ldots \mathrm{x}_{8}$, a  twelve-dimensional space is taken as the basis, which is structured according to 
$\mathrm{R}_{12}=\mathrm{R}_{3}\left(\mathrm{x}_{1} \ldots \mathrm{x}_{3}\right) \cup \mathrm{T}_{1}\left(\mathrm{x}_{4}\right) \cup \mathrm{S}_{2}\left(\mathrm{x}_{5}, \mathrm{x}_{6}\right) \cup \mathrm{I}_{2}\left(\mathrm{x}_{7}, \mathrm{x}_{8}\right) \cup \mathrm{G}_{4}\left(\mathrm{x}_{9} \ldots \mathrm{x}_{12}\right) \quad$, then the coordinates of the $\mathrm{G}_{4}$ can no longer be directly transferred to the $\mathrm{R}_{4}\left(\mathrm{x}_{1} \ldots \mathrm{x}_{4}\right)=\mathrm{R}_{3}\left(\mathrm{x}_{1} \ldots \mathrm{x}_{3}\right) \cup \mathrm{T}_{1}\left(\mathrm{x}_{4}\right)$. Via a 
4 (2M + 1)-dimensional intermediary space 
$\mathrm{R}_{4(2 \mathrm{M}+1)}^{*}\left(\mathrm{x}_{9}{ }^{-\mathrm{M}^{*}}, \mathrm{x}_{10}{ }^{-\mathrm{M}^{*}}, \ldots \mathrm{x}_{12}{ }^{-\mathrm{M}^{*}}, \ldots \mathrm{x}_{9}{ }^{\mathrm{o}^{*}}, \mathrm{x}_{10}^{\mathrm{o}^{*}}, \ldots \mathrm{x}_{12}^{\mathrm{o}^{*}} \ldots \mathrm{x}_{9}{ }^{\mathrm{M}^{*}}, \mathrm{x}_{10}{ }^{\mathrm{M}^{*}}, \ldots \mathrm{x}_{12}{ }^{\mathrm{M}^{*}}\right)$,  where -M, ...o, ...M denotes an indexing of the coordinates, the  transcoordinates $\mathrm{x}_{9} \ldots \mathrm{x}_{12}$ are mapped into a $\mathrm{R}_{4}^{\mathrm{a}}\left(\mathrm{x}_{1}{ }^{*} \ldots \mathrm{x}_{4}{ }^{*}\right)$ using the function  $\mathrm{F}\left(\mathrm{x}_{1}{ }^{*} \ldots \mathrm{x}_{4}{ }^{*}\right)$ in this space (the * symbol denotes the dimensionless coordinates here). In this process, an index change 9 ... $12 \rightarrow 1$... 4  takes place. The unique function $\mathrm{F}\left(\mathrm{x}_{1}{ }^{*} \ldots \mathrm{x}_{4}{ }^{*}\right)$ is only determined by a suitable choice of parameters during this mapping process; for now, any function F in this space is possible. 

As it turns out, F in $\mathrm{R}_{4}^{\mathrm{a}}$ provides the representation of probability amplitudes known from quantum theory and thus corresponds to the wave picture of quantum duality, whereas the $\mathrm{R}_{4}^{\mathrm{p}}$ is reserved for the corpus-calular picture of the theory in [Resch1989](../references/WDorg4_REF_Resch1989.md) and [Resch1984](../references/WDorg4_REF_Resch1984.md). 

Thus, an entangled space consisting of $\mathrm{R}_{4}^{\mathrm{p}}$ and $\mathrm{R}_{4}^{\mathrm{a}}$, $\mathrm{R}_{4}=\mathrm{R}_{4}^{\mathrm{p}} * \mathrm{R}_{4}^{\mathrm{a}}$, that reveals the inseparability of the corpuscular and wave pictures. This was discussed in detail in Chapter II, Section 4.

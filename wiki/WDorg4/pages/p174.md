Dipl.-Ing. Walter Dröscher, born in Vienna in 1940, studied communications engineering at the Vienna University of Technology after graduating from high school in 1959. Even  during his studies and later on, he took a keen interest in mathematics and  general relativity; He received his diploma in 1967. 
From 1968 to 1974, he worked at Siemens AG Austria as a technical specialist in a  development department. As part of his development work, he filed  several invention disclosures, which led to patents in Austria and its states. 
Joined the Austrian Patent Office in 1974; appointed as a permanent technical examiner in 1977; head of a technical department since early 1993. 
Publications: W. Dröscher – B. Heim: Structures of the Physical World and Its Non-Material Aspect (1996); Contributed to the new edition of: B. Heim: Elementary Structures of Matter: A Unified Structural Quantum Field Theory of Matter and Gravitation, Vol. 1 (3rd revised ed. 1996); Contributions in: A. Resch: The World of Worldviews (1994); B. Heim /  W. Dröscher: Introduction to Burkhard Heim: Unified Description  of the World with an Index of Terms and Formulas [Aufl1996](../references/WDorg4_REF_Aufl1996.md). 

Burkhard Heim, M.Sc. in Physics, was born in Potsdam in 1925. In 1944, an explosion occurred during laboratory work at the Reich Institute for Chemical and Technical Research in Berlin, in which he lost both hands, was nearly completely blinded, and suffered severe hearing loss. Beginning in 1945, he studied chemistry; from 1949, he studied theoretical physics in Göttingen, completing his studies in 1954 with the Hauptdiplom. Since 1949, he has been conducting independent research on a general field theory in which all physical fields and their sources are uniformly described as dynamic properties of purely geometric structures. This theory was developed over the past decades under the most difficult external conditions and has been partially published in several installments since 1975. Interest in Heim’s theory is growing steadily and has been particularly spurred by the publication of the complete edition (1996) under the title “Burkhard Heim: A Unified Description of the World”: B. Heim: Elementary Structures of Matter, Vol. 1 [Aufl1996](../references/WDorg4_REF_Aufl1996.md); B. Heim: Elementary Structures of Matter, Vol. 2 [Aufl1996](../references/WDorg4_REF_Aufl1996.md);  W. Dröscher / B. Heim: Structures of the Physical World and Its  Non-Material Aspect [Aufl1996](../references/WDorg4_REF_Aufl1996.md); B. Heim / W. Dröscher: Introduction to Burkhard Heim: A Unified Description of the World, with an Index of Terms and Formulas .

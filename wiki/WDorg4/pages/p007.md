Universality stems from the fact that the chain of mappings always appears as a control process whenever an event—in the sense of a nonstationary dynamic—establishes relative temporal zero points, whereby control occurs via non-energetic (or non-material) occurs precisely via these probability fields and restructures the temporal behavior of matter existing in the physical realm. 

This quantitative investigation evidently constitutes a logical framework that, although (despite the non-material aspect of the world) it permits only quantitative  statements, but is suitable for testing unquestionable theses regarding worldviews, because the thesis in question must in any case satisfy this quantitative “shadow” of the world. 

I would therefore like to thank the two authors for their extremely rewarding collaboration, which was the prerequisite for such a publication. My thanks go to Ms. Gerda Heim for writing the manuscript and to Ms. Priska Kapferer, M.A., for typesetting and designing the volume. 

I am well aware that this work places the highest demands on the reader. The “Introduction to Burkhard Heim: Unified Description of the  World with a Glossary and Formula Index” is intended to facilitate reading. 

Those who are unable to work through the aforementioned main work as an introductory aid  may refer to Heim’s more accessible publications: The Cosmic Experience Space of Man; The Elementary Process of Life; Postmortem States? The Televariant Area of Integral World Structures; and Uniform Description of the Material World: Informative Summary of “Elementary Structures of Matter,” Volume 1 and Volume 2 (Resch Verlag, Innsbruck). 

I hope the reader finds great enjoyment in this extraordinary work. 

Innsbruck, January 27, 1996 
Andreas Resch

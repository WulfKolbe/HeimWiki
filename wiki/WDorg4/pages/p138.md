The Lorentz equations  are subjected to an inertial transformation. However, this would mean that in this exceptional case of special information hermeticism, the constancy of inertia and the speed of light would be violated. 

According to the present theory of interactions, the $\mathrm{G}_{4} \rightarrow \mathrm{R}_{\mathrm{n}}^{*} \rightarrow \mathrm{I}_{2}^{*}$ not only controls the quantum-theoretical probability fields in $\mathrm{R}_{4}$ according to (9) and (9a), but, according to (24), it also intervenes directly via the set $\mathrm{K}_{\mathrm{T}}$ in the energetic couplings $\mathrm{K}_{\mathrm{E}}$ of spacetime structures. For this reason, the question must be raised as to whether this process may have initiated the cosmogony of matter (as a controlling mapping process).

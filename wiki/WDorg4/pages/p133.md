$\Gamma_{\mathrm{jkm}}^{(\mu v)}=\langle\mu v\rangle$ in which at least one coordinate index  corresponds to an element of the set ( $\mathrm{x}_{5} \ldots \mathrm{x}_{8}$ ) are considered to be transforming fields. 

A large portion of the discussed groups of fields $<\mu \nu>$ is characterized by  energetic, i.e., spacetime coupling constants from the set $\mathrm{K}_{\mathrm{E}}$, or—in the case of transmuting fields—by transforming couplings, i.e., elements of the set $\mathrm{K}_{\mathrm{T}}$, (24), whereby a coupling constant can be assigned to each of the selected fields. The mode of operation of the elements of $\mathrm{K}_{\mathrm{T}}$ becomes clear when, as an example,  the simplest field structure in the realm of the imaginary elements of $\mathrm{Z}_{2}$, namely the photon field $\underline{\mathrm{A}}=\mathrm{A}_{1}+\mathrm{B}_{1}+\mathrm{C}_{2}$, with respect to the coupling constants. We now return to the discussion in Chapter IV, Section 1. 

At very high energies, where the coupling constants $\mathrm{w}_{21}, \mathrm{w}_{23}$  and $\mathrm{w}_{24}$ approach the value $\mathrm{w}_{0}$, as has already been shown, $\mathrm{w}_{3 \alpha} \rightarrow 0$—and thus also $\underline{\mathrm{w}}_{3} \rightarrow 0$—but $\underline{\mathrm{w}}_{3} \neq 0$, which means $\left|\overline{\mathrm{w}}_{0}-\underline{\mathrm{w}}_{3}\right| \rightarrow 0$. The fields $\underline{\mathrm{w}}_{3}$ and $\underline{\mathrm{w}}_{3}$ increase as the energy decreases, so that $\left|\overline{\mathrm{w}}_{0}-\underline{\mathrm{w}}_{3}\right|=\mathrm{w}_{4 \alpha}$ becomes possible.  Since $\mathrm{w}_{0}$ is energy-dependent, there is a $\overline{\mathrm{w}}_{0}<\mathrm{w}_{0}$, where $\overline{\mathrm{w}}_{0}$ is the coupling constant shortly after unification. At high particle energies, $\mathrm{w}_{4 \alpha}-\underline{\underline{\mathrm{w}}}_{3}=\underline{\underline{\mathrm{w}}}_{5}$ therefore also becomes possible, so that at an energy of approximately $10^{17} \mathrm{GeV}$ the transitions $\overline{\mathrm{w}}_{0}-\underline{\mathrm{w}}_{3}=\mathrm{w}_{4 \alpha}$ and $\mathrm{w}_{4 \alpha}-\underline{\underline{\mathrm{w}}}_{3}=\underline{\mathrm{w}}_{5}$ appear.  By analogy with induced photon emission, $\mathrm{w}_{4 \alpha}$ transitions into $\sqrt{\mathrm{N}+1} \mathrm{w}_{4 \alpha}$ when N is the number of identical $\mathrm{w}_{4 \alpha}$quanta contained in a given $\mathrm{R}_{3}$ volume. If M is the number of degenerate $\mathrm{w}_{4 \alpha}$ terms of the same state and $\mathrm{N} \gg 1$, but also $\mathrm{M} \gg 1$, then the following holds for the transmutation, if $\overline{\mathrm{w}}_{0}^{\prime}>\overline{\mathrm{w}}_{0}$ is $\overline{\mathrm{w}}_{0}^{\prime}-\underline{\mathrm{w}}_{3}=\mathrm{w}_{4 \alpha} \cdot \sqrt{\mathrm{~N}}$ and $\mathrm{w}_{4 \alpha} \sqrt{\mathrm{~N}}-\underline{\underline{\mathrm{w}}}_{3}=\underline{\underline{\mathrm{w}}}_{5} \cdot \sqrt{\mathrm{M}}$. In this process, the energy principle must remain valid. 

We will now investigate whether the transmutation relationships valid for $10^{17} \mathrm{GeV}$—which may have governed the inflationary cosmogony of matter—can also take effect after this inflationary phase in the current low-energy state of the universe. For this current state, the low-energy values $\underline{\mathrm{w}}_{3}$ and $\underline{\underline{\mathrm{w}}}_{3}$ apply,  which numerically take the values $\underline{\mathrm{w}}_{3}=0,256362$ and $\underline{\underline{\mathrm{w}}}_{3}=0,0149492$.

Structured Hyperspace

<!--okf:source lang="de"-->
> **Original**

> Der strukturierte Hyperraum
<!--/okf:source-->

Due to its structure, it allows for three basic types of couplings. The first type consists of spacetime—that is, physical—couplings of an energetic nature, in which imponderable or ponderable interaction quanta Wq are exchanged between charges (in the broadest sense). These couplings and their Wq always presuppose a hermetic (i.e., non-Euclidean) structure of $\mathrm{R}_{4}=\mathrm{R}_{3} \cup \mathrm{~T}_{1}$. The second type of couplings is determined by a hermetic structure of the subspace $\mathrm{I}_{2} \cup \mathrm{G}_{4}$. These couplings are not of an energetic nature, but are always present in a latent form within or beyond the $\mathrm{R}_{4}$ background. Furthermore, no physical Wq appear in this second type; however, these couplings transform, in an informational sense, the organizational states of non-Euclidean $\mathrm{R}_{3}$ fields, such that the energetic couplings of the $\mathrm{R}_{4}$ undergo transmutations through the action of these informational-organizational fields of the $\mathbf{I}_{2} \cup \mathbf{G}_{4}$ realm. Thus, no Wq of the second type is generated; rather, the Wq in $\mathrm{R}_{4}$ and  its fields are altered by these transformative couplings.  The third type of coupling relates to a hermeticism in the domain  $\mathrm{S}_{2}\left(\mathrm{x}_{5}, \mathrm{x}_{6}\right)$, which, according to [1, Chap. IV], always occurs together with the non-Euclidean structures of $\mathrm{R}_{4}$ and accompanies them. In $\mathrm{R}_{4}$, these fields can also occur independently in gravitational form, but also as probability fields that, via $\mathrm{G}_{4} \cup \mathrm{I}_{2}$ via $\mathrm{S}_{2}$ and $\mathrm{T}_{1}$ control the structures in the region $\mathrm{R}_{3} \cup \mathrm{~T}_{1}$. This third type (identified by $\mathrm{S}_{2}$) thus has a mixed significance, such that this third type connects the latent fields of type 2 with the energetic couplings of type 1. The very weak energetic component of the Type 3 fields is reflected in the extraordinarily low numerical values of the coupling constants $\mathrm{w}_{22}$ and $\mathrm{w}_{4 \alpha}$ in the scheme (16). This also makes it clear why, in the set $\mathrm{w}_{4}$, the element $\mathrm{w}_{4 \alpha}$ represents the coupling of a repulsive gravitational field, which can appear not only in the additive combination $\underline{\mathrm{w}}_{22}$ but also independently.

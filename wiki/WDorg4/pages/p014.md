Images of the type $\mathrm{I}_{2} \rightarrow \mathrm{~S}_{2}$ are unproblematic, whereas $\mathrm{S}_{2} \rightarrow \mathrm{R}_{4}$ is possible only via the time structure $\mathrm{S}_{2} \rightarrow \mathrm{~T}_{1}$. However, since in $\mathrm{R}_{4}$ every point  of a $\mathrm{R}_{3}$ volume lies on a spacetime line, this mapping—which can be described as a  “rheomorphism”—$\mathrm{S}_{2} \rightarrow \mathrm{~T}_{1}$ acts as a “rheomorphism” when it is updated, in the sense of a change to the relevant $\mathrm{R}_{3}$ region. In contrast,  the mapping $\mathrm{G}_{4} \rightarrow \mathrm{I}_{2}$ proved problematic, since the structures  of the $\mathrm{G}_{4}$ can only be mapped into an “intermediate space,” which can be conceived of as  a subset of a general abstract function space. The mapping from this intermediary space to the $I_{2}$ is then performed via  multidimensional Fourier series. 

In and of themselves, the structures in $\mathrm{G}_{4}$ and $\mathrm{I}_{2}$ appear timeless; but $\mathrm{G}_{4} \rightarrow \mathrm{I}_{2} \rightarrow \mathrm{~S}_{2} \rightarrow \mathrm{~T}_{1}$ makes it possible to access every time slice of the spacetime cosmos. This means that whenever a stationary event in $\mathrm{R}_{4}$ changes, within the framework of this access, $\mathrm{R}_{6}$ opens into the $\mathrm{R}_{12}$ and, following the temporally dynamic process, changes as $\mathrm{R}_{6}^{\prime} \neq \mathrm{R}_{6}$ appears. Critical points in time were the spacetime origin event $\mathrm{t}=0$ according to [2, Chap. V] as well as the point in time $\mathrm{t}=\mathrm{T}_{1}$ (not to be confused with the time structure $\mathrm{T}_{1}\left(\mathrm{x}_{4}\right)$ as a subspace) of a material cosmogony. 

The essential question was in what form these appear in the physical world via the illustrated  mapping chain $\mathrm{G}_{4} \rightarrow \mathrm{R}_{4}$. The deducible result shows that all structures of the $\mathrm{G}_{4}$ that are mapped in physical spacetime always appear as probability amplitudes capable of superposition and interference, through which the matter (or energy) evidently present in the $\mathrm{R}_{4}$ is controlled at the micro level. Thus, the entire indeterministic quantum theory appears as a consequence of this hyperspace dynamics of the mapping chain $\mathrm{G}_{4} \rightarrow \mathrm{R}_{4}$, whereby the premises of both abstract and concrete quantum theory arise spontaneously from the mapping process itself. 

This suggests the possibility of a unified theory of interactions as well as the possibility of a general cosmogony of matter.

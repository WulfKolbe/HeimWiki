These correspondences indicate that, at least for some of the initially spacetime-independent coupling constants, there are physically significant energy values that characterize the respective constants, such that the initially energy-independent coupling constants become energy-dependent. The possible energy values here are the characteristic fixed values described by (21). 

If one considers the numerical values $\mathrm{w}_{21} \ldots \mathrm{w}_{24}$ of the set $\mathrm{w}_{2}$, it becomes apparent that $\mathrm{w}_{21}$ and $\mathrm{w}_{24}$ represent the electromagnetic and weak forces, respectively, but $\mathrm{w}_{22}$ and $\mathrm{w}_{23}$ represent the gravitational and strong forces, respectively, which becomes particularly clear when, instead of this set $\mathrm{w}_{2}$ from (16), the corresponding values for the interaction constants according to (16a) are presented numerically. Since the elements of the sets $\mathrm{w}_{1}$ and $\mathrm{w}_{4}$ are not yet additively combined with those of the set $\mathrm{w}_{2}$, only the imponderable Wq are accounted for by the squares of these elements. A comparison with the measured values according to [Teilchen1984](../references/WDorg4_REF_Teilchen1984.md) shows that, with the exception of $\mathrm{w}_{23}$ and  $\mathrm{W}_{23}=\mathrm{w}_{23}^{2}$, the elements of $\mathrm{w}_{2}$ approximate the measured values quite well, although the  greater deviation of the strong force $\mathrm{W}_{23}$ is likely due to a  stronger energy dependence. The gravitational field—and thus also its coupling constant—had to be related to the field-generating mass of the gravitons. Since there is no elementary mass, as is the case with, for example, the electric charge, this mass is naturally associated with the atomic structure of matter and the structure of nuclides $\mathrm{m}_{\mathrm{N}}$. In contrast to imponderable Wq, ponderable Wq are described by the coupling constants of the set $\underline{\mathrm{w}}_{2}$, whereby the ponderability of photonic Wq appears due to $\underline{\mathrm{w}}_{21} \approx \mathrm{w}_{0}$ appears when the electromagnetic force is unified with the strong and weak forces.

# Equation 3a

$$
\begin{aligned} & \mathrm{c}_{1}^{2}=\sum_{\mathrm{i}=1}^{3} \underline{\mathrm{~d}}_{\mathrm{i}}^{\prime 2}, \quad \mathrm{c}_{2}^{2}=\underline{\mathrm{d}}_{1}^{\prime 2}+\underline{\mathrm{d}}_{2}^{\prime 2}, \quad \mathrm{~b}_{1}^{\prime}=\mathrm{c}_{1} / 2, \quad \mathrm{~b}_{2}^{\prime}=\mathrm{c}_{2} / 4, \quad \mathrm{c}_{3}=\underline{\mathrm{d}}_{1}^{\prime 2}-\underline{\mathrm{d}}_{2}^{\prime 2} \\ & \mathrm{~b}_{3}^{\prime}=\mathrm{c}_{3} / 2 \approx \mathrm{~g} \end{aligned}
$$

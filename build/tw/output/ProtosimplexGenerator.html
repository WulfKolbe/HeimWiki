1. Protosimplex generator
    • German:** *Protosimplexgenerator*

A quantity formed multiplicatively from the structural power, the base rise, and an excitation function (dependent on the resonance orders), through which the occupancy of the configuration zones with protosimplexes can be determined. (vol. 2, p. 324)

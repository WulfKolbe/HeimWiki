1. Entelechial dimension
    • German:** *Entelechale Dimension*

A hidden world dimension orthogonal to physical spacetime, which, like the eonic dimension, is imaginary. (vol. 2, p. 21) Event, latent: A world point outside the spacetime subspace of the world. Event, manifest: A world point within the subspace of spacetime.

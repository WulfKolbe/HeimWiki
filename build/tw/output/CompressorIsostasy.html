1. Compressor isostasy
    • German:** *Kompressorisostasie*

The principle of dynamic equilibrium in ternary coupling structures of a term of complex hermetry, caused on the one hand by the compensatory tendency of the spatial compressor and on the other hand by the opposing tendency of the condenser flow. (vol. 2, p. 158) Condensation, metronic: Relative densification of metrons during the projection of hyperselectors onto a lattice.

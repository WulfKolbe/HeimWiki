1. Condenser flux
    • German:** *Kondensorfluß*

The temporal flux of the structural state of a fundamental condenser, which occurs cyclically in the form of basic fluxes of a fluctonic nature between the correlation and correspondence maxima. (vol. 2, p. 156)

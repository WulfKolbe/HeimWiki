1. Conjunctor
    • German:** *Konjunktor*

The integral selector that explicitly describes the correlation of protosimplexes caused by a conjunctive. (vol. 2, p. 202) Conjunctor, prototropic: The conjunctor of only one flucton. (vol. 2, p. 196)

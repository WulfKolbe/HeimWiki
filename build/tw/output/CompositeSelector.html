1. Composite selector
    • German:** *Verbundselektor*

A tensorial selector that specifies the correlation between a contravariant and a covariant acting element of the correlator in the binary field of a fundamental condenser, when a polymetry is present and the law of variance step change no longer applies. The composite selector thereby constructs the coupling tensor.

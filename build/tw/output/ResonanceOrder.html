1. Resonance order
    • German:** *Resonanzordnung*

These are positive integers that describe the possible resonances in the resonance spectrum of a fundamental pattern term, such that the resonance order 0 corresponds to the mass term of the fundamental pattern. The resonance orders are bounded above by a positive integer that, like the resonance order, depends solely on the set of quantum numbers of the ground pattern in question. (2.329)(2.329) (2.329)

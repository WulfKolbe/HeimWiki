1. Synmetronics
    • German:** *Synmetronik*

A special form of polymetry involving three tensorial lattice cores as structural units of the metronic world tensorium. (vol. 2, p. 75) Tensor, simply metronic: A simple structural tensor whose geodesic boundary is the metronic lattice. Tensor, primitive: A higher-dimensional tensor spanned by simple tensors

1. Shield field correspondence
    • German:** *Schirmfeldkorrespondenz*

An interrelation between two terms of complex hermetry that follows from their integral shield images (constructed from the prototropic shield fields of the protosimplexes). (vol. 2, p. 232)

**Original (German)**
